Quick FAQs

Is GTA 6 out now?

Yes. Grand Theft Auto VI launched worldwide on 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, according to Rockstar and Take-Two.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No official PC version, release date, store page, or system requirements had been announced as of 19 November 2026. Do not download unofficial “GTA 6 for PC” installers.

Is there GTA 6 Online at launch?

No. The launch game is a single-player story (PlayStation lists it as 1 player). Rockstar has not announced a GTA 6 Online mode or date. Existing GTA Online in Grand Theft Auto V is a separate, still-supported service. Full details: GTA 6 Online release date.

How much storage does GTA 6 need?

PlayStation officially advised players to clear a minimum of 212 GB of free PS5 storage. On Xbox Series X|S the Microsoft Store lists about 209.86 GB. Keep 230 GB or more free so updates can install. Details: GTA 6 file size guide.

What is the difference between Standard and Ultimate Edition?

Standard is $79.99 and includes the game. Ultimate is $99.99 and adds story-threaded vehicles, weapons, apparel, and exclusive shops. Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later. Purchases before 20 November 2026 also included the Vintage Vice City Pack; since 20 November it is sold as a separate add-on.
