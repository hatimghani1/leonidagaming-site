GTA 6 money guide: how to make money fast, by chapter

The best honest money methods in Grand Theft Auto VI change as the story opens Leonida. Start in the Keys, graduate to Moses in Port Gellhorn, then use the snap gun, Randy, and late jobs in Vice City. No cheats or exploits.

Updated as official and launch-day data drops — 19 November 2026. Platforms and setting are official. Payouts and unlock timing come from launch guides at Polygon, PC Gamer, and GameSpot and may be revised.

How the GTA 6 economy works

Launch coverage describes three kinds of money. BuckMe is your banked balance — deposit cash at ATMs and treat it as the safe pot. Cash on hand is what you are carrying; Polygon’s first-hour guide says you lose it if you die, so stash or deposit it. Loot bags are stolen goods that only become spendable after a fence buys them. Official Ultimate Edition copy already confirms that stolen goods can be fenced (for example at Paradise Garage in Watson Bay).

GameSpot and Polygon also report that some story missions require cash before you can continue. Do not spend your early earnings as if this were a sandbox with infinite resupply.

Rockstar has not published a list of player-owned businesses or passive property income. We will not invent any.

Choose your chapter

Chapter 1

Early game: the Keys

Stash cash, follow the story, and run Kenton’s treasure dives once they unlock. Car fences are not your opening loop.

Chapter 2

Mid game: Port Gellhorn

Sell untracked cars to Moses, hunt large animals for Bret, and make Starlet Motel rent if the story asks for it.

Chapters 3–4

Late game: Vice City

Snap-gun back-door robberies, Randy’s high-end cars, Pay ’N’ Spray loops, Ambrosia derby, and Pilot Pete drops.

Money FAQs

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

It depends on the chapter. Launch-day guides point to Kenton’s dives in Chapter 1, selling cars to Moses in Chapter 2, and snap-gun back-door jobs, Randy’s cars, and Pilot Pete’s drops later. There is no official single “best” method, and this site does not cover cheats.

Do you lose money when you die in GTA 6?

Launch reports say cash you are carrying and loot bags can be lost if you go Wasted. Money deposited in BuckMe (via ATMs) is described as safer. Early on, Polygon also recommends stashing cash under loose planks in Jason’s bedroom.

Can you buy businesses in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not published an official list of player-owned businesses or passive income properties. Do not assume GTA Online-style businesses exist in the story mode until that is verified.
