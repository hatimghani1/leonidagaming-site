GTA 6 100% completion FAQ

How do you get 100% completion in GTA 6?

Finish the story, then meet the 35 requirements on the in-game list under Progress › Stats › 100% Completion. Many need only part of a category, for example 100 of 255 animals or 300 of 397 stunts, according to Shacknews, GTABase and PowerPyx.

When does the 100% checklist appear in GTA 6?

Published guides say the in-game 100% Completion list becomes visible after you finish the main story. Everything you did before still counts.

Is anything missable for 100% in GTA 6?

GTABase reports that nothing on the 100% list is missable, as every requirement can still be completed in free roam after the story. Story missions themselves can’t be replayed, so keep manual saves if you want to revisit them.

How many story missions are in GTA 6?

Guides list five chapters. Our checklist tracks 94 story entries, including optional ones (sources: GamesRadar+, PC Gamer, Game Rant, VGC). Counts differ slightly between outlets depending on whether optional missions are included.

How long does it take to 100% GTA 6?

About 200 hours for the in-game 100% Completion checklist and the Platinum trophy, story included, according to PowerPyx; GameSpot also estimates upwards of 200 hours. The story alone usually takes 60 to 80 hours, and true completion of everything is estimated at over 300 hours.

How many trophies does GTA 6 have?

GamesRadar+ lists 55 trophies and achievements, including the platinum and A Long Road for 100% completion.

Does this checklist save my progress?

Yes. Progress is stored in your browser on this device (localStorage). Use Export to download a backup file and Import to move it to another device. No login needed.
