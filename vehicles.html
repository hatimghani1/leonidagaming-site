GTA 6 vehicles guide: official cars, editions, and selling rides

This page lists vehicles Rockstar and PlayStation have actually named — not a fan-made complete garage, and not a “fastest car in GTA 6” ranking. Comparable top-speed data is still settling on launch day. When we publish one, it will be measured, not guessed.

Updated 19 November 2026. Edition contents come from Rockstar support and the PlayStation product page. Selling-car loops are launch-day reports, attributed in the money guide.

Vintage Vice City Pack — free window closed 20 November 2026

Pre-orders and purchases before 20 November 2026 included the ’55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Eligible digital purchases also included one month of GTA+ (for the existing GTA Online / GTA+ library — not a GTA 6 Online service). Since 20 November 2026 the pack is listed as a separate add-on ($4.99 on the US Xbox Store, £4.49 on the UK PlayStation Store at the time of writing). Read the Vintage Vice City Pack guide for how to claim the Stanier in Chapter 3.

Ultimate Edition vehicles

The Ultimate Edition ($99.99) adds bonuses “threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s story,” with items uncovered as chapters progress. Officially described vehicles and related extras:

’95 Grotti CheetahGrotti’s mid-’90s sports car, an “ode to Shore Drive,” with a retro-futuristic livery, available to punctuate later-stage action.

Ultimate also lists exclusive shops such as Rideout Customs, Sara’s Unisex Salon, Stock 305, Electric Fang Tattoo, and One-Eyed Willie’s at Lake Leonida. Standard Edition owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later. See Daily Tips for the full edition comparison.

Jason’s Vapid Ganado

Official copy describes Jason’s truck as a well-worn Vapid Ganado low-riding pickup. The Retro Build is an Ultimate extra, not a Standard default. This is one of the few story vehicles named in marketing rather than only in player databases.

How selling cars works (launch-day reports)

  • Moses in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2): older, low-profile, untracked cars; slim jim; a cooldown between sales. Typical reported pays are $1,000 to a few thousand. Details in the Chapter 2 money guide.
  • Randy in western Vice City (Chapter 3+): higher-end cars, clone keys, a target list, and better pay if the car is in good condition. See the Vice City money guide.
  • Pay ’N’ Spray: buys certain cars without an advanced tracker. Polygon highlights a fast Ocean Beach loop at around $7,000 per valuable car, with a short shop cooldown.

We are not publishing a fastest-car list today. When multiple testers post comparable speeds, that comparison will live here and will say how it was measured.

Vehicle FAQs

What cars are in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

Officially listed Ultimate vehicles include the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, Shitzu Squalo, ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak, the Ganado Retro Build, and a Classic Car Collection. Bonuses are threaded across Jason and Lucia’s story.

What is the Vintage Vice City Pack car?

The ’55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, plus outfits, hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. It was free with purchases before 20 November 2026 (eligible digital copies also got one month of GTA+); since 20 November it is sold separately.

Can you sell cars in GTA 6?

Launch guides describe selling untracked or older cars to Moses in Port Gellhorn, higher-end cars to Randy in western Vice City, and certain cars to Pay ’N’ Spray shops if they do not have an advanced tracker.
