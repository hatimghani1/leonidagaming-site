GTA 6 vehicles guide: official cars, editions, and selling rides

Jason’s Vapid Ganado

Official copy describes Jason’s truck as a well-worn Vapid Ganado low-riding pickup. The Retro Build is an Ultimate extra, not a Standard default. This is one of the few story vehicles named in marketing rather than only in player databases.

How selling cars works (launch-day reports)

  • Moses in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2): older, low-profile, untracked cars; slim jim; a cooldown between sales. Typical reported pays are $1,000 to a few thousand. He refuses cars stolen too close to his garage and has 7 named Vehicle Drop-Offs that end with a free Emperor Sultan (Push Square). Details in the Chapter 2 money guide.
  • Randy in western Vice City (Chapter 3+): higher-end cars, clone keys, a target list, and better pay if the car is in good condition. PowerPyx counts 18 cars on his Wish List, usually $30,000+ each in perfect condition. See Randy’s Wish List and compare every method in the GTA 6 money guide.
  • Pay ’n’ Spray: shops unlock during the Chapter 1 mission Just a Scratch for repairs and resprays, and you can sell cars you own there (GamesRadar+). For stolen cars, launch-week guides report Pay ’n’ Spray buys cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker from early Chapter 3 and Advanced Tracker cars later in Chapter 3, after a Randy job. Reported limit: three sales per in-game day. Polygon highlights an Ocean Beach loop at around $7,000 per valuable car.
  • Payouts and stored cars: Pay ’n’ Spray money goes straight into your BuckMe account (unless it is at its balance limit). Press R1/RB in the lot to open Stored Vehicles and sell garaged or registered cars without driving them over.
  • Check before you steal: hold Up on the D-pad to open WAiNK, aim at a car and press R1/RB to see its sell value, tracker and which buyer wants it.

Fastest cars in GTA 6 (stock Speed rating)

Rockstar has not published vehicle stats. GameSpot’s 20 November ranking uses the Speed stat the game shows (out of 10, no upgrades): Truffade Thrax 8.7, Progen Emerus 8.3, Pegassi Vitello 8.2, Piccia Furia 7.8 and Pfister Comet S2 7.7. Beebom adds the Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire (7.8) as the fastest muscle car. The Thrax is the final car on Randy’s Wish List; the Vitello is the fastest one you can steal in free roam. These are ratings, not timed mph. Full ranking, locations and the Thrax route: Fastest car in GTA 6.

Vehicle FAQs

What cars are in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

Officially listed Ultimate vehicles include the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, Shitzu Squalo, ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy, Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak, the Ganado Retro Build, and a Classic Car Collection. Bonuses are threaded across Jason and Lucia’s story.

How do you get the Grotti Cheetah in GTA 6?

It is an Ultimate Edition bonus. Launch-week guides from TheGamer, VICE and GameSpot report it unlocks in Chapter 5 and waits in the Pegassi Residences parking garage in Vice City. Standard owners need the Ultimate Edition Upgrade.

What is the Vintage Vice City Pack car?

The ’55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and garage, plus outfits, hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. It was free with purchases before 20 November 2026 (eligible digital copies also got one month of GTA+); since 20 November it is sold separately.

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

The Truffade Thrax, with a stock Speed rating of 8.7 out of 10 (GameSpot, 20 November 2026), ahead of the Progen Emerus (8.3) and Pegassi Vitello (8.2). The Thrax is the final car on Randy’s Wish List; the Vitello is the fastest car you can simply steal. Ratings are in-game stats, not timed mph. See our fastest cars guide.

Can you sell cars in GTA 6?

Yes. Moses in Port Gellhorn buys untracked cars from Chapter 2. From early Chapter 3, Pay ’n’ Spray shops buy cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker, and Advanced Tracker cars later in Chapter 3; launch guides report a limit of three Pay ’n’ Spray sales per in-game day. Randy pays for high-end cars on his Wish List. Pay ’n’ Spray money goes into your BuckMe account, and you can sell stored cars from the Stored Vehicles tab (R1/RB).
