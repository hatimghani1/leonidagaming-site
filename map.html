GTA 6 map guide: Leonida, Vice City, and all six official regions

Grand Theft Auto VI is set in the state of Leonida, home of neon-soaked Vice City and five other officially named regions. This page sticks to confirmed geography from Rockstar and PlayStation — not leaked layouts or invented collectible lists.

Background: Rockstar Games (official screenshot)

Updated 19 November 2026. Region names and descriptions below come from the PlayStation Blog launch preview and official edition copy. Collectible maps will be added only after locations are verified in the released game.

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are caught in a criminal conspiracy that stretches across Leonida after an easy score goes wrong. Lucia’s official bio also mentions time in the Leonida Penitentiary and family roots in Liberty City. Jason’s official bio places him in the Keys, after an Army stint, working for local drug runners before he meets Lucia.

Vice City

Officially, “the glamour, hustle, and greed of America captured in a single city.” PlayStation names pastel art deco hotels and the white sands of Ocean Beach, the panaderías of Little Cuba, the bootleg brands of the Tisha-Wocka flea market, and VC Port, “the cruise ship capital of the world.” It is a long way from the 1980s, but Vice City is still described as the sun-and-fun capital of America. This is the Chapter 3 playground in launch walkthroughs, and the home of many late-game money jobs, from jewelry stores (15 of the 20 are in Vice-Dale, with clusters in the Decor District, Ocean Beach and Downtown) to Randy’s Wish List cars. Ocean Beach and Downtown also hold most of the game’s bank vaults. Music fans: the two secret radio playlists are unlocked by scanning posters in La Perle, Crosstown and Downtown Vice City (poster locations).

Leonida Keys

A tropical archipelago where “life isn’t flashy but it’s easy” — and where the waters are described as some of the most beautiful and dangerous in America. This is the early-game setting in launch coverage (Key Lento and Kenton’s dive shop sit here in those reports). See the early-game money guide. Just across the water on the mainland, launch guides place Mariana County’s swamps (Hamlet, Cottonmouth Lake, Pelican Valley), where you can find Bigfoot and play Kirk’s Skunk Ape missions.

Grassrivers

A primordial expanse of mangroves. Alligators are the famous attraction; official copy warns there are “far deadlier predators and weirder discoveries” under the surface.

Port Gellhorn

A once-popular vacation spot of cheap motels, shut-down attractions, and empty strip malls, now running on “malt liquor, painkillers, and truck stop energy drinks.” Launch guides place Moses and the Starlet Motel here in Chapter 2. Read the Port Gellhorn money guide, including the 7 Moses drop-off locations.

Ambrosia

In the heart of Leonida, “American industry and old school values still reign supreme.” The Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs; a local biker gang “provides almost everything else.” Launch coverage also points to racing at Ambrosia Raceway, including a repeatable demolition derby — see the demolition derby tips.

Mount Kalaga

A national landmark on the state’s northern border, offering “prime hunting, fishing, and off-road trails.” Official copy places hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals in the surrounding backwoods. The Ultimate Edition’s ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy is specifically pitched for this terrain. Hunting jobs reported in launch guides pair naturally with this region (see hunting for Bret).

Other official place names

Ultimate Edition and PlayStation copy also mention Shore Drive, Gambit Bay and Washington Beach (the Shitzu Squalo), Watson Bay and Paradise Garage, Lake Leonida (One-Eyed Willie’s mod shop), and Stockyard (Stock 305 and Electric Fang Tattoo). Revolvers in the Ultimate Edition are described as sourced from the Vercetti Estate.

Map FAQs

How many regions are in the GTA 6 map?

Officially, Leonida is made up of six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, and Mount Kalaga, according to PlayStation’s 18 November 2026 preview.

Is Vice City in GTA 6?

Yes. Vice City is the signature city inside the fictional state of Leonida. Official copy mentions neighborhoods such as Ocean Beach, Little Cuba, the Tisha-Wocka flea market, and VC Port.

Where can I download an official GTA 6 map?
