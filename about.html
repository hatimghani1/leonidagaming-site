About Leonida Guides

Leonida Guides is a brand-new, independent GTA 6 guides and news site. We publish practical, honest help for players on launch day and after — and we stay inside Rockstar’s Terms of Service.

What we cover

Money-making and the in-game economy, the official map of Leonida, vehicles, beginner tips, editions, and official news. Start at the home page or the money guide.

Sponsored links

Some pages include a clearly labeled “Sponsored giveaway” from a third-party partner. Those offers are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, entering is not a guaranteed win, and the promoter’s terms apply. We do not use pop-ups or auto-redirects for these links.

How we handle facts

  • Official facts (release date, platforms, editions, named regions, named edition vehicles) are taken from Rockstar, Take-Two, and PlayStation pages and are linked.
  • Launch-day gameplay reports (payouts, unlock timing, specific store names) are attributed to outlets such as Polygon, PC Gamer, and GameSpot, dated, and marked as subject to revision.
  • We do not invent chapter names, mission payouts, businesses, or PC/Online dates to fill a gap.
  • Pages carry an Updated stamp. When a number changes, we change the stamp.

What we will not publish

Cheats, trainers, glitches, exploits, hacks, leaked unreleased assets, or anything else that violates Rockstar’s Terms of Service. Designed in-game crimes (store jobs, car theft as a story activity, and so on) are part of the game and may be covered as gameplay. Breaking the live service or the license is not.
