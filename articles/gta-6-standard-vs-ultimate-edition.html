GTA 6 Standard vs Ultimate Edition: what you get, when it unlocks, and is it worth it?

Both editions contain the exact same game. The Ultimate Edition costs $20 more and adds 16 extras (cars, weapons, outfits, shops and two side activities) that unlock chapter by chapter, some of them dozens of hours in. Here is the full official list, when each bonus shows up according to launch-week guides, how to upgrade later, and who should actually pay for it.

Updated 20 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). No story spoilers beyond chapter numbers and mission names already in launch guides.

Status on 20 November 2026: GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Standard is $79.99 and Ultimate is $99.99 (US store prices). The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is on sale on its own on the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store, so you can start with Standard and upgrade later. The free Vintage Vice City Pack window closed on 20 November.

Quick answer

  • Same game? Yes. Story, map, missions and characters are identical. Ultimate adds bonus content only. (Official: PlayStation and Xbox store listings.)
  • Price: Standard $79.99 / £69.99 / €79.99; Ultimate $99.99 / £89.99 / €99.99. (Official store prices; other regions vary.)
  • Upgrade later? Yes. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is $20 / £20 / €20 on the Xbox Store and is live on the PlayStation Store. (Official store listings.)
  • Physical Ultimate? No. The boxed game is Standard with a download code (no disc). Upgrade digitally. (Official: Rockstar Support.)
  • Gameplay advantage? Small. Most extras are cosmetic or convenience items; the big draws are the ’95 Grotti Cheetah (Chapter 5) and Wyman’s Classic Car Collection side activity (Chapter 2). (Unlock timing: launch-week guides.)

All 16 GTA 6 Ultimate Edition bonuses (official list)

These are the items Rockstar lists on the official PlayStation Store and Xbox Store Ultimate Edition Upgrade pages. Rockstar describes them as “threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia’s story.”

Vehicles

  • ’95 Grotti Cheetah: Grotti’s mid-’90s sports car with a retro-futuristic livery, “available to punctuate later-stage action.”
  • Jason’s Safehouse Vehicles: an Army fatigue-tinged Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak.
  • Ganado Retro Build: exclusive mods for Jason’s well-worn Vapid Ganado pickup.
  • Shitzu Squalo: a pink-and-blue boat with an explosives-laden weapons crate.
  • ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy & Garage: a Mud Club off-roader, stored at the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay.

Weapons

  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers: his-and-hers revolvers “sourced from the Vercetti Estate.”
  • Personalized Weapon Variants: engraved versions of Jason’s Girardi ES9 and Lucia’s Klose K17 pistols.

Style

  • Vice City Style: exclusive outfits, hairstyles, tattoos and more for both characters.
  • Goodtime Gear: apparel inspired by the in-game TV character Macca the Gator.

Activities

  • PTT Youngin$ Compound: raid a gang compound and escape to score special items and contraband.
  • Classic Car Collection: track down and restore project cars for collector and fixer Wyman.

Ultimate-only shops

  • Rideout Customs: detailed interiors, rims and donk stylings.
  • Sara’s Unisex Salon: signature styles, facial hair for Jason, makeup and nails for Lucia.
  • Stock 305: streetwear store in Stockyard.
  • Electric Fang Tattoo: 50+ tattoos designed by the artist collective FAILE.
  • One-Eyed Willie’s: Lake Leonida mod shop for off-road parts and hand-painted liveries.

How to upgrade from Standard to Ultimate

You don’t need to rebuy the game. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is an add-on that requires GTA VI (sold separately).

On PS5

  1. Open the PlayStation Store and search for Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade, or open GTA VI’s game hub and pick the add-on.
  2. Buy it with the same PSN account that owns GTA VI.
  3. Let the small download finish, then restart the game. Your existing save keeps going; the bonuses unlock as you reach each chapter.

On Xbox Series X|S

  1. Open the Microsoft Store (or the GTA VI page under My games & apps › Manage game and add-ons).
  2. Buy Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade ($20 / £20 / €20 at launch).
  3. Install it and restart GTA VI.

Bought the boxed copy?

Redeem the code inside the box first (it unlocks the digital Standard Edition), then buy the upgrade from your console’s store. There is no physical Ultimate Edition, per Rockstar Support.

Price check: buying Standard ($79.99) plus the upgrade ($20) costs the same as Ultimate ($99.99) in the US, UK and euro stores we checked, so starting with Standard costs you nothing extra if you upgrade later.

Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it?

It depends on how you play. The extras don’t change the story or ending, and Standard players can still buy, steal and customize plenty of cars, clothes and weapons in the base game.

Buy Ultimate (or the upgrade) if you…

  • Plan to go for 100% and spend lots of time on side content. The Classic Car Collection and PTT Youngin$ Compound add real activities, not just skins.
  • Love car customization: Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie’s add mod options the base shops don’t have, plus the Cheetah, Buggy and Squalo.
  • Care about dressing Jason and Lucia: three exclusive style shops, Vice City Style and Goodtime Gear.

Stick with Standard if you…

  • Mainly want the story. Everything that matters to the plot is in Standard.
  • Aren’t sure yet. The upgrade costs the same later, so play the first chapters first and decide once you know whether you enjoy the customization side.
  • Want to save money for a bigger in-game budget: our money guide shows how to fund cars and gear legitimately.

Our take: because the upgrade is the same $20 later, there’s no rush. Start with Standard if you’re on the fence; upgrade around Chapter 2, when the Buggy and Wyman’s collection unlock, if you find yourself spending time in mod shops and clothing stores.

Ultimate owners: 5 tips so you don’t miss anything

  1. Use the Chapter 1 vehicles early. The Enduro, Kayak and Ganado Retro Build are reported as Chapter 1 only.
  2. Check Ammu-Nation lockers each chapter. Jason’s engraved pistol shows up first, Lucia’s and the revolvers in Chapter 2.
  3. Look for purple map icons after each chapter starts. That’s where most bonus pickups are marked.
  4. Visit Stockyard once you reach Vice City. Electric Fang and Stock 305 are both there.
  5. Track it all. Edition vehicles are in our 100% checklist, and Moses/Randy selling rules for non-edition cars are in the Chapter 2 money guide.

GTA 6 Standard vs Ultimate FAQ

What is the difference between GTA 6 Standard and Ultimate Edition?

Both contain the same full game. The Ultimate Edition ($99.99) adds 16 bonuses on top of Standard ($79.99): the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, Shitzu Squalo, ’67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and garage, Jason’s safehouse vehicles, the Ganado Retro Build, two revolvers, engraved pistols, exclusive outfits and gear, five Ultimate-only shops, the PTT Youngin$ Compound and Wyman’s Classic Car Collection.

How much is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition upgrade?

$20 in the US, £20 in the UK and €20 in the euro zone on the Xbox Store at launch, which equals the price gap between the editions. The upgrade is also on sale on the PlayStation Store. Check your own store for regional pricing.

Can I upgrade GTA 6 to the Ultimate Edition later?

Yes. Buy the Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade add-on from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store at any time. Your save carries on and bonuses unlock as you reach each chapter. Boxed-copy owners redeem their Standard code first, then buy the upgrade digitally.

When do you get the Grotti Cheetah in GTA 6?

Launch-week guides from TheGamer, VICE and GameSpot report that the ’95 Grotti Cheetah unlocks in Chapter 5 and is collected from the Pegassi Residences parking garage in Vice City. Rockstar only says it is available to punctuate later-stage action.

Is there a physical GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

No. Rockstar Support says the physical version contains a download code only, with no disc, and there is no physical Ultimate Edition. Redeem the Standard code and buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade digitally.

Does the Ultimate Edition include the Vintage Vice City Pack?

Not by itself. The Vintage Vice City Pack was a bonus for any edition bought before 20 November 2026. Since then it is sold separately, whether you own Standard or Ultimate.
