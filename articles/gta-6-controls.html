GTA 6 Controls: Every PS5 and Xbox Button, Hidden Moves and the Settings Worth Changing

GTA 6 changes the controls Rockstar fans have used for years. You now run by clicking the left stick (L3 / LS) instead of tapping Cross / A, the handbrake moved to Cross / A, and in vehicles you shoot with R1 / RB. If you hate it, the Classic control scheme puts sprint back on a face button. Below: every default control for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in quick tables (on foot, aiming, melee, driving, bikes, aircraft, swimming, parachute), the three control schemes, the hidden moves the game never explains, and the settings worth changing in your first hour.

Updated 27 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). Console version (PS5 / Xbox Series X|S) on title update 01.002.002. No story spoilers.

Status on 27 November 2026: Rockstar has not published a controls page for GTA VI outside the game, so these tables come from guide writers who copied the in-game control scheme screens of the release version (GTABase, TheGamer, GameSpot, Engadget, Game Rant). Every control below is listed by at least one of them; where outlets disagree or only one lists a move, we say so. Quick answer: the GTA 6 controls that changed

  • Run / sprint: click L3 (PS5) or LS (Xbox). No more tapping Cross / A. (GameSpot, GTABase, Engadget.)
  • Jump / climb: Cross / A; crouch is Circle / B. (GTABase, Game Rant.)
  • Handbrake: Cross / A in vehicles, which frees R1 / RB to shoot from cars, bikes, aircraft and parachutes. (GTABase.)
  • Classic controls: Pause → Settings → Control Scheme → Classic puts run back on Cross / A. (Engadget, Game Rant.)
  • Remapping: the PlayStation Store listing mentions remappable controls among its accessibility features, but Engadget, playing the release version, says there is no full button-by-button remap; the presets are the main option. Your console can also swap buttons system-wide (see settings).
  • Switch between Jason and Lucia: hold D-pad Right and press R1 / RB (when the story allows it). (GTABase, TheGamer.)

GTA 6 control schemes: Default, Classic and Left-Handed

GTABase counts three control schemes: Default, Classic and Left-Handed. You can preview each one on the same screen before you confirm.

  1. Press Options (PS5) or Menu (Xbox) and open Settings.
  2. The first tab is Control Scheme. Use the D-pad to switch from Default to Classic (or Left-Handed).
  3. Back out with Circle / B and confirm (Game Rant says you hold Cross to save the change).

Cinematic mode and auto-drive

  1. While driving, hold D-pad Down and press Options (Menu on Xbox) to switch on Cinematic Mode.
  2. In Cinematic Mode, hold Circle / B to hand the wheel to auto-drive. GameSpot says you can set the speed to slow, normal or fast.
  3. Press D-pad Down to toggle the minimap while it’s on (TheGamer).

Auto-drive is perfect for long trips to the Keys or for enjoying the radio stations. For quick routes, use a fast car from our fastest cars list and the vehicles hub. If a car gets taken after a bust, here’s how to get it back from the impound lot.

Motorcycle tricks

TheGamer lists a wheelie (pull the left stick back and accelerate with R2 / RT) and a stoppie (push the left stick forward and brake with L2 / LT). Kicks on bikes use Square / X, like in cars.

Hidden GTA 6 controls the game doesn’t explain

  1. Chain jumps: tap Cross / A right after climbing an obstacle to jump again and keep your momentum in a chase (GTABase, GameSpot).
  2. Slide over hoods: run at a car and press Cross / A to vault the hood without slowing down (GTABase, TheGamer).
  3. Combat roll: while aiming, push the left stick in a direction and press Cross / A (GameSpot, TheGamer).
  4. Backflip into water: run to the edge of a dock and hold Cross / A (GameSpot, TheGamer).
  5. Lean and rest: hold Circle / B next to a wall or car to lean, press L3 / LS at a railing to rest on it, and hold Circle / B on open ground to sit (GameSpot, TheGamer). Great for photos.
  6. Flip someone off: with no weapon out, hold L2 / LT on a person and press R1 / RB (TheGamer).
  7. Reject a call: press Circle / B when the phone rings (TheGamer). Be careful with story calls: see the patch notes for the phone-call fix in 01.002.002.
  8. Push a jet ski or boat off the beach: hold L1 / LB (TheGamer).
  9. Radio on foot: equip earbuds or headphones from your inventory (TheGamer).
What we don’t list: cheat codes, camera glitches and exploits. They break Rockstar’s rules or can corrupt a save. Everything on this page is a normal, intended control.

GTA 6 settings worth changing

These options come from Engadget’s walk-through of the release settings menu:

  • Control Scheme → Classic if your thumbs remember GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Gameplay → Combat → Targeting: switch from Partial to Lock-On for GTA 5-style auto-targeting, and turn on Lock-On Target Switch to flick between targets with the right stick. Or pick Free Aim if you’re confident.
  • Aim assist strength, Shooting Assist and drive-by auto-aim are in the same section.
  • UI: turn off the minimap, compass, GPS route and objective markers for an old-school run. Engadget doesn’t recommend switching them all off.
  • Camera: camera height, follow speed and the first-person phone display. See our camera settings section.

Remap buttons with your console

If no preset suits you, both consoles can swap buttons for every game: on PS5 go to Settings → Accessibility → Controllers → Custom Button Assignments; on Xbox Series X|S go to Settings → Accessibility → Controller → Button mapping (or the Xbox Accessories app). The game’s on-screen prompts won’t change, so only swap one or two buttons.

Settings don’t affect your progress, but change them outside missions and keep a manual save; our save guide explains slots.

GTA 6 PC and keyboard controls

There are no official GTA 6 keyboard and mouse controls, because there is no official PC version. On 27 November 2026 Rockstar still has not announced one (see is GTA 6 on PC?). Pages that list “GTA 6 PC controls” are guessing or describe unofficial builds made from leaked files, which Take-Two is taking down. Don’t download them: security firms have found fake GTA 6 PC files that steal passwords. If you play on console with a keyboard plugged in, the game still uses the controller layouts above.

FAQ: GTA 6 controls

How do you run in GTA 6?

Click the left stick: L3 on PS5 or LS on Xbox Series X|S. GTA 6 is the first modern Rockstar game that doesn’t use a face button for sprinting by default. If you prefer tapping Cross or A, switch to the Classic control scheme in Settings.

How do you get classic GTA 5 controls in GTA 6?

Pause, open Settings and go to the first tab, Control Scheme. Change Default to Classic. Run moves to Cross / A, jump and climb move to Square / X and crouch moves to L3 / LS. Game Rant says melee moves to Circle / B. Everything else stays the same.

Can you remap controls in GTA 6?

Partly. GTA 6 offers three preset schemes (Default, Classic and Left-Handed), and the PlayStation Store listing mentions remappable controls among its accessibility features, but Engadget says there is no full button-by-button remap. For anything else, use your console’s system remapping: Custom Button Assignments on PS5 or Button mapping in Xbox accessibility settings.

How do you switch between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6?

Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. You can only switch when the story allows it; during some missions and chapters only one character is playable.

What is the handbrake button in GTA 6?

Cross on PS5 and A on Xbox. Hold it while turning at speed to drift or make a U-turn. Rockstar moved it off R1 / RB, which now fires your weapon from vehicles.

How do you use auto-drive in GTA 6?

While driving, hold Down on the D-pad and press Options (Menu on Xbox) to turn on Cinematic Mode, then hold Circle (B on Xbox) to let the car drive itself to your waypoint. GameSpot says you can set the speed to slow, normal or fast.

Are there GTA 6 PC keyboard controls?

No. GTA 6 is only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version as of 27 November 2026. Any download that promises GTA 6 on PC is unofficial or fake and can contain malware.
