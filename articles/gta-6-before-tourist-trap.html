GTA 6 Tourist Trap: Everything to Do Before Chapter 1 Ends (Missables Checklist)

Tourist Trap is the Chapter 1 point of no return in GTA 6. It starts when you pick Lucia up from work after Kat and Mattia call about the pickup job, and it chains three missions straight into Chapter 2. When it ends you lose Jason’s house, his stash, his car and every dollar outside Family Savings. Before you drive to Lucia: put $5,000 into BuckMe Family Savings, empty the floor stash, spend the rest on guns or clothes, finish Shirley’s favours, read the 5 books and magazines in Jason’s house, meditate with Lucia and cash in Donnie’s lost drugs. Here is the full checklist, in the right order.

Updated 23 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S launch version. Spoiler-light: no plot details beyond mission names.

Status on 23 November 2026: Rockstar has not published an official list of missable content, so this checklist is built from named guide writers who played the release version: Game Rant and GamesRadar+ (both agree on the trigger, the reset and the money plan) plus KeenGamer for extra missables. Anything only one outlet reports is labelled. No cheats, glitches or exploits.

Quick checklist: what to do before Tourist Trap in GTA 6

  1. Empty Jason’s floor stash (loose floorboards in the bedroom). (Game Rant, KeenGamer)
  2. Cash in Donnie’s lost drugs and the boat off Corsair Key, then sell before the mission Swamped. (GamesRadar+, KeenGamer)
  3. Put $5,000 into BuckMe Family Savings, the Chapter 1 maximum. (Game Rant, KeenGamer; see the money hub)
  4. Spend everything else on guns or clothes, never on car mods. (Game Rant, KeenGamer)
  5. Finish all of Shirley’s requests (rum, then the scooter). (GamesRadar+, KeenGamer, Game Rant)
  6. Read the 5 books and magazines in Jason’s house. (GamesRadar+, KeenGamer)
  7. Meditate with Lucia on the balcony after Down to Earth. (GamesRadar+, KeenGamer)
  8. Clear the pink side missions around Key Lento and Mariana County, like Cal’s fishing competitions. (Game Rant, GamesRadar+)
  9. Make a manual save in a slot you never overwrite. Then pick Lucia up.

When is the Chapter 1 point of no return in GTA 6?

The point of no return is the mission Tourist Trap. Game Rant explains that it begins when you pick Lucia up from work after Kat and Mattia call to say the pickup job is back on. From there you are locked into three back-to-back missions, and the next time you can roam freely is the start of Chapter 2.

KeenGamer names the chain as Tourist Trap → Home Sweet Home → Discharge Time and adds a useful early warning: the mission Recalibration with Cal leads straight into that phone call, so treat Recalibration as your last reminder. The good news: nothing forces you to go. The call comes, but you can leave Lucia at work and keep playing Chapter 1 for as long as you want.

Why it matters: GTA 6 has 5 chapters and each one ends with a small point of no return (Game Rant). Chapter 1’s is the harshest because the story moves Jason and Lucia out of the Keys. See the chapter list in our how long is GTA 6 guide.

Step 1: protect your money before Tourist Trap

Only Family Savings survives, so the order matters: collect everything, bank the maximum, spend the rest.

  1. Collect every source of cash first. Empty the floor stash and sell Donnie’s lost drugs (see below) so it all counts toward your deposit.
  2. Deposit at an ATM. KeenGamer: open the BuckMe app and use the ATM Finder to route to the nearest machine.
  3. Move $5,000 into Family Savings. In BuckMe, open your checking account and transfer to savings. Family Savings unlocks during the mission Buck Me and is capped at $5,000 in Chapter 1. Deposits can never be withdrawn, but they can never be lost either (Game Rant). KeenGamer adds that hitting $5,000 completes the first savings goal and raises your checking limit.
  4. Spend what is left on guns. Every gun you own waits in the Ammu-Nation weapons locker in Chapter 2. Game Rant’s writer spent his leftovers on a fully kitted assault rifle.
  5. Or on clothes. Outfits stay in your wardrobe.
  6. Skip car upgrades. Jason’s cars and their mods disappear, so mod-shop money is wasted (KeenGamer).

Need more cash to reach $5,000? Store robberies, Kingfisher camps and Kenton’s dives are covered in the early game money guide. Planning ahead? The GTA 6 money guide shows what pays best in every chapter.

Step 2: Chapter 1 missables to finish

Shirley’s favours (rum and the scooter)

Shirley is Jason’s closest neighbour. GamesRadar+ says to keep talking to her and do what she asks until her story ends. KeenGamer lists two requests: buy Salty Squid Rum at the Three Sheets liquor store and bring it to her, then steal Peggy’s scooter (Game Rant’s “stealing the old lady’s scooter”). KeenGamer reports that finishing both gives Jason access to Shirley’s Penumbra, which does not respawn if you wreck it.

Meditate with Lucia

After Brian’s mission Down to Earth, Lucia offers to meditate at Jason’s house. GamesRadar+ warns it is easy to miss and that skipping it can lock you out of a trophy/achievement. KeenGamer’s steps: talk to Lucia inside the house until she says she is going to meditate, follow her outside, hold L2/LT to focus on her and press X/A to accept. KeenGamer says this is the first of 18 meditations and the only one at Jason’s house.

Donnie’s lost drugs

GamesRadar+: Cal locates drug drop sites in the swamp southwest of Hamlet, and after the mission Swamped there is a boat to search in the ocean east of Corsair Key. KeenGamer adds that Donnie pays more than Brian’s fence but stops fencing after Swamped, so sell before you start it. Bring an off-road vehicle.

Donnie’s H&L .38 revolver (single source)

KeenGamer reports a unique H&L .38 revolver in the trunk of Donnie’s car during the mission Don Gone: look in the trunk before you get rid of the car. Unique weapons carry over, so it stays with Jason. Not yet confirmed by a second outlet.

Pink side missions around Key Lento

GamesRadar+ sums it up: wrap up all the pink missions in the Key Lento area. Game Rant adds the rest of Mariana County, such as Cal’s fishing competitions.

Jason’s house: all 5 books and magazines (missable)

Books, magazines and comics count toward 100% completion (KeenGamer: 27 in total). The five in Jason’s house are lost with the house. Walk up to each one and examine it. Locations from GamesRadar+:

Lucia’s relationship boosts (earlier deadline: Coming Home)

KeenGamer reports two Chapter 1 ways to raise Lucia’s relationship level that close even earlier, when you pick her up in the mission Coming Home. If you are already past that mission, skip this section.

  • Homecoming errands (marked with an hourglass icon): clothes from Andy, a necklace from The Siren’s Chest in southwest Key Lento, flowers, groceries and a haircut.
  • House repairs: Brian lets Jason live rent-free if he keeps the house in shape. KeenGamer lists the stairs, the leaky roof, the balcony railing and the bedroom wall.

Single-source details (KeenGamer). We will mark them confirmed once a second outlet lists them.

What is NOT missable at the end of Chapter 1

No need to grind everything in the Keys. GamesRadar+ confirms these stay available:

  • Illegal Operations, including the Exotic Animal Front and the two Kingfisher robbery opportunities.
  • The Bigfoot mission chain: available from Chapter 1, but it never expires.
  • Kenton’s shipwrecks and Cal’s fishing challenge: only temporarily disabled.

Bank vaults are an endgame activity anyway: see the GTA 6 bank vault guide.

Make a backup save before Recalibration

GTA 6 has no mission replay or chapter select, and Save Game is greyed out during missions. Before Recalibration (or at the latest before you pick Lucia up), pause → Game → Save Game and use a slot you will never overwrite, labelled in your head as “end of Chapter 1”. If you later find something you missed, you can load it. Full steps and a 15-slot plan: how to save in GTA 6.

Then head to Lucia. In Chapter 2 your first stops are the Ammu-Nation weapons locker and the Port Gellhorn money plan. Keep an eye on the GTA 6 map guide for the new region.

FAQ: GTA 6 Tourist Trap and the end of Chapter 1

When is the point of no return in GTA 6 Chapter 1?

The mission Tourist Trap. It starts when you pick Lucia up from work after Kat and Mattia call to say the pickup job is back on. It locks you into three back-to-back missions and free roam only returns at the start of Chapter 2 (Game Rant, KeenGamer). Until you pick Lucia up, you can keep playing Chapter 1 as long as you like.

What do you lose at the end of Chapter 1 in GTA 6?

Jason’s Key Lento house, his floor stash and his car. Your pocket cash and BuckMe checking balance are reset to zero (Game Rant, GamesRadar+). KeenGamer adds that the Chapter 1 cars, including the Ultimate Edition Enduro and Kayak, and any mods on them are gone too.

What carries over from Chapter 1 to Chapter 2 in GTA 6?

Money in BuckMe Family Savings (up to the $5,000 Chapter 1 limit), guns you own (collect them from the weapons locker at Ammu-Nation), and clothes in your wardrobe (Game Rant). KeenGamer reports that unique weapons and Lucia’s relationship level carry over as well.

Is the Lucia meditation missable in GTA 6?

Yes. The chance to meditate with Lucia at Jason’s house appears after Brian’s mission Down to Earth and is gone once you leave the house at the end of Chapter 1. GamesRadar+ and KeenGamer warn that missing it can block the meditation trophy/achievement on that save, so keep a manual save before Tourist Trap.

Should I spend my money before Tourist Trap in GTA 6?

Yes, after you have put $5,000 into Family Savings. Spend what is left on guns (recoverable from the Ammu-Nation locker in Chapter 2) or clothes. Do not spend it on car upgrades: Jason’s cars do not carry over.

Is Bigfoot missable in GTA 6 Chapter 1?

No. GamesRadar+ says the Bigfoot mission chain can be done from Chapter 1 but does not expire. Illegal Operations such as the Kingfisher robberies also stay available, and Kenton’s shipwrecks and Cal’s fishing are only paused for a while.
