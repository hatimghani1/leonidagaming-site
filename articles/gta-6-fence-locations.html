GTA 6 Fence Locations: All 7 Fences, Who Pays Most and Where to Sell Loot

Robberies in GTA 6 don’t pay you on the spot. Narcotics, jewelry and marked bills go into a stash bag, and they only turn into money when a fence buys them. There are seven fences: five for goods and two for cars. Here is where each one is, when they unlock, who pays the most for what, and the shortcuts (garage pickup and your partner) that save the drive.

Updated 28 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: mission names that unlock fences, Chapters 1–3.

Status on 28 November 2026: the seven fences, their map locations and their rate labels (Very Low to High) match across PowerPyx, PC Gamer, GamesRadar+ and Game Rant. Rockstar has not published dollar rates, so we show the in-game labels only. Garage pickup timing differs between outlets and is credited line by line. Rockstar’s 23 November update (01.002.002) fixed partners not handing over bags for fences. No glitches or exploits.

Fences are the last step of every robbery on the GTA 6 money guide. Steal from jewelry stores, crack safes or empty bank vaults, and the haul is worth nothing until it reaches the right buyer. Cars are a separate trade: see how to sell cars. To place every name below on the map, use our GTA 6 map hub.

How to sell stolen goods, step by step

  1. Lose the police first. Never lead a chase to a fence. Clear your stars and heat with our wanted level escape plan.
  2. Check your bag. Loot sits in a stash bag on your back, or in your car’s trunk if you stored it there (PowerPyx).
  3. Pick the fence by goods. Open the map, filter Robberies (money-bag icon) and hover the green initials to compare rates (PowerPyx, PC Gamer).
  4. Drop the bag in the container at the fence’s icon. Fences are open at any hour (PC Gamer).
  5. Collect the money. Tyrone and Yasmani pay immediately. Brian, Donnie and Dunk pay later. Early in Chapter 1 the cash arrives in the mailbox outside Jason’s home; once your bank account is set up, sales are paid by transfer to BuckMe (PowerPyx). More on that account: BuckMe and Family Savings.

Garage pickup and partner fencing

Sell from your garage (Chapter 3)

Once it unlocks, you can leave stolen goods at any garage you own and choose which fence collects them, so you don’t have to cross the map. PC Gamer says it opens after two Payday Loan jobs for Yasmani and costs an extra fee; PowerPyx says it opens once Yasmani is unlocked. Tech4Gamers adds that the fee comes out of the price and that any garage works, including the free Port Gellhorn Station Garage. For a six-figure vault haul, driving to Yasmani yourself keeps the full price.

Official: two edition garages come with a drop point. Rockstar describes both the Ultimate Edition’s Paradise Garage in Watson Bay and the Vintage Vice City Pack’s Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach as having “a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced” (rockstargames.com/VI). Edition details: Standard vs Ultimate.

Send your partner

When Jason and Lucia are together, put the bag in the trunk of your personal car, hold D-pad Right for partner commands and choose Fence Goods. Your partner drives off and sells it after a while (PowerPyx). PC Gamer says you get a bit less money than selling it yourself, and PowerPyx notes the option is not always offered. Rockstar’s 23 November patch fixed partners failing to hand over bags for fences (patch notes). Button reference: GTA 6 controls.

Pawn shops: what they buy (they are not fences)

A pawn shop will not take your stash bags. It buys collectibles: collectible cards at any pawn shop, from about $20 to over $1,000 each by rarity, and selling a card does not remove it from your Collections list (PowerPyx). Polygon and HowToVideoGame report that pawn shops also buy doubloons and meteorites (for the doubloons, see treasure locations). Pawn shops are also smash ’n’ grab targets, and the jewelry you take from them still goes to a fence.

Mistakes that cost you the haul

  • Dying or getting busted with bags. Bags you carry are usually lost if you are Wasted or Busted, and trunk bags can go too if you don’t escape (Tech4Gamers). Sell or store first, explore later.
  • Selling bills before Vice City. Very Low rates from Brian, Donnie and Dunk waste a big haul.
  • Selling drugs to Yasmani. He pays Medium; Tyrone pays High.
  • Getaway on a bike. A motorcycle leaves you only the bag on your back; vans hold more (Tech4Gamers). Fast picks: fastest cars.
  • Pawn shop for jewelry. Pawn counters buy collectibles only.
  • Ignoring your reputation. How you rob shapes your Criminal Profile.

FAQ: GTA 6 fences

Where are the fences in GTA 6?

There are seven. Goods fences: Brian and Donnie in south Key Lento (Chapter 1 only), Dunk in Port Gellhorn (Chapter 2 on), Tyrone in La Perle and Yasmani in Little Cuba, Vice City (Chapter 3 on). Car fences: Moses in Port Gellhorn and Randy in Balsero, Vice City. All show on the map as green icons with the fence’s initials (PowerPyx, PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, Game Rant).

Who is the best fence in GTA 6?

It depends on the loot. Tyrone in La Perle pays High for narcotics. Yasmani in Little Cuba pays High for jewelry and marked bills. Both pay on the spot, so split your bags between them (PowerPyx, Game Rant, GamesRadar+). Before Chapter 3, Dunk in Port Gellhorn is the best you have.

Can you sell stolen goods at a pawn shop in GTA 6?

No. Pawn shops buy collectibles such as cards, doubloons and meteorites. Narcotics, jewelry and marked bills from robberies only sell to a fence, even jewelry you take from a pawn shop smash and grab (PowerPyx, Polygon).

How do you sell marked bills in GTA 6?

Get the Dye-Pack Jammer in the Chapter 3 mission Credit Check, rob a bank or Payday Loans store, lose the police and take the bags to Yasmani in Little Cuba, who pays High. Tyrone pays Medium; the earlier fences pay Very Low (Tech4Gamers, PowerPyx).

Can you sell stolen goods from your garage in GTA 6?

Yes, from Chapter 3. Leave the bags at a garage you own and choose which fence collects them, for a pickup fee. PC Gamer says this opens after two Payday Loan jobs for Yasmani; PowerPyx says once Yasmani is unlocked.

Do you lose stolen goods if you die in GTA 6?

Usually, yes. Bags you carry are lost if you are Wasted or Busted, and bags in the trunk can go too if you don’t escape after the job. Sell or store a big haul right away (Tech4Gamers).

How do you get the Dime on the Dollar trophy?

Launder $250,000 of marked bills through fences in total. It does not have to be one sale; selling to Yasmani gets you there fastest (Tech4Gamers, XboxAchievements).
