GTA 6 Safe Codes: Every Known Combination and How to Get Random Codes

Most wanted codes: Kingpin Bowling 0300, Brooks Boatworks 6823, La Cascara Motel 6969, and the Chapter 1 Lab Fees doors 0420 and 7700. Story and gang-hideout codes are fixed for every player. Store, pawn shop, bank and jewelry-store safes are mostly random, so you find the code on site, threaten staff for it, or blow the safe with a sticky bomb. Below: every code reported by at least one major guide outlet (with sources), where each clue is, and what to do when a code does not work.

Updated 24 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S launch version. Mission and location names only, no story spoilers.

Status on 24 November 2026: Rockstar has not published a list of safe codes. These are in-game puzzle answers reported by Vice (tested in its own save), PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, Polygon, VGC, Destructoid, Game Rant and Output Lag. Codes confirmed by two or more outlets are marked confirmed; single-outlet or disputed codes are labelled. These are normal gameplay, not cheats, glitches or exploits. If the clue in your own game shows a different number, trust your game.

Safes are one of the best cash sources in robberies and gang hideouts. For the full earning plan by chapter, see the GTA 6 money guide hub; for the Port Gellhorn hideouts these codes belong to, read the Port Gellhorn money guide.

Are GTA 6 safe codes random or fixed?

Both, depending on the safe:

  • Fixed: codes you need for story missions and the safes in gang hideouts (the map calls them Illegal Operations). These are the same for every player, and Vice and VGC confirm you can type a known code without picking up the clue first (Vice, VGC, GamesRadar+).
  • Random: most store, pawn shop, bank and jewelry-store safes you rob in free roam. GamesRadar+ says many of these codes change from player to player, and Output Lag lists store, bank and jewelry safes as having no fixed code. PC Gamer’s summary: many robbery codes are fixed, but some appear to be random.
  • Can change on a repeat visit: Game Rant warns that the Sunrise RV Park big-safe code changes the next time you rob the hideout, so read the notebook again on a replay.

That is why no honest site can give you “the” code for every jewelry store or pawn shop. For those, use the step-by-step method.

Port Gellhorn safe codes (Kolt & Associates)

The first big safes you meet are the three Kolt & Associates hideouts in Chapter 2. All three codes sit on one wall map:

  1. Go to Brooks Boatworks in north Port Gellhorn. Skip the guarded main gate: follow the dirt track to the right, jump the rusted chain gate and walk through the gap in the fence (PC Gamer, Polygon).
  2. Enter the small grey building on the east side, up the outside stairs, next to a blue container and a boat under a tarp. Inside are cash, medicine and the unique Clunker carbine (PC Gamer, Polygon).
  3. Inspect the map of Port Gellhorn on the wall (hold L2/LT). Polygon says it hangs above a calendar with a cat in sunglasses. This saves 6823, 6969 and 0300 to your notes (Vice, Polygon).
  4. Brooks Boatworks (6823): the safe is in the main warehouse, in the room with the pool table. PC Gamer suggests the lift on the side of the warehouse up to the roof and dropping in through an opening. Polygon reports around $5,000 inside.
  5. La Cascara Motel (6969): west Port Gellhorn, south of the bridge to Draper Island. The safe is on the 4th (top) floor, next to the room with the Scrapper VF5C SMG (Game Rant, VGC).
  6. Kingpin Bowling (0300): south Port Gellhorn, office safe on the east side. You meet it in Hit Kingpin Bowling, one of the jobs that help fill the $15,000 Vice City fund (Destructoid, PowerPyx via our Port Gellhorn guide).
Typo warning: one GamesRadar+ list prints the bowling code as “0330”, but GamesRadar+’s own Bowling Alley guide, Vice, VGC, Polygon and Destructoid all give 0300.

How to get a random safe code in GTA 6 (step by step)

  1. Check the map first. Select a robbery target on the map: it shows whether there is a safe, the security level and the rough value of the goods (PC Gamer). Wear a mask if you want to stay anonymous.
  2. Find the safe before you threaten anyone. GamesRadar+ found the “Safe Code?” prompt only appears after you have seen the safe. At Avery Pawn it recommends going in through the back door straight to the office.
  3. Use Focus. Press L3 + R3 (LS + RS) to highlight clues: sticky notes, notebooks, computer screens, wall maps, or a knocked-out guard carrying the code (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+).
  4. Threaten staff. Aim (hold L2/LT) and press Square/X to Threaten. After a couple of threats it becomes Safe Code? (GamesRadar+). Keep at least one employee alive until you have the code.
  5. Enter the code. Interact with the safe, pick Enter Code and type the digits one by one. Known codes are shown on screen and in the keypad’s Notes (Output Lag, GTA BOOM).
  6. Turn off the alarm and leave. At Avery Pawn the alarm panel is in the same office as the safe (GamesRadar+). Speed matters, because every second adds police pressure.

For the store and jewelry jobs where this matters most, see the Vice City money guide (snap-gun back-door jobs and Yasmani’s jewelry jobs) and the bank vaults guide.

How to open a safe without the code

Every keypad safe gives two options: Enter Code or Plant Explosive (Output Lag). A sticky bomb always works:

  • Sticky bombs go on sale at Ammu-Nation after the Chapter 3 mission Credit Checked; before that, some hideouts have them lying around, including Kingpin Bowling (GamesRadar+). Vice adds that most Illegal Operations have crates with them in a nearby room.
  • The catch: an explosion is loud, brings heat, and Output Lag says it can cost part of the cash. Destructoid also recommends using the code at Kingpin Bowling, because explosives attract noise and heat.

GTA 6 safe code not working? 6 fixes

  1. Type the leading zero. 0300 and 0420 are four digits.
  2. Check the digit count. The Grassrivers trailer uses five digits (44017), and some garages and doors use six.
  3. Check it is the right lock. Saber Shipping Yard and Saber Shipping Facility are two different places with different codes, and a garage-door code is not the safe code inside.
  4. Random safe? Stores, pawn shops, banks and jewelry stores usually have random codes. Threaten staff or read the note on site.
  5. Repeat visit? Some hideout codes change when you rob the place again (Game Rant, Sunrise RV Park). Find the clue again.
  6. Still stuck? Use a sticky bomb, or read the clue in your own game: if it shows a different number, your game is right.

Afterwards, put the money somewhere safe. Deposit it at an ATM and make a manual save (how to save in GTA 6), and track every hideout on the GTA 6 100% checklist. Plan routes on the Leonida map guide and get new tips daily on Daily Tips.

FAQ: GTA 6 safe codes

What is the Kingpin Bowling safe code in GTA 6?

0300. Enter all four digits including the leading zero. The code is not inside the bowling alley: it is on a wall map of Port Gellhorn in the small grey office building at Brooks Boatworks, and you can type it without finding the clue first (Vice, VGC, Polygon, Destructoid, GamesRadar+).

What is the Brooks Boatworks safe code in GTA 6?

6823. The same wall map in the small grey building on the east side of the Brooks Boatworks compound also saves the La Cascara Motel (6969) and Kingpin Bowling (0300) codes. The safe is in the room with the pool table inside the main warehouse (PC Gamer, Vice, Polygon).

Are GTA 6 safe codes random?

Some are, some are not. Story-mission and gang-hideout (Illegal Operation) codes are fixed and the same for every player, while store, pawn shop, bank and jewelry-store safes in free-roam robberies are generally randomized, so you must find the code on site or get it from staff (GamesRadar+, PC Gamer, Output Lag).

How do you get a safe code from staff in GTA 6?

Aim at an employee and use the Threaten prompt (hold L2/LT, then Square/X). After a couple of threats it changes to Safe Code?. GamesRadar+ notes the prompt only appears after you have seen the safe, so go to the back office first and keep at least one employee alive.

How do you open a safe without the code in GTA 6?

Use a sticky bomb: every keypad safe offers Enter Code and Plant Explosive. Sticky bombs are sold at Ammu-Nation after the Chapter 3 mission Credit Checked, and some hideouts such as Kingpin Bowling have them lying around. Explosives are loud and can cost part of the cash (GamesRadar+, Output Lag, Vice).

What are the Lab Fees codes in GTA 6?

0420 opens the back-room lab door in the Moonstone Boutique in Key Lento (the clerk tells you, and it is on a sticky note on the back of a notepad), and 7700 opens Brian’s Lockup next to the Isla Tropical Private Club (Brian gives it on the phone) (Game Rant, Output Lag).

Where do I see the safe codes I have found in GTA 6?

Codes you find or are told are saved automatically. They appear on screen when you stand at the matching keypad and in the Notes section of the keypad screen, so you do not have to write them down (Vice, Output Lag, GTA BOOM).
