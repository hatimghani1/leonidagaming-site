GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: All 5 Levels and How to Stay Professional

GTA 6 quietly scores how violent you are. Jason and Lucia each have a Criminal Profile with five levels: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and the permanent Irredeemable. Your level changes how Focus recharges, whether police let you surrender, how your partner feels about you and which ending you get. Here is how each level works, how to check it, and exactly how to climb back to Professional, without spoilers.

Updated 25 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Spoiler-free: no story events or ending details.

Status on 25 November 2026: the five level names, icon colours and how to check them match across hands-on guides from Polygon, PC Gamer, GamesRadar+ and GameSpot. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson confirmed to Polygon that the profile is one of the ending factors. Rockstar has not published the exact thresholds, so the behaviour lists below are reviewer and launch-week reports, credited line by line. Where outlets disagree, we say so.

Quick answer: how to keep a Professional Criminal Profile

  1. Wound, don’t kill. In Focus, shoot the yellow weak spots (hands, elbows, knees, feet), not the red head/heart spots. (Polygon, GameSpot)
  2. Leave civilians alone, including fistfights and running people over in traffic. (Polygon, GameSpot)
  3. Rob clean. Enter through back or side doors and stay undetected; if a witness runs, let them go and just get away. (Polygon)
  4. Defuse when offered. Some missions let you talk someone down or spare an enemy who surrenders. (GameSpot)
  5. Be kind in free roam: rescue civilians, pet dogs and cats, even bin your rubbish. These also refill Focus at Professional/Hardened. (GameSpot, GamesRadar+)
  6. Never harm pets or defenceless animals, and don’t go on cop-killing sprees. (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+)

Threats and intimidation do not hurt a Professional rating (GamesRadar+), so pointing a gun at a clerk to open a safe is fine. Small icons under your wanted stars show when an action has moved your profile (GamesRadar+).

How to check your Criminal Profile

  • Quick view: hold right on the d-pad. The icon sits next to each character’s picture on the wheel (GamesRadar+, Polygon).
  • Full view: press the touchpad (PS5) / View button (Xbox) to open the GTA 6 Index, then go to Jason & Lucia. The same screen shows your relationship heart (GamesRadar+, GameSpot).
  • Live feedback: small icons flash under your wanted level when an action counts, for better or worse (GamesRadar+).

Step-by-step: how to go from Psycho back to Professional

The profile works like a slow sliding scale (Polygon): one clean robbery will not fix a week of chaos, and one accident will not ruin you. Stick to this for a few in-game days:

  1. Save first. If you are Psycho, make a manual save now in a fresh slot, so you can roll back if it tips into Irredeemable (how to save in GTA 6). Mobalytics recommends this the moment you hit Psycho.
  2. Stop the bleeding. No headshots, no civilian hits, no hits on pets. If cops chase you, run, don’t fight: use our step-by-step escape.
  3. Switch to limb shots. In gunfights, hold Focus and aim for the yellow markers. A yellow hit marker means the enemy is incapacitated; don’t shoot them again (GameSpot, GTA Wiki).
  4. Drive carefully. Accidental pedestrian deaths count, so slow down in busy streets (GameSpot, Polygon).
  5. Do two or three clean robberies. Back door, no alarms, no casualties. GamesRadar+ says a couple of non-violent robberies is enough to lift Hardened back to Professional. Our Vice City money guide lists good targets.
  6. Pick the calm option in missions that offer “defuse” or “spare” prompts (GameSpot).
  7. Do small good deeds: help people in trouble, pet animals, bin rubbish (GameSpot). They also refill Focus.
  8. Check the icon on the d-pad wheel each session, and repeat for the other character: Jason and Lucia are tracked separately (Polygon).
Tip: want chaos without consequences? Do it on a separate save slot and reload afterwards. Don’t overwrite your main story save after a rampage (Game Rant, GameSpot).

Irredeemable: the point of no return

Irredeemable is the only level you cannot leave. You reach it by staying at extreme Psycho behaviour for a long time (Polygon, PC Gamer). The warning sign: a crack or scratch through the Psycho icon (GameSpot, GamesRadar+). If you see it, stop and save to a new slot.

What changes once a character is Irredeemable:

  • Permanent: good deeds will not remove it; only loading an older save does (Game Rant, Mobalytics, PC Gamer).
  • No surrender, and police shoot on sight even at a 1-star wanted level (Game Rant, Mobalytics, GamesRadar+).
  • Relationship damage: GamesRadar+ says it drives a wedge between Jason and Lucia that may never fully recover (see relationship status).
  • Civilians react differently and are easier to intimidate in robberies (GamesRadar+, Game Rant).
  • Ending impact: Game Rant reports it locks you out of the best endings (details below, no spoilers).

The in-game warning itself, per Polygon, says Irredeemable “may have irreversible consequences”, and PC Gamer quotes Rockstar that it “will have a lasting impact on Jason and Lucia”.

Missions, choices and both characters

  • Story crimes don’t count. Violence a mission requires does not affect your profile; only extra, unnecessary violence does (GamesRadar+).
  • Choices do count. Some missions offer defuse or spare options that lead to non-lethal outcomes (GameSpot).
  • Separate profiles. Jason can be Psycho while Lucia is Professional (Polygon, Mobalytics), but GamesRadar+ notes one character’s behaviour influences the other when you are out together.
  • Wanted level is separate. Your stars reset once you lose the cops; the profile is a long-term score. Killing cops raises both (wanted level & Criminal Profile).

Does the Criminal Profile affect the ending? (spoiler-free)

Yes. Rob Nelson told Polygon there are several endings, shaped by story choices, the relationship, your Criminal Profiles and your Family Savings balance. He also said there are no strictly “good” or “bad” endings, just ones that match how you played.

Game Rant’s spoiler-free target for the best possible outcome is both profiles at Professional or Hardened, plus Hot Together and $2,000,000 in Family Savings before the final point of no return. Our GTA 6 money guide covers the savings side. Rockstar has not published exact ending conditions, so treat these as reviewer findings.

FAQ: GTA 6 Criminal Profile

What is the Criminal Profile in GTA 6?

It is GTA 6's reputation system, similar to Red Dead Redemption 2's honor. It tracks how violent Jason and Lucia are, separately, and rates each one Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho or Irredeemable. It changes your Focus bonuses, how police treat you, the relationship and the ending (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, Polygon).

How do you check your Criminal Profile in GTA 6?

Hold right on the d-pad to see the icon next to each character's picture, or open the GTA 6 Index (touchpad / View button) and go to the Jason & Lucia menu for the full view (GamesRadar+, Polygon, GameSpot).

How do you get a Professional Criminal Profile in GTA 6?

Avoid unnecessary violence for a while: incapacitate enemies by shooting the yellow weak spots (hands, elbows, knees, feet) in Focus, do not hurt civilians or run people over, rob clean and undetected through back doors, and choose to defuse or spare when a mission offers it (Polygon, GameSpot). Threats do not count against you (GamesRadar+).

Can you get rid of Irredeemable in GTA 6?

No. Irredeemable is permanent for that character, even if you play cleanly afterwards. The only fix is loading a save from before it happened, so make a manual save when you hit Psycho or see the crack on the icon (Game Rant, GameSpot, PC Gamer).

Does the Criminal Profile affect the ending in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told Polygon the Criminal Profile is one of the factors behind your ending, alongside story choices, the relationship and Family Savings. Game Rant reports Irredeemable locks you out of the best endings. Exact ending conditions are not published by Rockstar.

Do missions affect your Criminal Profile in GTA 6?

Crimes a mission requires do not count against you; only violence beyond what the job needs does (GamesRadar+). Some missions offer choices such as defusing a situation or sparing someone who surrenders, which help keep you Professional (GameSpot).

Why can't I surrender to the police in GTA 6?

Surrender is removed when your character is Psycho or Irredeemable (PC Gamer, GameSpot, GamesRadar+). Game Rant and Mobalytics add that at Irredeemable police will open fire even at a 1-star wanted level. Calm down until the profile drops back to Violent or lower.

Do Jason and Lucia share a Criminal Profile?

No. Each has their own profile, so Jason can be Psycho while Lucia is Professional (Polygon). GamesRadar+ notes one character's behaviour can still influence the other when you are out together.
