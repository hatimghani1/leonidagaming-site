GTA 6 Online release date: does GTA 6 have multiplayer?

Short answer: not yet. GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 as a single-player game, and Rockstar has not announced GTA 6 Online or given it a date. Here is what is official, what is only a claim, what Rockstar’s past launches tell us, and what to play with friends right now.

Updated 22 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). No story spoilers.

Status on 22 November 2026: still no GTA 6 Online announcement, no release date, no beta, and no multiplayer mode in the game. Rockstar’s store pages describe GTA VI as a single-player experience, and Take-Two’s 20 November launch-sales release ($3.79 billion day one, 32.5 million units sold before release) doesn’t mention online either. Reuters reports investors are still waiting to hear Take-Two’s plans and that some analysts expect the PC version and online mode in 2027. That is an analyst expectation, not an announcement. We’ll update this page as soon as Rockstar says anything official.

Quick answer

  • Does GTA 6 have online? No. GTA VI is a single-player experience at launch. (Official: PlayStation Store FAQ, Take-Two pre-order release.)
  • GTA 6 Online release date? Not announced. No name, price, platforms or beta. (Official silence: Rockstar, Take-Two.)
  • Any timeframe at all? Twitch CEO Dan Clancy said multiplayer launches “next year” (2027). (Claim by a third party, not confirmed by Rockstar.)
  • Is GTA Online (GTA 5) ending? No. Take-Two says it will keep getting new content and events. (Official: Take-Two, 9 November 2026.)

What Rockstar and Take-Two have actually said

The official sources all say the same thing: GTA VI is a single-player game, and nothing about a new online mode has been announced.

  • PlayStation Store FAQ: to the question “Does Grand Theft Auto VI have any multiplayer modes or features?” the official PlayStation page answers: “Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience.” The listing also shows 1 player and offline play enabled.
  • Take-Two pre-order release (24 June 2026): the announcement says GTA VI “features a single-player experience”. The only online element mentioned is GTA+ for the existing GTA Online.
  • Take-Two shareholder meeting (17 September 2026): “GTA 6 is a single-player title and is due to release this November. We will continue to support GTA Online after GTA 6 release and beyond.” (As quoted by Gear Live and Game Rant.)
  • Rockstar VP of design Stephen Donkin (Kotaku, 18 November 2026): asked why the map’s casinos can’t be entered, he told Kotaku: “The map has been built for the long term and with many factors in mind. So there are a few areas on the map that give us options in the future should we have a gameplay or narrative need for them.” Kotaku says Rockstar gave no further detail. It is not an online or DLC announcement.
  • Take-Two launch-sales release (20 November 2026): the announcement reports $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and gives no online or PC news.
  • Rockstar “Out Now” page (19 November 2026): the launch announcement lists PS5 and Xbox Series X|S and says nothing about multiplayer.

None of these statements rule out a future online mode. They just mean that, today, there is nothing official to report beyond “single-player now, online not announced.”

The “GTA 6 multiplayer in 2027” claim (unconfirmed)

On 17 September 2026, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg: “We spent a lot of time talking with Rockstar about their plans… You are going to see this big bump with GTA 6, but then an even bigger one next year when they launch multiplayer.” PC Gamer, The Verge and Kotaku all reported the quote.

Treat 2027 as a claim, not a date. Clancy doesn’t work for Rockstar, Bloomberg described it as something he anticipates, and neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has repeated it. “Next year” is also a 12-month window. Don’t trust any site that gives you a specific GTA 6 Online day.

Analysts point the same way. Reuters (21 November) says “some industry analysts are expecting the PC launch and online mode in 2027”, and TD Cowen’s Doug Creutz calls “when” and “how big” the next GTA Online will be “the most important question for the stock”. Analysts don’t speak for Rockstar either, so 2027 is still a forecast, not a date.

Take-Two has also not said what form online play would take: a new mode inside GTA VI, a stand-alone game, or an update to the existing GTA Online. Anyone telling you which one is guessing.

Rockstar’s last three big launches each handled multiplayer differently, so history shows a separate, later online launch is normal, but it can’t predict the date.

What happens to GTA Online (GTA 5) now

It keeps going. In Take-Two’s prepared remarks for its 9 November 2026 earnings call, the company said “Rockstar Games will continue to support Grand Theft Auto Online with new content and ongoing events for its vast community.” The same day, CEO Strauss Zelnick told IGN: “We intend to keep supporting GTA Online and it has been very successful.”

  • Your GTA Online character, cash and properties are untouched by GTA VI’s release.
  • GTA Online still runs on PS4, PS5, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S and PC. GTA VI is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only.
  • Rockstar has not said whether anything will carry over to a future GTA 6 online mode. Ignore videos promising “transfer your GTA 5 money to GTA 6”.

GTA+ in GTA 6 and the free month: what it is (and isn’t)

Digital purchases of GTA VI (Standard or Ultimate) made before 20 November 2026 include one month of GTA+ ($7.99 a month in the US after that). It is not a GTA 6 online pass, but it is no longer only for GTA Online and the GTA+ Games Library either.

Updated 21 November 2026: Rockstar’s GTA+ page now has a Grand Theft Auto VI section of “optional benefits” that work in story mode, as quoted by Polygon, GamesRadar+ and GTA 6 Everything:
  • ’90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy (widebody sports car) and a single-car garage in Crosstown, “available to claim from Chapter 3”. Reports say the garage has a weapon locker and lets you fence stolen goods.
  • Transit bonuses: Scooter Bros Premium, premium RydeMe membership, free scooter and bike rentals (Polygon adds Seashark rentals) and Leonida metro benefits.
  • 10% store discount at select weapon and clothes shops, hairdressers, tattooists and vehicle mod shops.
  • 10% more when you sell owned vehicles. Useful alongside our car-selling guide and the money guide hub.

Rockstar’s fine print says an active membership and an internet connection are required to access and claim the benefits. Outlets disagree on whether a claimed Elegy and garage stay after you cancel (Polygon says you lose access; GTABase and GTA Boom say claimed items remain), so don’t subscribe just for that until Rockstar clarifies. Prefer a pure run? Polygon reports you can switch the boosts off in the pause menu under Account › GTA Plus Boosts. None of this is needed to finish the story.

How to claim it on PS5 (official steps)

  1. Make sure your digital GTA VI purchase was made before 20 November 2026.
  2. Open the GTA+ product page on the PlayStation Store.
  3. Follow the on-screen steps to check out at no extra cost.
  4. Note the date the free month turns into a paid subscription. To avoid being charged, cancel in Settings › Users and Accounts › Account › Payment and Subscriptions › Subscriptions before that date.

Per the PlayStation FAQ, you must claim it by 31 March 2027, it works once per account, and existing GTA+ members get the month added to their current subscription. Some regions are excluded. Missed the cutoff? See our Vintage Vice City Pack guide for what you can still buy separately.

Fake “GTA 6 Online beta” and “early access” offers

Because there’s no GTA 6 Online yet, any site, Discord or video offering a GTA 6 Online beta key, early-access invite, “online unlock”, mod menu or free Shark Cards for GTA 6 is fake. These are the same tricks security firms flagged around fake PC downloads: survey walls, fake sign-in pages and password-stealing files. Never give your Rockstar, PlayStation or Xbox login to a third-party site. Our PC release date guide lists what to do if you already ran a suspicious file.

GTA 6 Online FAQ

Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

No, not at launch. The official PlayStation Store FAQ says “Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience,” and the store listing shows 1 player. There is no co-op, free mode, or online lobby in the game as of 22 November 2026.

When is the GTA 6 Online release date?

There is no official date. Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced GTA 6 Online, its name, price, or platforms. The only timeframes in circulation are Twitch CEO Dan Clancy telling Bloomberg in September 2026 that Rockstar would launch multiplayer “next year” (2027), and analysts quoted by Reuters on 21 November who expect online and PC in 2027. Rockstar has not confirmed either.

Will GTA Online (GTA 5) shut down now that GTA 6 is out?

No. Take-Two said on 9 November 2026 that Rockstar will continue to support GTA Online with new content and ongoing events, and CEO Strauss Zelnick told IGN: “We intend to keep supporting GTA Online.” No end date has been announced.

Does GTA+ do anything in GTA 6?

Yes, since launch. Rockstar’s GTA+ page now lists optional GTA VI story-mode benefits: the ’90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy widebody and a Crosstown garage (claimable from Chapter 3), transit perks, 10% off at select Leonida shops and 10% more for owned vehicle sales. An active membership and an internet connection are required. GTA+ costs $7.99 a month in the US; the free month for digital purchases before 20 November 2026 must be claimed on PlayStation by 31 March 2027 and auto-renews unless cancelled. It is still not a GTA 6 Online pass.

Will GTA 6 get DLC or new map areas?

Nothing is announced. Rockstar’s Stephen Donkin told Kotaku on 18 November 2026 that the map “has been built for the long term” and has “a few areas” that give Rockstar options if a future gameplay or narrative need comes up. He did not say what those areas are or confirm any DLC, expansion or online mode.

Will my GTA Online character transfer to GTA 6?

Unknown. Rockstar has not said anything about character, money, or progress transfers to a future GTA 6 online mode. Be wary of any site or video claiming otherwise.
