GTA 6 Family Savings and BuckMe: Every Goal, Limit and Perk

Family Savings is the shared BuckMe account Jason and Lucia pay into for their future. Money you move in can never be withdrawn, but it is never lost: not on death, arrest or the Chapter 1 reset. There are six goals, from $5,000 to $2,000,000, that raise your checking limit, unlock discounts and feed into the ending. Below: how to deposit, every goal and perk, when your bank limit goes up, and exactly how much to save in each chapter.

Updated 26 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: mission names only, no story events or ending details.

Status on 26 November 2026: the goal amounts, tier names and perks come from PowerPyx’s Save for the Future walkthrough and match Beebom and gta.guide. The timing of checking-account upgrades is Game Rant’s. Outlets still differ on two details (the livery discount and the last tier’s reward), and we flag both below. Rockstar Support only confirms that BuckMe limits rise as the story progresses. These are launch-week player reports, not official Rockstar figures.

This is a spoke of our GTA 6 money guide. Family Savings decides how much money you carry out of Chapter 1, and later it is where your jewelry store and bank vault profits can go once checking is full.

Quick answer: GTA 6 Family Savings

  1. It unlocks in Chapter 1 after the main mission Buck Me at the Fleeca branch in Key Lento. The long-running goal Save for the Future starts right after it. (gta.guide, PowerPyx)
  2. Deposit: phone (Up on the D-pad) > BuckMe > Family Savings > Buck Yourself. Pocket cash has to go into checking at an ATM first. (GamesRadar+, gta.guide)
  3. It is one-way. You can never take the money out, and it is never lost.
  4. Chapter 1: fill the $5,000 goal before Tourist Trap. It is the only money that carries over.
  5. Later goals: $10,000, $100,000, $500,000, $1,000,000 and $2,000,000, each opening after more story progress. (PowerPyx, Beebom)
  6. Keep enough in checking for story goals and purchases. Savings never count toward a price.

How BuckMe works: cash, checking and savings

GTA 6 splits your money into three places. Knowing which is which stops you losing a heist payout to a bad death.

WhereWhat it doesLost on death or arrest?
Pocket cashPays for purchases up to $1,000. Bank it at any ATM or bank branch (Deposit menu).Yes, all of it (gta.guide, Dork)
BuckMe CheckingOne per character (Jason and Lucia each have their own). Pays for anything over $1,000 and has a balance limit that rises with the story.No, but an arrest takes a bail fee from it (Game Rant)
BuckMe Family SavingsOne shared account. Money goes in from checking and never comes out. Goals raise your checking limit and unlock perks.Never

Before Buck Me, the only safe place for money is Jason’s floorboard stash in the bedroom of his Key Lento house (gta.guide). When the mission ends, BuckMe puts $100 in your checking account and the tutorial moves it into Family Savings, so you start the goal at $100.

How to put money in Family Savings

  1. Bank your cash. Walk up to any ATM or bank branch and choose Deposit. You cannot send pocket cash to savings straight from the app. The BuckMe ATM Finder can put the nearest machine on your map (gta.guide).
  2. Open the phone with Up on the D-pad and launch the purple BuckMe app.
  3. Select Family Savings to see your balance and the current goal.
  4. Press Buck Yourself and choose how much to move from the current character’s checking account (GamesRadar+). Lucia can pay in from her own checking account too.
  5. Check the goal. You cannot save past the current goal. Track it any time under Map > storybook > Save for the Future, which has no map marker (PowerPyx).

All Family Savings goals and perks

Save for the Future runs for the whole story. Each goal you hit upgrades your BuckMe account, and the next goal only appears after more story progress (PowerPyx). Tier names are the in-app ones.

  • Interest: the in-game quest text promises “higher interest payments” as you hit goals (quoted by PowerPyx), and GamesRadar+ says hitting a target before the limit rises earns a big interest boost. Interest is paid into Family Savings, so it is still money you cannot spend.
  • Premium is free at Chapter 4. You get BuckMe Premium automatically when Chapter 4 starts, whatever your savings balance. The text message calls it BuckMe Black (PowerPyx, gta.guide).

BuckMe checking limits and when they go up

If a payment does not arrive, your checking account is probably full. Incoming money then sits as pending until there is room. You do not lose it (Game Rant, Dork). Game Rant reports these story upgrade points:

AccountChecking limitWhen you get it (Game Rant)
Regular$15,000Buck Me, Chapter 1
Silver / BuckeDry$100,000During Coffee Break, Chapter 2, after the map scene in the coffee shop
Gold$500,000After Credit Check, Chapter 3
Premium$2,000,000 (maximum)Start of Chapter 4

Checking full right now? Spend some (guns, a garage, car upgrades), or move the overflow into Family Savings if the current goal is not full yet. Pending payments land as soon as the next upgrade arrives.

How much to save in each chapter

Chapter 1: exactly $5,000

Cash and checking money do not survive the end of Chapter 1. Only Family Savings, owned guns (collect them later from the Ammu-Nation weapons locker) and clothes carry over (Game Rant). Put $5,000 in before Tourist Trap and spend what is left. Stores, gang hideouts, races and Kenton’s treasure dives get you there. Full list: things to do before Tourist Trap.

Chapter 2: protect $15,000 in checking first

The late Chapter 2 mission Prepare for Vice City asks you to have at least $15,000, and savings do not count toward it (PowerPyx). Hit that first, then reach the $10,000 goal for 20% off RydeMe rides. Selling cars to Moses and the Port Gellhorn money guide cover the fastest cash.

Chapter 3: $100,000 before you buy garages

Jewelry stores, Payday Loans and the first bank jobs pay well here. If you plan to buy garages, reach the $100,000 goal first: the 20% discount saves more than the wait costs. Keep the garage price in checking too, because the money in savings cannot pay for it. Guides: Vice City money, jewelry stores, Randy’s Wish List.

Chapter 4 and endgame: $500,000 to $2,000,000

Premium arrives for free at the start of Chapter 4, so the last three goals are about interest and the ending, not limits. Bank vaults (up to about $205,000 each, PowerPyx) are the quickest way to $2,000,000.

Does Family Savings change the ending?

Yes, it is one of the factors. Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson told Polygon that the ending depends on your choices at branching story moments, your relationship status, your Criminal Profiles and how much is in Family Savings when the credits roll. GamesRadar+ says the balance also changes some character interactions. Beebom and GTABase report that the full $2,000,000 matters for the best outcome. If you care about the ending, keep feeding the account and keep a manual save before the final missions. We do not describe the endings here.

Is Family Savings worth it?

  • The first $5,000: always. It is the only money that survives Chapter 1.
  • $10,000 and $100,000: usually. RydeMe and garage discounts pay you back if you use fast travel or buy property.
  • $500,000: optional. You get Premium free at Chapter 4 anyway, so the 10% livery discount is the only extra.
  • $1,000,000 and $2,000,000: for the ending and 100% players. No perks or higher limits, but interest and an ending factor. PowerPyx calls these goals optional and not needed to finish the story.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Saving story money. Savings never pay for anything, including the $15,000 Prepare for Vice City goal.
  • Carrying cash. Pocket cash is gone on death or arrest. Bank after every robbery. See the wanted level guide for clean escapes.
  • Ignoring a full account. Pending payments wait for space; move overflow into savings or spend it.
  • Expecting an exploit. There is no legitimate duplication trick, and we do not cover glitches. Make money with the methods in the money guide.
  • Forgetting the checklist. Track Save for the Future alongside everything else on the GTA 6 100% checklist.

FAQ: GTA 6 Family Savings

What is Family Savings in GTA 6?

A shared BuckMe savings account for Jason and Lucia that opens in Chapter 1 after the mission Buck Me. Either character can move money into it from their own checking account with Buck Yourself in the BuckMe phone app. The money can never be withdrawn or spent, but it is never lost either (GamesRadar+, gta.guide).

Can you withdraw money from Family Savings in GTA 6?

No. Every outlet that has tested it (GamesRadar+, Game Rant, gta.guide, PowerPyx) agrees the transfer is one-way. Only move money you will not need for guns, cars, garages or story goals such as the $15,000 Prepare for Vice City target, which counts checking money only.

What are the Family Savings goals in GTA 6?

PowerPyx and Beebom list six: Buckin’ Broke $5,000, BuckeDry $10,000, BuckMe Gold $100,000, BuckMe Premium $500,000, Rich as Buck $1,000,000 and a final $2,000,000 goal. Each new goal only opens after more story progress, so you cannot fill the account in one go.

Should I put $5,000 in Family Savings in Chapter 1?

Yes. Cash and checking money are lost at the end of Chapter 1, and Family Savings is the only money that carries over. The Chapter 1 cap is $5,000, so fill it before Tourist Trap and spend the rest on guns or clothes (Game Rant, PowerPyx).

How do I increase my BuckMe bank account limit in GTA 6?

Play the story. Game Rant reports the first upgrade during Coffee Break in Chapter 2 ($100,000), BuckMe Gold after Credit Check in Chapter 3 ($500,000) and BuckMe Premium at the start of Chapter 4 ($2,000,000, the maximum). Payments that arrive while you are full wait as pending and go through after the upgrade.

Does Family Savings affect the ending of GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar North’s Rob Nelson told Polygon that the money in Family Savings is one of the factors behind the ending, with your choices, your relationship status and your Criminal Profiles. Beebom and GTABase report that the full $2,000,000 matters for the best outcome. We keep the details spoiler-free.

Is Family Savings worth it in GTA 6?

The first $5,000 always is. After that it depends on your play style: the mid goals bring discounts on RydeMe rides, garages and liveries, the last two goals add no perk but pay interest (PowerPyx reports $200,000 at the $2,000,000 goal), and the balance feeds the ending.
