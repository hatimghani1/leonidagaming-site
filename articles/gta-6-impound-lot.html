GTA 6 Impound Lot: Every Location and How to Get Your Impounded Car Back

Got busted and your car vanished? In GTA 6 the police tow your registered cars to an impound lot, and the car is not lost. You have two ways to get it back: steal it back for free (and take a wanted level), or pay the Scooter Bros app to bring it to you from Chapter 2. Here is how to find which lot holds your car, all five known impound locations, what retrieval costs, and how to stop it happening again.

Updated 26 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: Chapter 1–2 mission names only.

Status on 26 November 2026: the two retrieval methods, the Scooter Bros unlock (Chapter 2 mission Moses) and the Premium price are confirmed by several hands-on guides (Polygon, Game8, Tech4Gamers, PowerPyx, gta.guide). Rockstar has not published an official impound list, so the lot locations and fees below are launch-week player reports, credited line by line. Where outlets disagree, we say so. No glitches or exploits.

Why your car gets impounded in GTA 6

Only registered personal vehicles are tracked by the game, so only they end up in a lot. Launch guides list three triggers (Tech4Gamers, gta.guide):

  • You get Busted while driving it.
  • You get Wasted while driving it.
  • You leave it inside the police search area. Ditching your car can help you escape, but officers who find it tow it.

It can happen as early as Chapter 1: the game shows a message that Jason’s vehicle “was recognized by the cops and impounded” and tells you to check the map (Tech4Gamers, gameta.gg). Police recognise a car after a chase until you respray it, which is why the vehicle heat icon matters.

How to find your impounded car

  1. Open the map and switch the index to the Personal tab. It lists Impounded Vehicles next to your home, garages and cash deposits (gameta.gg, Tech4Gamers).
  2. Select the car. Its card shows the model name and the line “Impounded Vehicle”.
  3. Set Destination to get a GPS route to the lot (HowToVideoGame).
  4. From Chapter 2 you can also check the Scooter Bros app: impounded cars sit in their own list with a police badge icon (Polygon, HowToVideoGame).

The car goes to the impound lot nearest to where police took it (Tech4Gamers), so a car lost in Port Gellhorn will not be waiting in Vice City.

Method 1: steal your car back from the impound (free)

The game itself tells you that you can try to steal the car back from the lot (gameta.gg). It costs nothing, works in every chapter, and is the only option in Chapter 1.

  1. Bank your cash first. You will get a wanted level, and pocket cash is lost if you are Busted or Wasted. Use an ATM on the way (BuckMe guide).
  2. Plan the escape. Set a waypoint far from town before you go in (Polygon).
  3. Get inside. Some gates stand open. A locked gate has a Break Open prompt (Square on PS5) with a short timing game, or climb the fence or wall, as Jason does at Hamlet (HowToVideoGame, gameta.gg, Tech4Gamers).
  4. Drive it out. Ram the front gate, or open it from the guard house first to avoid damage (Tech4Gamers). The game reports a crime as you leave.
  5. Lose the cops. Reports range from 1 star (HowToVideoGame, Key Lento) to 1–2 stars (Game8) and 2 stars in the story mission (Tech4Gamers). Break line of sight and leave the search area: step-by-step escape plan.
  6. Respray it. A Pay ’n’ Spray stops police recognising the car, so it does not get towed again straight away (Game8, gameta.gg).
Criminal Profile: don’t shoot your way out. Hurting officers pushes Jason or Lucia toward Violent and Psycho. Drive, don’t fight: see the Criminal Profile guide.

Method 2: pay Scooter Bros to retrieve your car (Chapter 2+)

Scooter Bros is the phone app that manages your registered cars. It unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission Moses (Take a Vehicle to Moses) (Polygon, Game8, PowerPyx, gta.guide). In Chapter 1 there is no app; Joey the mechanic can bring Jason’s car, but impounded cars must be stolen back (gta.guide).

  1. Take out your phone (Up on the d-pad) and open Scooter Bros (green car icon) (PowerPyx, HowToVideoGame).
  2. Pick the car under Impounded Vehicles; it has a police badge icon.
  3. Choose the first option, retrieve, and check the quote. The fee is paid from your BuckMe account (Tech4Gamers, HowToVideoGame).
  4. Wait for the notification that the car has been delivered.

Where does the car arrive? Polygon, Tech4Gamers and HowToVideoGame say the car is delivered near you; gameta.gg saw a message assigning the retrieved car to one of your garages. It may depend on the situation; check the notification and the map after paying. The fee is not fixed and rises with distance and damage (TheGamer).

Scooter Bros also recovers a registered car that was destroyed, so a wreck is not the end either (HowToVideoGame).

Scooter Bros Premium and daily credits

  • Price: a one-time $12,500 upgrade inside the app (PowerPyx, TheGamer, gta.guide, Tech4Gamers). Game Rant saw a $12,000 prompt.
  • Benefit: three free credits per in-game day, usable on deliveries, repairs and impound retrievals. They refill when a new in-game day starts (gta.guide, Game Rant).
  • Impound rule: a retrieval with credits uses all of that day’s remaining credits, so you can only do one per day this way. Wait until you have one credit left, then use it on the impound (Polygon, Game8).
  • GTA+: Rockstar lists Scooter Bros Premium among the GTA+ transit perks for GTA VI story mode. See what GTA+ gives you in GTA 6.

Worth it? If you call your car several times a day, yes. Early on, $12,500 is better spent elsewhere: check the GTA 6 money guide for the fastest legit cash by chapter.

The Chapter 2 “Impounded Vehicle” mission

The game teaches impounds with a Chapter 2 job in Port Gellhorn. Per Tech4Gamers, a car is towed automatically after Breakfast Run, and Impounded Vehicle appears on the map once Coffee Break ends, as one of four jobs you can do in any order with either character.

  1. Go to the lot by the Port Gellhorn police station (green car icon).
  2. Get through the fence, take the car and drive out.
  3. Two officers on site give you a 2-star wanted level; lose them to finish.

Tech4Gamers says the car is Lucia’s; gameta.gg saw Jason’s ’72 Tulip towed at a set point in Chapter 2. It is a short, low-risk job. The rest of the chapter’s money jobs are in our Port Gellhorn money guide.

Trunk loot and stash bags

Your trunk can hold owned weapons and stash bags, so an impound can cost more than the car. Tech4Gamers reports that stash bags can go missing with a paid Scooter Bros delivery, and that bags the police removed may be sitting in a police car near the lot. This is a single-source report, but the safe play is simple:

  • If loot is in the trunk, fetch the car yourself and check nearby cruisers before you leave.
  • Sell stolen goods to a fence before long drives (where to sell jewelry).

How to stop your car getting impounded

  • Do crimes in stolen, unregistered cars. If police take one, you lose nothing (Tech4Gamers). Sell the good ones later: how to sell cars.
  • Respray after every chase. A Pay ’n’ Spray clears the vehicle heat so police stop recognising it.
  • Don’t abandon your own car inside the flashing search area.
  • Surrender less, escape more. Being Busted in your car sends it to the lot. Know your escape routes: wanted level guide.
  • Save before risky jobs so a bad chase doesn’t cost your favourite car: how to save in GTA 6.
  • Rare cars like the fastest cars in GTA 6 or Randy’s Wish List rides deserve extra care.

FAQ: GTA 6 impound lot

Where is the impound lot in GTA 6?

There are several. Launch guides have found police impounds in Key Lento (by the Key Lento Medical Offices), Hamlet (Penny Bun Street), Port Gellhorn (across from the police station on Butternut Rd), Vice Beach (Vice City Parking Department, Ocean Beach) and the Vice City Police Department in Tequesta. Your car goes to the lot nearest to where police took it, and the map marks it (Game8, gameta.gg, Tech4Gamers).

How do you get your impounded car back in GTA 6?

Two ways. Free: go to the lot, climb the fence or break open the gate, and drive your car out, then lose the 1–2 star wanted level. Paid: from Chapter 2 (after the mission Moses) open the Scooter Bros app, pick the car with the police badge icon and pay the retrieval fee (Polygon, Game8, Tech4Gamers).

How much does it cost to get your car out of impound in GTA 6?

Stealing it back costs nothing but a wanted level. A Scooter Bros retrieval is a $100 base rate plus a retrieval and distance charge: reported quotes include $550 and $604 (HowToVideoGame) and $699 from Port Gellhorn to Vice City (Polygon). With Scooter Bros Premium you can use your daily credits instead.

Why did my car get impounded in GTA 6?

Police tow a registered personal vehicle if you are Busted or Wasted in it, or if you leave it inside the police search area. In Chapter 1 you can also get the message that Jason’s vehicle was recognised by the cops and impounded. Respraying the car at a Pay ’n’ Spray after a chase stops police recognising it (Tech4Gamers, gta.guide, gameta.gg).

Can you get a stolen car back from the impound in GTA 6?

Only if you registered it. The impound and Scooter Bros only track registered personal vehicles. A stolen car you never registered is simply gone once police take it, so do crimes in cars you don’t care about (Tech4Gamers, gta.guide).

Is Scooter Bros Premium worth it for impounded cars?

Premium is a one-time $12,500 upgrade (PowerPyx, TheGamer, gta.guide; Game Rant saw $12,000) that gives three free credits per in-game day. An impound retrieval uses all of the day’s remaining credits, so use it when you have one credit left (Polygon, Game8). It pays off if you call your cars often.

Do you lose what is in the trunk when your car is impounded?

You can. Tech4Gamers reports stash bags can go missing with a paid Scooter Bros delivery, and that confiscated bags may sit in a police car near the lot. If valuable loot is in the trunk, fetch the car yourself. This is a single-source report, so bank cash and sell loot before risky driving.
