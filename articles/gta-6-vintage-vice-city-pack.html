GTA 6 Vintage Vice City Pack: what’s inside, the deadline, and how to get it now

The free window for the Vintage Vice City Pack has closed, and the pack is now sold separately. This guide covers who got it free, what it costs now, and how to claim the ’55 Vapid Stanier, outfits and weapon pattern in-game.

Updated 20 November 2026. Light spoilers: mentions that pack items unlock in Chapter 3 and names one Chapter 1 mission (“Coming Home”). No plot details.

Updated 20 November 2026. Rockstar said pre-orders and purchases before 20 November 2026 included the pack. As of today, the pack is listed as a separate add-on on the Xbox Store and PlayStation Store. Pack contents and eligibility are official. The in-game claim steps are a launch-day report from PC Gamer.

Just bought GTA 6? Start with the Daily Tips page for file size, editions, and PC/Online status. Then come back here to find out whether you still have the retro bonus or need to buy it.

Quick answer

  • What it is: a pre-order and launch-window bonus with the ’55 Vapid Stanier sedan and a garage, retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti’s palm tree shirt. (Official: Rockstar Support, store listings.)
  • Free cutoff: purchases before 20 November 2026. PlayStation’s offer terms say “by November 19, 2026.” (Official.)
  • Bought it later? The pack is now listed separately: $4.99 on the US Xbox Store and £4.49 on the UK PlayStation Store, both with a release date of 20 November 2026. (Store listings checked 20 November 2026. We have not checked prices in other regions. Check your own store.)
  • How to claim it: the pack shows up in the in-game store after mission 11, “Coming Home” (Chapter 1). You can’t claim the items until Chapter 3. (Launch-day report: PC Gamer, 18 November 2026.)

What’s in the Vintage Vice City Pack (official)

The official store copy and Rockstar Support list three items:

ItemWhat official copy says
’55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and GarageA classic sedan for cruising Shore Drive. It comes with the Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach, which has a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced.
Outfits and hairstylesJason gets a linen suit in vintage pastel. Lucia gets a red sequin mini dress and curls.
Exclusive weapon patternA tropical pattern for “most guns,” inspired by Tommy Vercetti’s palm tree button-up.

The store listings also say: “All items will be available to Jason and Lucia as their story progresses.” So the pack is cosmetic and convenience content. It isn’t a shortcut through the story. More officially named cars are on the vehicles page.

Who got it for free?

According to Rockstar Support (last updated 18 November 2026):

  • Digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition (Standard or Ultimate) before 20 November 2026 include the pack plus one month of GTA+.
  • Physical pre-orders contain one code that covers the game and the Vintage Vice City Pack. The physical copies don’t include a GTA+ month in that list.
  • The pack is not an Ultimate Edition exclusive. Standard buyers got it too.

On PlayStation, the GTA+ offer terms say eligible digital buyers can claim the free GTA+ month by 31 March 2027. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade purchase on its own is not eligible for that GTA+ offer. GTA+ is the existing membership for GTA Online and the GTA+ Games Library. It isn’t a GTA 6 multiplayer pass.

Missed the deadline? You can still buy the pack

Both console stores list the Grand Theft Auto VI: Vintage Vice City Pack as a stand-alone add-on with a release date of 20 November 2026:

  • Xbox Store (US): $4.99, Xbox Series X|S.
  • PlayStation Store (UK): £4.49, PS5 / PS5 Pro Enhanced.

Both listings say GTA VI is required and sold separately. They describe the same three in-game items. Neither stand-alone listing mentions a GTA+ month, so don’t expect one with a paid add-on.

Unconfirmed: we couldn’t confirm the US PlayStation Store price today, and Rockstar hasn’t published a Newswire post about the stand-alone release. Prices can change by region and over time, so check the listing on your own store before you buy.

Is it worth $4.99 / £4.49?

That depends on you. You get a classic car, a second garage with a weapon locker and a place to fence stolen goods, two outfits, a hairstyle set, and a weapon pattern. If you want the retro Vice City look or an extra garage, it’s cheap. If you only care about the story, skip it. Nothing in the pack is described as necessary for the story. If you’re thinking about the bigger Ultimate Edition Upgrade instead, see the edition table on Daily Tips.

How to claim the Vintage Vice City Pack in-game

Rockstar hasn’t published official claim steps. The steps below come from PC Gamer’s launch guide (18 November 2026). Dexerto’s GTA 6 wiki describes the same Chapter 3 Ocean View Hotel pickup.

Step 1: Unlock the in-game store (Chapter 1)

The pack doesn’t show up until the in-menu store unlocks during mission 11, “Coming Home,” in Chapter 1. Then:

  1. Open the main menu.
  2. Select the Store option.
  3. Scroll down past GTA+ to find the Vintage Vice City Pack.

The store entries explain how to get each item. They also say you can’t claim anything until Chapter 3.

Step 2: Collect the ’55 Vapid Stanier (Chapter 3)

When you reach Chapter 3, a new task appears in the Exploration section of the map index. It sends you to the Ocean View Hotel to collect the ’55 Vapid Stanier & Garage. Collecting the car also unlocks the rest of the pack. Vice City and Ocean Beach are covered on the map page.

Note: official copy names the garage Shore Court, near Ocean Beach. Some fan sites report a different garage name in the in-game task text. We haven’t verified that, so check what your task card says.

Step 3: Outfits, hairstyles, and the weapon pattern

After you collect the car, PC Gamer reports:

  • Outfits are in your wardrobe.
  • Hairstyles are in the Special section of any salon.
  • The weapon pattern is in the Cosmetics / Wraps section of the modify menu at any Ammu-Nation.

Pack missing? Checklist before you contact support

  1. Check your story progress. Nothing can be claimed before Chapter 3, and the store entry won’t appear before “Coming Home.” This is the most likely cause.
  2. Check the purchase date and account. The free pack required a purchase before 20 November 2026 on the same account that plays the game. For boxed copies, check that your retailer’s offer included the pack.
  3. Check the region. Rockstar Support says code redemption follows the region of your platform account.
  4. PS5 owners: if you own the content but can’t access it, try Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licenses on the account that bought it. This is a standard PlayStation step, not a game-specific fix.
  5. Still missing? Contact Rockstar Support or your platform’s support with your order confirmation. Don’t use “unlock tools,” modded saves, or code generators. They break Rockstar’s Terms of Service and are a common scam.

Practical takeaways

  • If you bought GTA 6 before 20 November 2026, you already own the pack. Play to Chapter 3 to collect it.
  • If you bought on or after 20 November 2026, the pack is a paid add-on: $4.99 (US Xbox) or £4.49 (UK PlayStation) at the time of writing.
  • The pack is cosmetic plus one garage. It doesn’t give money, skip story content, or unlock anything early.
  • Pair the Shore Court garage with honest Vice City money loops from the Vice City money guide. Earlier chapters are in the early-game and Port Gellhorn guides, and the money hub compares all methods.

FAQ

Is the Vintage Vice City Pack still free?

No. The free offer covered purchases before 20 November 2026. Since 20 November 2026 the pack has been sold separately ($4.99 on the US Xbox Store, £4.49 on the UK PlayStation Store).

Do Standard Edition owners get the Vintage Vice City Pack?

Yes, if they bought in the window. Rockstar lists it as a bonus for digital purchases of either edition before 20 November 2026 and for physical pre-orders.

When can I use the ’55 Vapid Stanier?

PC Gamer reports that the items can only be claimed from Chapter 3. A map-index Exploration task then sends you to the Ocean View Hotel.

Does buying the pack separately include GTA+?

Neither stand-alone store listing mentions GTA+. The free GTA+ month was part of the digital pre-order and launch-window offer.

Does the pack give money or gameplay advantages?

No. It’s a car, a garage with a weapon locker and a place to fence stolen goods, outfits, hairstyles, and a weapon pattern.
