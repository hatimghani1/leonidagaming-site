GTA 6 Jewelry Store Locations: All 20 Stores, Payouts and Safe Codes

GTA 6 has 20 jewelry stores you can rob: 2 in Mariana County, 15 in Vice-Dale County and 3 in Leonard County. They open in Chapter 3 once you finish Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs, the best ones show a $14,000–$25,000 take on the map, and you sell the haul to Yasmani in Little Cuba. Below: every store with its area and take, a three-district route, the only fixed safe codes, and a quiet method that keeps the police off your back.

Updated 26 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: mission names only, no story events.

Status on 26 November 2026: the store list, counties and take values come from PowerPyx (all 35 Smash ’n’ Grabs, numbered) and match games.gg’s jewelry checklist. Neighbourhoods are IGN’s wording as collected by ALLGTA World; Leonard County stores have no confirmed neighbourhood yet. Take values are the range shown on each store’s map card, not a guaranteed fence payment. Where guides disagree (the Decor District top end, two store spellings) we show both.

This is a spoke of our GTA 6 money guide. Jewelry stores are the big Chapter 3 earner in the Vice City money guide, and they are the step between snap-gun store jobs and the endgame bank vaults.

Quick answer: GTA 6 jewelry stores

  1. Unlock them by finishing Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs in Chapter 3 (it starts after Boobie Time). You also get the Snap Gun during it. (PowerPyx)
  2. Find them under Map > Robberies (money bag icon) > Smash ’n’ Grabs. Stores get a green icon once you uncover their area. (PowerPyx, games.gg)
  3. Go for the $14K–$25K tier first: the Decor District trio (Blanc & Giroux, Roval, Covgari), the Ocean Beach trio (Akoya, Rococo Rich, Legendary Custom) and Icegem Downtown.
  4. Rob quietly: back door with the Snap Gun, disarm the panel, destroy the footage, open the safe, cases last.
  5. Always loot the back-room safe, or the store will not count as complete.
  6. Sell to Yasmani in Little Cuba for the best rate, then bank the cash at an ATM.

How to unlock jewelry store robberies

Jewelry stores are one half of GTA 6’s Smash ’n’ Grab robberies. The other half, pawn shops, opens earlier. Here is the unlock path, per PowerPyx and games.gg:

  1. Reach Chapter 3 and finish the main mission Boobie Time. Yasmani, the Little Cuba fence, then calls about a few store jobs.
  2. Play Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs. You rob Casa de Empeño, Richardson Jewelry and Donovan Banks & Co. for one item each (a buffalo skull chain, a diamond ring and an amethyst necklace), in any order. The three items are worth about $20,000 together (PowerPyx).
  3. Deliver the first item straight away. PowerPyx and GTABase say Yasmani hands you the Snap Gun after the first delivery, which makes the other two stores far easier. PC Gamer ties the Snap Gun to the earlier Dubious Provenance job, so you may already have it.
  4. Sell all three items to Yasmani. The mission ends, and the other jewelry stores open as Smash ’n’ Grabs.

Opening pawn shops in Chapter 2 does not unlock jewelry stores early (games.gg). Make a manual save before you start, because GTA 6 has no mission replay.

Best route: three districts, ten stores

Ten of the 15 Vice-Dale stores sit in three tight clusters, which makes a quick money run. Bank your cash and change clothes between clusters.

  1. Decor District (3 stores, top tier): Blanc & Giroux, Roval Jewelry and Covgari. Highest top end on the map. This is also the trio games.gg uses for the Retail Therapy challenge (see tips).
  2. Ocean Beach and Washington Beach (4 stores): Akoya Jewelers, Rococo Rich and Legendary Custom Jewelry (all top tier), then Marshal’s. The Casa de Empeño pawn shop is in Ocean Beach too.
  3. Downtown (4 stores, mixed): Icegem (top tier), All That Glitters and Peacock (middle), and Richardson. Richardson is the weakest, but you already know its layout from Yasmani’s job.
  4. Then the rest when you pass by: Donovan Banks & Co. (Peacock Bay), Plush (Tequesta), the two Balsero stores (handy when visiting Randy’s showroom for Randy’s Wish List), Victoria Lee in Capri, The Siren’s Chest in Key Lento and the three Leonard County stores.

How to rob a jewelry store without the alarm

Store layouts differ, but the same order works almost everywhere (PowerPyx, games.gg, PC Gamer):

  1. Prepare. Wear a mask and carry a spare outfit in your car. Police describe your face, clothes and vehicle. Bring body armour if you plan a loud job and a sticky bomb in case you cannot get the code.
  2. Enter through the back. At a back door, pick Use Snap Gun instead of Break Open. The lock opens quietly, but the alarm is still live (PC Gamer).
  3. Disarm the security panel and destroy the CCTV footage at the box under the security screen. If you leave before officers arrive, they have no description of you (PowerPyx).
  4. Get the safe code. Threaten an employee and ask for the code (not every employee knows it), look for a written note in the back office, or search a downed guard. No code? Use a sticky bomb, which is loud (games.gg).
  5. Loot the safe first, then the display cases. Breaking glass alerts everyone, so do it last if you want the quiet part done first. Smashing a case does not collect the jewelry: use the loot prompt on each case.
  6. Leave and lose the heat. Change outfit or car and drive out of the search area. Our wanted level guide explains stars, heat icons and the fastest way to lose the cops.

Threatening staff instead of shooting them is also kinder to your Criminal Profile. Rockstar’s Rob Nelson told Eurogamer (reported by IGN on 26 November) that the studio wanted to give players ways to deal with the world beyond pure violence, and the threat of a weapon is often enough here.

Pawn shops and 100% completion

  • 35 Smash ’n’ Grabs in total: 20 jewelry stores and 15 pawn shops (PowerPyx). Pawn shops unlock after Coffee Break in Chapter 2 and show a $1,000–$12,000 take.
  • For 100%: fully complete 34 of the 35 Smash ’n’ Grabs (it is part of the A Long Road trophy/achievement, PowerPyx) and fence $100,000 in jewelry. games.gg says those are tracked separately, so selling matters as much as robbing.
  • Check the icon: bright green = done, dark green = something left, usually the safe (PowerPyx).
  • Tick stores off as you go on our GTA 6 100% checklist.

Tips, Retail Therapy and mistakes to avoid

  • Retail Therapy: games.gg describes a challenge in which you rob three shops with your partner (it uses Covgari, Blanc & Giroux and Roval) without letting your wanted level drop between them. Mark all three first, bring armour, and don’t try it quietly. Reported by one guide so far; we will confirm the exact requirement.
  • Partner or solo? From Chapter 2, Jason and Lucia can rob together, and games.gg says this builds their relationship status. Go alone if you don’t want that.
  • Don’t stop at the cases. The store will not tick off and the safe often holds the best loot.
  • Don’t reuse codes. Free-roam codes are random. Only 6815, 0104 and 1680 are fixed.
  • Carry less, bank more. Fence and deposit after each cluster in the route.
  • Next step: once you have Credit Check and the Dye-Pack Jammer, banks pay more. See the bank vaults guide.

FAQ: GTA 6 jewelry stores

How many jewelry stores are in GTA 6?

20. PowerPyx and games.gg both list 2 in Mariana County, 15 in Vice-Dale County and 3 in Leonard County. With the 15 pawn shops there are 35 Smash ’n’ Grabs in total, and you need 34 of them fully robbed for 100% completion.

How do you unlock jewelry store robberies in GTA 6?

Finish the Chapter 3 main mission Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs, which starts after Boobie Time. The other stores then appear on the map as green Smash ’n’ Grab icons as you uncover each area. Pawn shops open earlier, after Coffee Break in Chapter 2 (PowerPyx, games.gg).

Which GTA 6 jewelry store pays the most?

The top tier shows a $14,000–$25,000 take on its map card: Donovan Banks & Co., Icegem, Blanc & Giroux, Roval Jewelry, Covgari, Akoya, Rococo Rich, Legendary Custom Jewelry, Skala and Kowalski (PowerPyx). games.gg reads the three Decor District stores (Blanc & Giroux, Roval/Royal, Covgari) as up to $35,000. The Siren’s Chest, The Jewelry Place and Crown Jewels are the lowest at $1,000–$10,000.

What is the jewelry store safe code in GTA 6?

Only the story stores have fixed codes: Richardson Jewelry & Diamonds 6815, Donovan Banks & Co. 0104 and the Casa de Empeño pawn shop 1680, all from Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs (PowerPyx, GTABase, games.gg). In free-roam robberies the code is random: threaten an employee, read a note in the back office or check a downed guard, or use a sticky bomb.

Where do you sell stolen jewelry in GTA 6?

To Yasmani at his shop in Little Cuba, Vice City. He pays the best rate on the spot. Dunk in Port Gellhorn (after Slam Dunk) pays less, and Brian and Donnie in the Keys pay very little. Leaving the haul in a garage for pickup is easier, but Yasmani takes a bigger cut (games.gg).

Why does a jewelry store not count as complete?

You probably skipped the back room. A Smash ’n’ Grab only ticks off once you also loot the back-room safe, not just the display cases. Bright green on the map means complete and dark green means something is left (PowerPyx, games.gg).

How do you rob a jewelry store without the alarm in GTA 6?

Wear a mask, go in through the back door with the Snap Gun, disarm the security panel and destroy the CCTV footage, open the safe, and smash the display cases last because breaking glass is loud. If you leave before police arrive they have no description of you (PowerPyx).
