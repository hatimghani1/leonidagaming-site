GTA 6 PC release date: is GTA 6 on PC?

Short answer: no. GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has not announced a PC version. Here is what is official, what is only reported, what cloud and Remote Play can actually do, and how to avoid the fake “PC downloads” going around.

Updated 20 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). No spoilers.

Status on 20 November 2026: no official PC version, PC date, PC pre-order, Steam or Epic listing, or PC system requirements. The only PC window in circulation is a report (GamesBeat, Q1 2027), not an announcement. We’ll update this page the moment Rockstar says anything.

Quick answer

  • Is GTA 6 on PC? No. It launched on 19 November 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. (Official: Rockstar, Take-Two.)
  • PC release date? Not announced. GamesBeat reports a Q1 2027 target from four anonymous sources. (Report, unconfirmed.)
  • Cloud on PC? No. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams GTA VI to Xbox consoles only at launch. (Official: Xbox Wire, Xbox.com.)
  • PC download sites? All fake. Several contain password- and crypto-stealing malware. (Cynet, NordVPN, McAfee, Malwarebytes.)

What Rockstar and Take-Two have actually said

Rockstar’s official GTA VI pages and Take-Two’s 9 November 2026 earnings materials list the same two launch platforms: PlayStation 5 (including PS5 Pro Enhanced) and Xbox Series X|S. Neither mentions a PC edition, and there is no PC pre-order on any store.

Take-Two hasn’t ruled PC out, either. At its annual shareholder meeting in September 2026, CEO Strauss Zelnick said the company’s strategy is “over time” to launch on platforms with a meaningful audience, and that “the PC platform is becoming more and more important to our company” (as quoted by Meristation). PC Gamer notes that Take-Two has consistently declined to reveal its PC plans, including when analysts asked on the investor call just before launch.

In plain terms: a PC version is widely expected, but it is not announced, and nobody outside Rockstar can give you a real date today.

The Q1 2027 PC report (unconfirmed)

On 13 October 2026, GamesBeat reported that Rockstar plans to release GTA VI on PC in the first quarter of 2027, citing four industry sources who asked not to be named. GamesBeat said Rockstar did not respond to a request for comment. The Verge and other outlets repeated the report, and PC Gamer said it was treating it with caution.

Treat it as a report, not a date. Q1 2027 is a three-month window (January–March), the sources are anonymous, and Rockstar has delayed GTA VI twice before. Don’t pre-order anything that claims to be a PC version.

Xbox Cloud Gaming: works on Xbox, not on PC

According to Xbox Wire (10 November 2026), GTA VI is playable from day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. That is how Xbox One owners can play a game that doesn’t run natively on their console.

  • No PC, phone, TV or browser streaming at launch. The Verge and Dexerto both note the cloud version is restricted to Xbox consoles, and Polygon reports Xbox chief strategy officer Matthew Ball said it won’t stream on PC at launch.
  • You must own the game. Xbox.com says GTA VI is not in Game Pass. Owners use Game Pass cloud hours, buy cloud playtime, or play with ads.

Remote Play: possible, but you still need a console

Rockstar says GTA VI “will also fully support Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox”, as quoted by Dexerto. Remote Play is not cloud gaming: it streams the game from your own PS5 or Xbox Series X|S to another device over your network or the internet.

Sony and Microsoft both offer Remote Play apps for Windows, so in principle a console owner could stream GTA VI to a PC that way. Rockstar has not specifically confirmed PC as a Remote Play device for GTA VI, and we haven’t tested it, so treat this as a maybe. It also doesn’t help anyone who doesn’t own a current-gen console.

Fake “GTA 6 PC download” sites: avoid all of them

Because there’s no PC version, every site offering a GTA 6 PC installer, crack, keygen, APK or “early access” build is fake. Security firms have documented several:

  • Cynet found a “GTA 6 EXE” zip spread via a Google Colab notebook. It contained the WARDEN Stealer, which steals browser passwords, cookies, Steam and Roblox logins, and crypto wallet data. Its README told victims to disable antivirus warnings.
  • NordVPN, via TechRadar, found a $200 dark-web scam kit with a fake “GTA6 Keygen.exe” cookie stealer, a crypto wallet drainer and a hidden crypto miner.
  • Malwarebytes and McAfee warn about fake free-game offers, “discount keys”, survey walls and fake sign-in pages.

If you already ran a fake file

  1. Disconnect the PC from the internet.
  2. Run a full scan with a trusted antivirus.
  3. From a different, clean device, change your passwords (email, Steam, Rockstar, PlayStation, Xbox) and turn on two-factor authentication.
  4. Check bank accounts and crypto wallets for activity you don’t recognise.

These steps follow McAfee’s published advice. Only buy GTA VI from the PlayStation Store, the Microsoft/Xbox Store, the Rockstar Store or a retailer you trust.

  • PC-only players: wait for an official Rockstar announcement. Nothing else counts.

GTA 6 PC FAQ

Is GTA 6 out on PC?

No. As of 20 November 2026, Grand Theft Auto VI is only out on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced a PC version, a PC release date, PC pre-orders, or PC system requirements.

When is the GTA 6 PC release date?

There is no official date. GamesBeat reported in October 2026, citing four anonymous industry sources, that Rockstar plans a PC release in the first quarter of 2027 (January to March). Rockstar has not confirmed that report.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Not at launch. Microsoft says GTA VI streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S consoles only. Xbox’s Matthew Ball said it will not stream to PC at launch. You must own the game and have cloud playtime; it is not in Game Pass.

Can I use Remote Play to play GTA 6 on a PC?

Rockstar says GTA VI fully supports Remote Play on PlayStation and Xbox. Remote Play streams from a console you own, so you still need a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S with the game. Rockstar has not specifically confirmed PC as a Remote Play device.

Is there a safe GTA 6 PC download?

No. There is no official PC version, so every “GTA 6 PC download”, crack, keygen, or installer is fake. Security researchers at Cynet, NordVPN, McAfee and Malwarebytes have documented fake GTA 6 files that steal passwords, Steam logins and crypto wallets.
