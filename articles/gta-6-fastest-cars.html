Fastest Car in GTA 6: Top 7 Cars Ranked by Speed and Where to Find Them

The fastest car found in GTA 6 so far is the Truffade Thrax, with a stock Speed rating of 8.7 out of 10. Next come the Progen Emerus (8.3) and the Pegassi Vitello (8.2). The Vitello is the fastest one you can just go and steal. Below is the full top 7, where launch guides found each car, how to get the Thrax from Randy, and how to make any car faster with legit upgrades.

Updated 21 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). Light spoilers: Randy’s Wish List (Chapter 3) and one story mission are named in the “where to find” section.

Status on 21 November 2026: Rockstar has not published vehicle stats. The ratings below are the Speed stat the game shows when you scan a car with the WAiNK app. They come from GameSpot’s stock-speed ranking (20 November) and match Beebom’s list (21 November). No major outlet has timed real top speeds in mph or km/h yet, so treat this as a reported ranking. It can change as players find more cars.

Quick answer: the fastest car in GTA 6

  • Fastest overall (stock): Truffade Thrax, Speed 8.7. GameSpot says this is higher than most cars reach even when fully upgraded. (Reported: GameSpot, Beebom.)
  • Fastest you can steal in free roam: Pegassi Vitello, Speed 8.2 with 8.4 acceleration. (Reported: GameSpot, Beebom, Push Square location.)
  • Fastest muscle car: Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire, Speed 7.8. (Reported: Beebom, Push Square location.)
  • Fastest sports car (not a supercar): Pfister Comet S2, Speed 7.7. (Reported: GameSpot.)
  • Fully upgraded: engine, transmission and other performance parts can push some cars to 9.0 or higher, the Progen Emerus included. (Reported: GameSpot.)
  • Real top speed in mph: not verified yet. Ignore exact mph lists for now.

Where to find each fast car

Most of these cars are on Randy’s Wish List, his list of high-end cars to steal. It opens in Chapter 3 (see the Randy section of our Vice City money guide). Push Square counts 22 cars on the list. Track the ones you still need under Touch Pad > Robberies > Randy’s Wish List, and talk to Randy at Leonida Auto World for hints. Locations below are launch-week reports. Many cars also spawn in more than one place.

Pegassi Vitello (8.2): the best fast car you can steal

  1. Drive to the posh villa on Flamingo Pkwy, Waning Sands, east of Belville in Leonard County.
  2. Hop the fence. Push Square suggests planting explosives on the gate first so you have a quick exit.
  3. The Vitello is in the garage closest to the house, on the right as you face the entrance. Use the Clone Key, then drive out and deliver it to Randy or keep it (see keeping a supercar).

Beebom also reports Vitellos at a pale green mansion in northwest Capri and at a Starfish Island mansion garage.

Piccia Furia (7.8)

  1. Go to the mansion on Palladia Ln, Peacock Estates (Peacock Bay, Vice City).
  2. Skip the front gate. Take the path on the left and hop the hedge, which puts you between the water and a garage.
  3. Go through the garage door, Clone Key the Furia, open the shutters and leave through the gates.

Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire (7.8): fastest muscle car

It is parked at the back of an M7 junkyard east of Boone Speedway, off Galerina St in Hamlet. Open the entrance gates first, then sneak down the right-hand side of the yard, staying low, and Clone Key it. Push Square was able to do this without being spotted.

Pfister Comet S2 (7.7)

GameSpot only says you can get it “later on” in the story. Beebom reports you can choose it for Jason during the story mission Company Cars, and it then joins your garage. We will confirm this once a second guide lists it.

Pegassi Tempesta (7.6)

Push Square found it during a repossession random event on NE 3rd St, Vice City, around 1 PM in-game. The owner is fighting the tow-truck driver. Threaten the owner, then release the Tempesta from the tow truck. Beebom also reports one outside a cabin on the west side of Lake Tallaloko.

Progen Emerus (8.3)

No outlet we checked has a confirmed fixed street location. Some guides tie it to finishing every vehicle challenge after the story. That route uses the game’s cheat system, which turns off trophies and saving, so this site does not cover it. We will add a normal location when one is confirmed.

How to get the Truffade Thrax (Randy’s last car)

Spoiler note: this is the final Wish List job. Cultured Vultures and GTAVIMaps describe the same route:

  1. Finish every other car on Randy’s Wish List. The Thrax does not appear until the rest are delivered. Randy then calls you about it. (PowerPyx counts 18 Wish List cars, usually $30,000+ each; see our Randy guide.)
  2. Take the ferry from Waxman Island to Dalton Island. During this job, the ferry is the way across.
  3. Head to the large car park behind the Dalton Island Club, deal with the security guard and take the Thrax.
  4. Drive around the golf-cart roadblocks that security sets up, and get back on the ferry before it leaves.
  5. Back on the mainland, police will chase you. You cannot respray this car, so keep your speed up and avoid narrow stretches of highway.
  6. Park it inside Bayside Drive Garage. Cultured Vultures reports Randy pays about $18,000, which is low for the fastest car in the game. You are doing this for the car and the list, not the cash.
Tip: scan every nice car you see with WAiNK as you play Chapter 3. It shows which buyer wants the car, so you tick off Wish List cars without trying to.

How the Speed rating works

  • Hold Up on the D-pad to open WAiNK, aim at a car and press R1/RB to scan it. The scan shows sell value, interested buyers, condition and security (GamesRadar+).
  • Press R3 to switch between tabs. Game Rant (via GTA EXP) reports the performance tab only fills in once you get in the car and start the engine.
  • Speed, acceleration and handling are shown out of 10. They are not mph or km/h. A higher Speed should mean a higher top speed, but acceleration and handling decide most real races and police chases.
  • The ratings in our table are stock. A tuned car can beat a stock car ranked above it.

How to make any car faster (legit upgrades)

  1. Pick a car you will keep. Register it or use a story or edition car, so the money you spend on parts stays in your garage.
  2. Visit a mod shop. Mod shops sell performance parts as well as cosmetics and repairs. PowerPyx reports each shop specializes in one type of vehicle, so take a supercar to a shop that works on supercars.
  3. Buy engine and transmission first. GameSpot says engine, transmission and other performance parts can lift a car’s Speed rating to 9.0 or higher.
  4. Check the numbers again. Re-scan with WAiNK or open the car’s stats to see the new rating before you spend more.

No cheats, trainers or glitches needed. Everything here uses normal gameplay. Pay ’n’ Spray shops are for repairs, resprays and selling, not performance parts (launch guides).

Keeping a supercar: trackers and registration

  • Trackers: cars have no tracker, a Standard Tracker or an Advanced Tracker. Push Square notes Wish List cars like the Infernus and Mamba GT have trackers, so drive fast so police cannot trace you.
  • Selling vs keeping: at a buyer who accepts the car you can sell it or register it to keep it. Registering costs money and needs a free vehicle slot, and high-end cars cost far more to register than everyday ones, so check the fee in the WAiNK scan first.
  • Freeing a slot: at any Pay ’n’ Spray, press R1/RB for the Stored Vehicles tab and sell cars you no longer want. The money goes into your BuckMe account (GamesRadar+).
  • Edition cars: the Ultimate Edition’s ’95 Grotti Cheetah is yours to keep. See Standard vs Ultimate Edition for when it unlocks.

Fastest cars in GTA 6 FAQ

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

The Truffade Thrax, with a stock Speed rating of 8.7 out of 10, according to GameSpot’s ranking (20 November 2026) and Beebom’s list. The Progen Emerus (8.3) and Pegassi Vitello (8.2) come next. Rockstar has not published official stats, and nobody has timed real top speeds in mph yet.

What is the fastest car you can steal in GTA 6?

The Pegassi Vitello (Speed 8.2). Push Square found one in the villa garage on Flamingo Pkwy, Waning Sands, east of Belville. Use the Clone Key to take it. It is also on Randy’s Wish List.

How do you get the Truffade Thrax in GTA 6?

Launch guides report it is the final car on Randy’s Wish List. After you deliver every other car, Randy calls you. Take the ferry from Waxman Island to Dalton Island, steal the Thrax from the car park behind the Dalton Island Club, get back on the ferry, outrun the police and park it inside Bayside Drive Garage.

What is the fastest muscle car in GTA 6?

The Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire, with a stock Speed of 7.8 (Beebom). Push Square found it at the back of an M7 junkyard east of Boone Speedway, off Galerina St in Hamlet.

Can you upgrade cars to go faster in GTA 6?

Yes. Mod shops sell engine, transmission and other performance parts. GameSpot says upgrades can lift a car’s Speed rating to 9.0 or higher. Pay ’n’ Spray shops only repair, respray and buy cars.

What is the top speed of cars in GTA 6 in mph?

No verified figure yet. The game shows Speed ratings out of 10, not mph, and no major outlet has published timed top-speed tests. Treat lists with exact mph numbers as estimates.
