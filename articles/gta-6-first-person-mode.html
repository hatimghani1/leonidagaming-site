GTA 6 First Person Mode: Is There One? Every Official Way to See Through Jason and Lucia’s Eyes

GTA 6 does not have a full first-person mode. There is no setting that keeps the camera behind Jason’s or Lucia’s eyes while you walk, drive and shoot, and Rockstar has not announced one. What the game does have are four official first-person views: Inspect Surroundings (hold L2/LT and press R3/RS while standing still), the phone (which can show the road from the driver’s seat), weapon scopes and sights, and binoculars. Below: exact buttons for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, the camera settings that get you closer to the action, what Rockstar said about first person, and why we skip the viral glitch.

Updated 27 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). Console version (PS5 / Xbox Series X|S) on title update 01.002.002. No story spoilers.

Status on 27 November 2026: Rockstar has not published a camera guide for GTA VI, so the controls below come from guide writers who played the release version (GTABase, Engadget, IGN, GameSpot, PC Gamer), with each control confirmed by at least two outlets where possible. The pre-launch statement on first person comes from Rockstar North’s Rob Nelson, as reported by Flow Games and VICE. The only title update so far, 01.002.002 (23 November), lists seven bug fixes and no camera changes.

Quick answer: does GTA 6 have first person?

  • Full first-person mode: no, not at launch. Rob Nelson said the team put its effort into third-person animation. (Flow Games via VICE, August 2026; confirmed at launch by GTABase, Engadget, IGN.)
  • Inspect Surroundings: stand still, no weapon out, hold L2 + press R3 (PS5) or LT + click RS (Xbox). Look and zoom, but you can’t walk. (GTABase, Engadget, IGN, GameSpot.)
  • Phone view: Settings → Camera → Phone Display: First Person, then press Up on the D-pad. Works while driving. (GTABase, Engadget.)
  • Scopes and sights: aim with L2/LT, then press R3/RS to look down the sight. (GTABase; first-person aiming also reported before launch by Flow Games.)
  • Binoculars: in Jason’s inventory from the start; you can move while looking through them. (GTABase.)
  • Future first-person mode: not announced. Treat any date you see on social media as rumour.

Inspect Surroundings: how to go first person on foot

This is the closest thing GTA 6 has to a first-person button, and it works from the very first hours of the game.

  1. Stop moving. Stand still on foot as Jason or Lucia. IGN notes you need your weapon unequipped; with a gun out the same buttons aim instead.
  2. Hold the left trigger. L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox Series X|S. GameSpot points out this first highlights whatever you’re looking at.
  3. Click the right stick. R3 on PS5 or RS on Xbox. The camera moves into your character’s eyes.
  4. Look around and zoom. Move the right stick to look, and press R2 or RT to zoom in (GTABase).
  5. Let go to return to third person. You can’t walk while inspecting, so release the trigger to move again.

Tip: it’s the cleanest first-person view for photos because nothing covers the screen. It’s also a quick way to read street signs, shop names and posters while you hunt for things like Kenton treasure dive sites or safe codes written on notes.

How to drive in first person in GTA 6 (phone view)

There’s no in-car first-person camera, but the phone gets you close. With the right setting, raising the phone puts the camera in the driver’s seat with the dashboard and road ahead of you (Engadget, GTABase).

  1. Pause the game and open Settings.
  2. Go to the Camera tab.
  3. Scroll to Phone Display and set it to First Person.
  4. Back in the game, press Up on the D-pad to raise the phone. It works on foot and behind the wheel.
  5. Put the phone away to return to the normal camera.
Heads-up: the phone covers part of the screen, so this view is worse for fast driving than the normal chase camera (GTABase). Avoid it in police chases; see our wanted level guide for how to break line of sight properly, and the fastest cars list if you just want a quicker escape.

First person with scopes, sights and binoculars

Weapon scopes and sights

Any weapon with a scope or sight can switch to a first-person aiming view: aim with L2 or LT, then press R3 or click the right stick to look down the sight (GTABase). This matches what Flow Games reported after its pre-launch visit to Rockstar North: no full first-person mode, but first-person aiming with certain weapons. You can add optics at any Ammu-Nation.

Remember that carrying a gun in public is suspicious and pointing one at people raises your heat. Your Criminal Profile also changes with how violent you are, so don’t scope random pedestrians just to test the camera.

Binoculars (the best moving first-person view)

  1. Hold L1 (PS5) or LB (Xbox) to open the weapon wheel.
  2. Select the binoculars, down and to the left by default. Jason has them from the start of the game.
  3. Hold L2 or LT to look through them. You can move while doing this.
  4. Use the D-pad to zoom in or out. They zoom much farther than Inspect Surroundings.

GTABase notes binoculars can annoy some NPCs, but nobody reacts the way they do to a pointed gun, so they’re the safest way to scout a jewelry store or a bank job before you commit.

GTA 6 camera settings and vehicle camera heights

If you want a closer, more immersive third-person view, these are the official options Engadget lists in the Settings → Camera tab:

  • On foot: camera height, plus horizontal and vertical follow speeds.
  • Land and water vehicles: similar height and follow-speed options, with separate follow speeds for driver and passenger.
  • Aircraft: follow speed, and whether the camera follows the plane during a barrel roll.
  • Phone Display: switch to First Person for the cockpit-style phone view above.

Change the camera while driving

In a car or boat, hold Down on the D-pad, pick one of three camera heights and confirm. The same menu has the cinematic camera, which makes chases look great but makes steering harder (Engadget). None of the three heights is a hood or cockpit view.

Where to try it: the long bridges of the Keys and Ocean Beach at night. Our vehicles hub lists the cars worth driving, and the radio stations guide covers the soundtrack for the ride.

What Rockstar said about first-person mode

Before launch, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told YouTuber Davy Jones (Flow Games) during a studio visit that GTA 6 would not have a full first-person mode at launch, because the team chose to focus on third-person animation, while some weapons would allow first-person aiming. VICE reported this on 28 August 2026. The release version matches that description.

Will it come later? Nobody outside Rockstar knows. The precedent: GTA 5 launched in 2013 without first person and gained a full first-person mode with its PS4 and Xbox One release in November 2014. But Nelson’s “not at launch” wording isn’t a promise, and Rockstar hasn’t announced a first-person update or a PC version (see is GTA 6 on PC?). The first title update, 01.002.002, added no camera options; we track every patch in GTA 6 patch notes and will update this page the day Rockstar adds a first-person toggle.

The GTA 6 first-person glitch: why we don’t cover it

You may have seen clips of players running and shooting in full first person. PC Gamer, IGN and GTABase report that this comes from a community-found camera glitch, not a setting. We don’t publish the steps, in line with our no-glitches policy, but here’s what the reports say about it so you can decide for yourself:

  • It isn’t finished. PC Gamer says the view looks rough, with shoulders clipping through the camera when you raise a long gun and odd-looking car break-ins.
  • Getting out means reloading. PC Gamer says the only way back to the normal camera is to save and reload, so keep a spare save slot if you ever try unintended behaviour.
  • It could disappear. GTABase warns it could be patched out in a future update.
  • No, it doesn’t prove a hidden DLC. PC Gamer calls the “Rockstar is holding it back to sell it” theory a conspiracy and thinks the feature is more likely unpolished. Neither is confirmed.

Best uses for first person in GTA 6

  1. Photo mode-style shots: Inspect Surroundings at sunset on Ocean Beach or in the Keys, with nothing on screen.
  2. Scouting robberies: binoculars from across the street to spot staff and exits before a money job.
  3. Reading clues: zoom in on notes, signs and posters for codes and collectibles on your 100% checklist.
  4. Wildlife and Bigfoot hunting: binoculars help you spot animals at a distance on the Skunk Ape missions.
  5. Long-range fights: a scoped rifle’s first-person sight for careful shots, instead of spraying at full heat.

FAQ: first person in GTA 6

Does GTA 6 have a first person mode?

Not a full one. GTA 6 launched on 19 November 2026 without a toggle that keeps the camera in first person while you walk, drive and fight. Rockstar North’s Rob Nelson said before launch that the team focused on third-person animation instead. You do get first-person views in four official situations: Inspect Surroundings, the phone, weapon scopes and sights, and binoculars.

How do you go first person in GTA 6?

On foot, stand still with no weapon out, then hold L2 and press R3 on PS5 (hold LT and click the right stick on Xbox Series X|S) to use Inspect Surroundings. Press R2 or RT to zoom. You can look around but not move. For a moving first-person view, use binoculars or aim down a scope.

Can you drive in first person in GTA 6?

Only with the phone out. Set Settings, Camera, Phone Display to First Person, then press Up on the D-pad while driving to see the road from the driver’s seat. The phone covers part of the screen. Holding Down on the D-pad in a vehicle only switches between three third-person camera heights and the cinematic camera.

Will Rockstar add first person mode to GTA 6?

Rockstar has not announced it. Rob Nelson only said there would be no full first-person mode at launch. GTA 5 got first person a year after release with the 2014 PS4 and Xbox One version, but that is precedent, not a promise. Title update 01.002.002 on 23 November 2026 added no camera options.

Is the GTA 6 first person glitch safe to use?

It is an unintended camera bug, not a setting. PC Gamer says it looks unfinished (shoulders clip through the camera) and you have to reload a save to get out of it, and GTABase warns it could be patched. We don’t publish glitch steps; the official views on this page work without any risk to your save.

Can you aim in first person in GTA 6?

Yes, with scoped or sighted weapons. Aim with L2 or LT, then press R3 or click the right stick to look down the sight. You can add optics at any Ammu-Nation. Binoculars, which Jason carries from the start, also give a first-person view you can move with.
