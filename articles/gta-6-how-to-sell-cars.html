How to Sell Cars in GTA 6: Where to Sell, Trackers, Prices and Daily Limits

You cannot sell cars in Chapter 1. From Chapter 2, Moses in Port Gellhorn buys older cars with no tracker. From early Chapter 3, any Pay ’n’ Spray buys stolen cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker (about three a day), and Randy in western Vice City pays the most for the high-end cars on his Wish List. Cars with an Advanced Tracker only sell at a Pay ’n’ Spray after a Randy job in Chapter 4. Below: who buys what, step-by-step selling, how to sell your own stored cars, the limits, and how to get the best price.

Updated 28 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Mission names only, no story spoilers.

Status on 24 November 2026: Rockstar has not published a car-selling guide. Prices and limits are launch-week player reports and are labelled; where sources disagree we show both. Normal gameplay only: no glitches, duplication tricks or exploits.

Quick answer: where to sell cars in GTA 6

  1. Chapter 1: nowhere. You can steal any car, but you cannot sell or register one yet (The Games Wiki, gta-6-map.org). Pay ’n’ Sprays unlock in the Chapter 1 mission Just a Scratch, but only for repairs and resprays at first (GamesRadar+).
  2. Chapter 2: Moses at his garage in Port Gellhorn, after the main mission Moses. Untracked, older, low-profile cars only (PowerPyx, PC Gamer).
  3. Early Chapter 3: after Welcome to Vice City, Moses puts in a word and every Pay ’n’ Spray buys stolen cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker (Game Rant, GamesRadar+, The Games Wiki).
  4. Mid Chapter 3: Randy (green RK icon, west Vice City) buys the high-end cars on his Wish List and gives you the Key Cloner (PowerPyx, Insider Gaming).
  5. Chapter 4: after Shoot a Promo and Randy’s ’64 Declasse Mamba GT job, Pay ’n’ Sprays also take Advanced Tracker cars (Game Rant, Destructoid, Insider Gaming).
Money goes to the bank, not your pocket. Car sales are paid straight into your BuckMe account, as long as it is not at its balance limit (GamesRadar+). That also means the cash is safe if you get Busted later. See the wanted level guide for what you lose.

There is no single “sell” button. Whether a buyer takes a car depends on how far you are in the story and on the car’s tracker: none, Standard or Advanced (GamesRadar+).

Vehicle fences you have unlocked also appear in the map index under Robberies, with what they will pay (GamesRadar+).

Step 1: scan the car with WAiNK before you steal it

  1. Hold Up on the D-pad to raise your phone’s WAiNK camera app.
  2. Aim at the car and press or hold R1 / RB to scan it.
  3. Read the card: model, security (locked, alarm, keyless) and tracker (none / Standard / Advanced). After the Moses mission it also shows the sell value and the cost to register (The Games Wiki, GamesRadar+).
  4. Pick the buyer from the tracker: none → Moses (or a Pay ’n’ Spray from Chapter 3), Standard → Pay ’n’ Spray from Chapter 3, Advanced → Randy (Wish List cars) or a Pay ’n’ Spray only after the Chapter 4 unlock.

Scanning first saves you a pointless drive: a tracked car that no one will buy just earns you police attention. Wish List cars trigger a pop-up when you scan or enter them (PowerPyx).

How to sell a car to Moses (Chapter 2)

  1. Unlock him by finishing the Chapter 2 main mission Moses (Take a Vehicle to Moses). It also gives you the slim jim for locked doors (PowerPyx, PC Gamer).
  2. Steal an older, untracked car away from his shop. Moses refuses cars taken from near his doorstep (PowerPyx via fan wikis, GTAXTREME). Use the slim jim instead of smashing a window (Beebom).
  3. Check he is buying. Hover over his MW icon on the map: it shows what he will pay, or that he is still handling a recently sold vehicle (gta-6-map.org).
  4. Lose any wanted level and drive carefully; damage lowers the price (GamesRadar+).
  5. Drive into the garage, talk to Moses (L2 / LT) and choose Sell. The money lands in BuckMe.

Moses also texts you specific cars (his 7 Vehicle Drop-Offs), and he often tells you exactly where the car he wants is parked (PC Gamer, Push Square). Finishing all seven earns his Emperor Sultan. Full list and locations: Moses Vehicle Drop-Offs.

Why won’t Moses buy my car? The usual reasons: it has a tracker (even Standard), it was stolen too close to his garage, it is a large or high-end model he does not deal in, or he is on cooldown after your last sale (The Games Wiki, Beebom, gta-6-map.org).

How to sell a stolen car at Pay ’n’ Spray (Chapter 3)

  1. Unlock stolen-car sales: shortly after Welcome to Vice City in early Chapter 3, Moses says he has contacts, and Pay ’n’ Sprays start buying and registering cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker (Game Rant, The Games Wiki). The on-screen tip reads: “Pay ’n’ Sprays now buy and register untracked vehicles and any with a standard tracker” (HowToVideoGame).
  2. Find the nearest shop: open the Index → Vehicles → Pay ’n’ Sprays. Shops you have discovered are listed from nearest to farthest, and picking one sets a GPS route (GamesRadar+). There are 19 shops across Leonida (PowerPyx, Mobalytics, GTAVibes): every one with nearby landmarks is in our GTA 6 Pay ’n’ Spray locations guide.
  3. Drive straight into the open bay, not just the forecourt, and open the shop menu.
  4. Choose Sell on the current vehicle. Payment goes to BuckMe (GamesRadar+).

Polygon’s quick loop: steal an untracked (or at least not Advanced) car parked close to a Pay ’n’ Spray, ideally from a parking lot rather than a moving driver, and sell it straight away. They highlight the Ocean Beach shop, where a valuable nearby car brought about $7,000. More Vice City earners: late-game money guide.

How to sell your own or stored cars

Pay ’n’ Sprays also buy back cars you own: registered vehicles and anything parked in your garages (GamesRadar+, PowerPyx).

  1. Drive any vehicle into a Pay ’n’ Spray lot.
  2. Press R1 / RB to switch to the Stored Vehicles tab.
  3. Sell one car, or several at once. You do not have to drive each one over.
  • Exotic car? GamesRadar+ notes a dedicated vehicle fence can pay more than a Pay ’n’ Spray.
  • GTA+ members get 10% more when selling owned vehicles, according to Rockstar’s GTA+ page as reported by Polygon. See GTA 6 Online and GTA+.
  • Edition bonus cars (Ultimate Edition or Vintage Vice City Pack) are better kept than sold. See Standard vs Ultimate Edition.
  • Garages full? Selling a stored car frees a slot for a new registration. Registering costs money each time, so do not sell a car you might want back (PowerPyx).

Randy and the Wish List (best prices)

Randy is the high-end car contact in western Vice City, marked by a green RK icon (PowerPyx). Launch guides describe the unlock path like this:

  1. Meet Randy in Chapter 3 through the main story (Game Rant names First Impressions). The Games Wiki reports he becomes a drop-off for Standard Tracker cars and his request list after Car Service.
  2. In the main mission Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS you steal a Granforma Zorrusso for him and get the Key Cloner phone plugin, which opens keyless high-end cars (PowerPyx, Insider Gaming).
  3. His Wish List opens: track it under Map → Robberies → Randy’s Wish List. He pins some cars to fixed spots; any listed car you find on the street also counts (PowerPyx).
  4. Deliver cars in good condition for more money; repairing before delivery earns a bonus (PowerPyx, Destructoid).
How many cars? Sources disagree: PowerPyx’s trophy guide lists 16, while Game Rant and Destructoid say 20. Good hunting grounds are hotel driveways around Vice Beach and Ocean Beach (PowerPyx). Finishing Randy’s final mission unlocks the Supply & Demand trophy, and the list is still available after the story (PowerPyx). Track it on our 100% checklist.

Several Wish List cars are among the quickest in the game: see the fastest cars in GTA 6.

How to sell cars with Advanced Trackers

An Advanced Tracker notifies the police when you steal the car and again whenever you drive too slowly: a red tracker icon sits above the minimap and its ring fills while you are slow (The Games Wiki, Insider Gaming). Before the unlock, only Randy takes these cars, and only the ones he asked for.

  1. Work through Randy’s Wish List (Destructoid and Insider Gaming say the next step comes after 10 deliveries).
  2. Finish the Chapter 4 main mission Shoot a Promo. A few in-game days later Randy calls and pins a ’64 Declasse Mamba GT for you to steal (Game Rant, Destructoid).
  3. Deliver the Mamba GT. About a day later (in game) Randy calls back and promotes you: every Pay ’n’ Spray now sells and registers Advanced Tracker cars (Game Rant, Destructoid, Insider Gaming).
  4. Confirm by hovering over a Pay ’n’ Spray on the map; the description now says it deals in tracked vehicles (Destructoid).
Registering Advanced cars: sources differ. HowToVideoGame reports that after Randy’s deal Pay ’n’ Spray buys Advanced Tracker cars but never registers them. Game Rant, Destructoid and Leonipedia say they register too, and gta6.house lists an Advanced Tracker Toros registered for $29,900. Before the deal, the game says “Pay ’n’ Sprays don’t currently service vehicles with trackers installed.” Check the menu on your own save before you count on registering one.

Driving tip: keep your speed up all the way to the shop so the tracker ring never fills, and pick a route with few junctions. You may arrive with some damage, which costs a little; getting caught costs the whole car. If it does go wrong, use our guide to losing the cops.

8 tips for the best price when selling cars

  1. Scan everything. The WAiNK sell value is the fastest way to tell a $1,000 car from a $7,000 one.
  2. Steal parked cars. A parked car with no witness is less likely to be reported than a carjacking. Police only respond when a witness or alarm reports a crime (see the wanted level guide).
  3. Steal near the buyer, but not too near Moses. Short drives mean less damage. Moses is the exception: he rejects cars from his doorstep.
  4. Drive like a taxi driver. Damage comes straight off the price. If you do bump it, compare the repair cost with the lost value before you sell.
  5. Use the three daily Pay ’n’ Spray sales on your best cars. Save cheap untracked cars for Moses.
  6. Mix it with other jobs. While a fence is on cooldown, do a hunt, a store robbery or a story mission. Ideas: early-game money and Vice City money.
  7. Bank and save. Sales already go to BuckMe; make a manual save after a good run (how to save in GTA 6).
  8. Don’t lose your own car to the cops. Registered cars get towed if you are Busted in them; get them back with our impound lot guide, and do jobs in stolen cars instead.

FAQ: selling cars in GTA 6

Where can you sell cars in GTA 6?

Moses at Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn buys untracked cars from Chapter 2. From early Chapter 3 any Pay ’n’ Spray buys stolen cars with no tracker or a Standard Tracker, and Randy in western Vice City buys the high-end cars on his Wish List. Advanced Tracker cars sell at Pay ’n’ Spray only after Randy’s Mamba GT job in Chapter 4 (GamesRadar+, Game Rant, PowerPyx).

How much does Pay ’n’ Spray pay for a car in GTA 6?

It depends on the model and condition. HowToVideoGame was offered $4,795 for a Standard Tracker Obey 8F Drafter at the La Perle Pay ’n’ Spray, against an $18,650 fee to register the same car. Polygon saw about $7,000 for a valuable car near Ocean Beach, and fan wikis put good Standard Tracker cars at roughly $2,500–$4,000. Scan the car with WAiNK first to see its sell value.

When can you start selling cars in GTA 6?

Not in Chapter 1. Selling opens in Chapter 2 after the main mission Moses, which also gives you the slim jim. Pay ’n’ Spray stolen-car sales open shortly after Welcome to Vice City in early Chapter 3 (PowerPyx, Game Rant, The Games Wiki).

Why won’t Moses buy my car in GTA 6?

Moses only buys older, low-end cars with no tracker. He refuses any tracked car, cars stolen right next to his garage, and he pauses buying for a short cooldown after each sale. Hover over his MW icon on the map to see whether he is buying (Beebom, The Games Wiki, gta-6-map.org).

How many cars can you sell per day in GTA 6?

Several launch guides report that a Pay ’n’ Spray takes three stolen cars per in-game day. GamesRadar+ confirms Pay ’n’ Sprays have daily limits and hit them sooner than Moses or Randy, who run on a budget that refills over time.

How do you sell cars with Advanced Trackers in GTA 6?

Deliver cars from Randy’s Wish List, finish the Chapter 4 mission Shoot a Promo, then steal the ’64 Declasse Mamba GT Randy pins for you. About an in-game day after delivery he calls to say every Pay ’n’ Spray now buys and registers Advanced Tracker cars. Keep your speed up on the way, because the tracker alerts police when you drive slowly (Game Rant, Destructoid, Insider Gaming).

How do you sell your own car in GTA 6?

Drive into any Pay ’n’ Spray lot, press R1 or RB to open the Stored Vehicles tab, and sell one or several registered or garaged cars at once. The money goes into your BuckMe account. GTA+ members get 10% more for owned vehicles (GamesRadar+, Polygon).

Where does the money from selling cars go in GTA 6?

Straight into your BuckMe bank account, as long as it is not at its balance limit, so it is not lost if you are later Busted or Wasted (GamesRadar+).
