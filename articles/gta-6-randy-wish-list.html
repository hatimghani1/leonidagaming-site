GTA 6 Randy’s Wish List: Every Car Location, Payouts and the Thrax

Randy’s Wish List is GTA 6’s high-end car theft side job. It opens in Chapter 3 after Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs and pays up to about $20,000 per car before the condition bonus. It ends with the Truffade Thrax, the fastest car in the game. Below is a fixed, repeatable spot for every core car, the payout for each, and the route that clears five cars from a single dealership.

Updated 25 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: mission names only, no story events.

Status on 25 November 2026: locations and payouts come from hands-on guides by PowerPyx (full walkthrough with base rewards, 25 November), Push Square (Randy’s lead missions, Mansion Thrax and Delivery Day) and Destructoid. Guides do not agree on the car count yet. Cars that only one outlet lists are labelled. Times of day such as “~6pm” are the times reporters found the cars, not guaranteed spawns.

Quick answer: how to finish Randy’s Wish List

  1. Unlock it in Chapter 3 with Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs. The Zorrusso counts as car #1 and you get the Key Cloner. (PowerPyx)
  2. Check what’s left under Map > Robberies (money bag icon) > Randy’s Wish List. (PowerPyx, Push Square)
  3. Hit the Bayside dealership west of Dynamix Fitness for the Windsor Drop, Paragon, Jubilee, Itali RSX and Dubsta. Then visit the Southside showrooms for the Toros, Growler and Banshee. (PowerPyx)
  4. Grab the rest from fixed driveways on Vespucci and Casanova Island, a Belville garage, a Capri hotel and Randy’s two first leads (Vitello and Infernus).
  5. Drive fast, then repair. Most list cars have Advanced Trackers, so keep your speed up. Fix damage at the gas station just north of Randy’s showroom before you deliver. (PowerPyx)
  6. Finish with the Mamba GT and the Thrax, then play Delivery Day for $25,000 and the Supply & Demand trophy. (Push Square)

How to unlock Randy’s Wish List

Randy runs Leonida Auto World, a showroom in Balsero, western Vice City (Push Square, Destructoid, PowerPyx). You meet him in the Chapter 3 main mission Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs. That mission has you steal a Granforma Zorrusso, gives you the Key Cloner and starts the Wish List. Either Jason or Lucia can take it on (PowerPyx).

  • Leads: talk to Randy and he gives you one car location at a time, like Moses did in Chapter 2. Lead cars only spawn after he gives you the lead (PowerPyx). Push Square marks these as purple missions on the map.
  • Any matching car counts. If you steal a listed model somewhere else, Randy still takes it and skips that lead (Push Square).
  • Two free leads: when the list opens, Randy marks the Pegassi Vitello and Pegassi Infernus straight away (PowerPyx).

Fastest route: 5 cars from one dealership

PowerPyx found fixed open-world spots that do not depend on Randy’s leads. Grouped by area, this route covers most of the list in one session:

  1. Bayside dealership (west of Dynamix Fitness, Vice City): Buckingham Windsor Drop, Ascot Paragon, Buckingham Jubilee and Grotti Itali RSX inside, plus two Benefactor Dubstas parked out front. Take one car, drive it out through the showroom glass, deliver it and come back. That’s five list cars and about $60,000 in base pay.
  2. Southside showrooms (three hexagonal buildings, north-east Southside): the Pegassi Toros and Bravado Banshee in the centre building and the Pfister Growler in and around the north one. If a car is missing, save and reload to refresh the showroom (PowerPyx). Push Square points to Sunrise Premium Autos on El Joven Blvd for the Banshee.
  3. Rialto Islands driveways: Piccia Furia (two houses north of the central road, Vespucci Island, with a Jubilee beside it), Pegassi Tempesta (four houses north, Vespucci Island) and Gallivanter Baller ST-D (two houses north, Casanova Island).
  4. Capri and Belville: Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire in the covered hotel parking south-west of Hair by Clair, Capri. Ocelot Jugular in a locked garage north of Peewees, Belville.
  5. Randy’s leads: Pegassi Vitello (Waning Sands villa) and Pegassi Infernus (Crosstown police car park). Details below.

Pegassi Vitello (Waning Sands)

Go to the villa on Flamingo Pkwy at the south-east tip of Waning Sands, east of Belville. The Vitello sits in the garage closest to the house, on the right as you face the entrance. Hop the fence, clone the key and drive out the front gate. Push Square suggests planting explosives on the gate first and setting them off once you are in the car.

Pegassi Infernus (Crosstown police car park)

It is on the 7th floor of the police car park on NW 9th St, Crosstown. PowerPyx uses the stairwell door on the south side with the code 5270 that Randy gives you. Push Square waits for a patrol car to open the gate and sneaks in behind it. Either way, avoid the officers, clone the key and drive out. Push Square uses the stunt jump on the top floor as a quick exit. The car has a tracker, so keep moving on the way to Randy.

Piccia Furia (Peacock Estates lead)

For Randy’s lead, go to the mansion on Palladia Ln, Peacock Estates. Skip the gate: take the path on the left, hop the hedge and enter the garage between the water and the house. Clone the key, open the shutters and leave through the gates (Push Square).

Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire (M7 junkyard lead)

Randy’s lead sends you to an M7 gang junkyard off Galerina St in Hamlet, east of Boone Speedway. Open the front gates first, then keep low along the right-hand side to reach the car at the back (Push Square). If you would rather avoid the gang, the Capri hotel spot in the table is easier.

Cars Randy adds later

  • ’64 Declasse Mamba GT, mission ¡Dale Mamba!: once you have delivered several cars, Randy sends you to a guarded mansion in Leaf Links, Vice Beach. The Key Cloner won’t work on this one. Use the Snap Gun on the main house doors behind the pool and take the key from a bowl on the table behind the sofa. The car is behind the building on the right and has a tracker (Push Square). Our vehicles hub explains why this job matters: after it, every Pay ’n’ Spray buys Advanced Tracker cars.
  • Invetero Coquette D10: Randy adds it by phone call later (Push Square, GTA Town). Community guides point to a red shipping container at the western docks on Waxman Island. Destructoid saw it in Capri driveways.
  • Ubermacht Cypher and Pfister Neon: listed by Destructoid. Look along Shore Drive (Cypher) and around Capri, where the Neon is common.

The Truffade Thrax: Mansion Thrax

The Truffade Thrax unlocks only after every other car is delivered. It is the fastest car in GTA 6 by stock Speed rating (8.7, GameSpot). Here is Push Square’s walkthrough:

  1. Randy calls. Take the ferry from Waxman Island to the Dalton Island Club. Reaching the objective starts Mansion Thrax.
  2. The Thrax is parked opposite Spa Spendido. Two guards patrol: one walks towards the helipad and one stands by the car. Take them out quietly when nobody is looking.
  3. Clone the key and drive calmly onto the ferry.
  4. Police are waiting when you get off: expect a 3-star wanted level. Lose them (our wanted level guide helps), then park the Thrax in the garage Randy names, using a route away from the cops.

Delivery Day and the final reward

Soon after the Thrax, Randy texts you about exporting the cars. This is Delivery Day, and it needs both Jason and Lucia at Leonida Auto World. Pick the Mamba GT or the Thrax and follow Randy to a ferry in Millport. When police stop you on the highway, protect Randy through the chase. You get $25,000 and the Supply & Demand trophy/achievement (Push Square).

Push Square adds that Randy then rewards you with Raul’s Pegassi Tempesta, registered to you, in a garage in Balsero, plus the Domestic Timbers garage. That is single-source for now; we will confirm it when a second outlet reports it. Every Randy delivery also counts toward completion on our GTA 6 100% checklist.

Delivery tips for full pay

  • Repair before delivering. Damage cuts the reward and a clean car earns a bonus. Use the gas station directly north of Randy’s showroom (PowerPyx). Destructoid says the bonus can be very large on some models.
  • Outrun the tracker. Advanced Tracker cars alert police if you drive slowly, so pick a fast route. See how to sell cars for how tracker tiers work.
  • Stay quiet at guarded spots. Guards at the Mamba GT mansion and on Dalton Island raise the alarm. Unneeded violence also hurts your Criminal Profile.
  • Save first. Make a manual save before the Infernus and Thrax jobs, so a failed escape costs you minutes, not the car.
  • Bank the cash. Wish List money pairs well with safe codes and the endgame bank vaults if you are saving toward Family Savings goals.

FAQ: GTA 6 Randy’s Wish List

How do you unlock Randy’s Wish List in GTA 6?

Finish the Chapter 3 main mission Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ Gs. Stealing the Granforma Zorrusso for Randy in that mission gives you the Key Cloner, counts as the first Wish List delivery and opens the list (PowerPyx). Track the rest under Map > Robberies (money bag icon) > Randy’s Wish List (PowerPyx, Push Square).

How many cars are on Randy’s Wish List in GTA 6?

Guides count differently. PowerPyx’s walkthrough lists 16 cars as the core list (Zorrusso to Baller ST-D). Push Square counts 20, Destructoid lists 21 including the ’64 Mamba GT and the Truffade Thrax, and community trackers reach 22 by adding cars Randy requests later, such as the Coquette D10. The Mamba GT and Thrax always come last through their own Randy missions.

Where is Randy in GTA 6?

At Leonida Auto World, his showroom in Balsero, western Vice City (Push Square, Destructoid, PowerPyx). Deliver every Wish List car there. Talk to him and he gives you a location lead for one car at a time.

How much does Randy pay for Wish List cars?

PowerPyx lists base rewards from $3,150 (Bravado Banshee) to $20,200 (Piccia Furia and Gallivanter Baller ST-D). Damage reduces the payout and a clean car earns an extra condition bonus. Destructoid says that bonus can be very large on some models, so repair the car at a gas station before you hand it over.

Do Wish List cars have to come from Randy’s locations?

No. Randy accepts the right model from anywhere, including parked cars, dealerships or cars driving past (PowerPyx, Push Square). His lead cars only spawn after he gives you the lead, so the fixed open-world spots in this guide are usually faster.

How do you get the Truffade Thrax for Randy?

It unlocks after you deliver every other car. Randy sends you to the Dalton Island Club (take the ferry from Waxman Island) for the mission Mansion Thrax. Quietly deal with the two guards, clone the key, drive onto the ferry and expect a 3-star wanted level when you get off. Then lose the police and park it in the garage Randy names (Push Square).

What is the reward for finishing Randy’s Wish List?

After the Thrax, Randy’s Delivery Day mission (Jason and Lucia together) pays $25,000 and the Supply & Demand trophy/achievement. Push Square reports that Randy then gives you Raul’s Pegassi Tempesta in a garage in Balsero and the Domestic Timbers garage. That last part comes from one outlet so far.
