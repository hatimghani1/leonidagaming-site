GTA 6 Bigfoot Location: How to Find the Skunk Ape and Finish Kirk’s Missions

Yes, Bigfoot is in GTA 6. Leonida calls him the Skunk Ape (or Mr. Mangrove), and he is more than an Easter egg: he anchors a three-part Strangers & Freaks questline for a cryptid hunter named Kirk. You can see Bigfoot at Cottonmouth Lake, south of Hamlet, from early Chapter 1, then start Kirk’s missions from a flyer in the Rusty Anchor bar (Key Lento) or Skeeter’s bar (Hamlet). Here is where to find him, every step of all three missions, the nine tree effigies, and which ending pays $10,000.

Updated 24 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, version 01.002.002. Spoiler-light: the ending is hidden behind a spoiler toggle.

Status on 24 November 2026: Rockstar has not published a guide to this questline, so everything below comes from hands-on launch-week walkthroughs (GamesRadar+, PowerPyx, GTABase, Game Rant, Beebom), cross-checked against each other. Where they disagree (for example, who can play the final mission), we say so.

Just starting out? The Skunk Ape chain is one of the best things to do in the Keys alongside our early-game money guide and the Chapter 1 to-do list before Tourist Trap. Tracking 100%? Tick each part off in our interactive 100% checklist, which now has a Strangers & Freaks section.

Quick answer: where is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

  1. Sighting: drive the road south of Hamlet to the wooded dirt road on the south-east side of Cottonmouth Lake (Mariana County, across the water from Key Lento). Bigfoot is eating a deer carcass. (PowerPyx, Game Rant, Beebom)
  2. Follow him into the trees to a tree effigy with green mist, inspect it (hold L2/LT) and you pass out. (PowerPyx, Beebom)
  3. Start the missions: read Kirk’s Skunk Ape flyer, just inside the front door on the right at the Rusty Anchor, Key Lento, or at Skeeter’s bar, Hamlet, then call Kirk. (GamesRadar+, GTABase, Game Rant)
  4. Play all three parts at night in the Mariana County swamps: Search for the Skunk Ape, Search for Tree Effigies, Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove. (PowerPyx, GTABase)
  5. Reward: the unique Mister Mangrove Laird 350 tranquilizer rifle for everyone, plus $10,000 for one of the three endings. (GamesRadar+, PowerPyx, GTABase)

Where to find Bigfoot in GTA 6 (open-world sighting)

The sighting is optional. GamesRadar+ says you do not need it for the questline, but it is the fastest way to meet the creature and some players report that Kirk’s flyers only show up after it (The Games Wiki). Game Rant notes there is no time-gate: you can go as soon as the first mission is done.

Cottonmouth Lake (main spot)

  1. From Key Lento, head north onto the mainland and take the road south of Hamlet toward Cottonmouth Lake in Mariana County.
  2. On the south-east side of the lake, turn onto the wooded dirt road. Keep driving as the tyres start to sink and listen for a low growl (Beebom).
  3. Near the end of the road, Bigfoot is eating a deer carcass. He runs off when you approach (PowerPyx, Game Rant).
  4. Chase him on foot into the swamp. You cannot catch him and, per Game Rant, bullets do not hurt him, so save your ammo.
  5. He disappears behind a tree effigy surrounded by green mist. Stand at it and hold L2/LT to inspect it. The spores knock you out, Bigfoot comes to look at you, and you wake up near your vehicle (PowerPyx, Beebom).

South of Watson Bay (second spot, single source)

Beebom reports a second sighting south of Watson Bay: find the small island ringed by a circular dirt road, take the road east of it, then use Focus to follow the growling until Bigfoot crosses the mud flats. The chase ends at the same kind of effigy. Only one outlet lists this spot so far, so treat it as a backup.

How to start Kirk’s missions: the Skunk Ape flyer

The questline starts with a “Have you seen the Skunk Ape?” flyer. Interact with it to add Kirk to your phone contacts, then call him. He gives you a meeting place (marked KK on the map, per GamesRadar+) and asks you to come in the evening.

  • Rusty Anchor, North Key Lento: walk in through the main doors and look immediately right; the flyer is on the notice board behind the open door (GamesRadar+, Beebom, The Games Wiki).
  • Skeeter’s bar, Hamlet: the flyer Game Rant and GTABase point to.
  • Elsewhere: The Games Wiki says 20+ flyers are posted around the Keys on notice boards (Ammu-Nation, Fleeca, bars). Any of them works.

Part 1: Search for the Skunk Ape (Skunk Apeology 101)

1. Meet Kirk at night

The mission only starts at night. If it is daytime, sleep in your bed to skip ahead (GTABase, The Games Wiki). Head to the marker near Pelican River; Kirk’s drone lights lead you to him, standing by his truck. Talk to him (Focus mode on Kirk unlocks extra dialogue).

2. Hit the truck and follow the drone

Kirk hands you a stick: hit the back of his truck and a howl answers. Equip your flashlight from the equipment slot of the weapon wheel (L1/LB) before you go in. Follow the drone (pink blip on the minimap) with Kirk to a nesting site (GamesRadar+, The Games Wiki).

3. Search the area and chase Mr. Mangrove

When Kirk splits up, search the pink circle. Use Focus when you hear the howl to see the sound ripples, check the feeding site and the claw marks on a tree, then chase Mr. Mangrove to an effigy and inspect it (GamesRadar+, GTABase).

4. Run back to the truck

A huge wild boar appears. Run for Kirk’s truck (pink dot). The run is scripted: the boar rams you either way and Kirk gets you out (Beebom, GTABase). Kirk then sends you money and says he will be in touch, which GamesRadar+ says takes about half an in-game day.

Part 2: Search for Tree Effigies

Keep playing until Kirk texts you; it can take a while (GTABase). A large search area is then marked in the swamps of Pelican Valley.

  1. Drive to the pink search area. Night is easier for spotting them with Focus, but this part is not listed as night-only.
  2. Activate Focus to make effigies stand out through the trees (GamesRadar+, Beebom).
  3. Walk up and hold L2/LT to study each one. Beebom points to the northern dock area and the north-east of the zone as good places to start.
  4. Inspect any three. GamesRadar+ counts nine effigies in total and GTABase found extras outside its three marked spots, so you do not need specific ones. The mission ends as soon as you inspect the third.

Part 3: Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove (Advanced Skunk Apeonomics)

A few in-game days after the effigies, Kirk texts you again and the final mission appears at Cottonmouth Lake, night only.

Can Jason do it in Chapter 1? Sources disagree. PowerPyx, GTABase and Game Rant list this mission as Lucia-only, from Chapter 2. GamesRadar+ and Beebom played it as Jason in Chapter 1, and GTABase readers report the same. Practical answer: if Kirk’s text arrives in Chapter 1, go; if not, keep playing the story. It does not expire.

1. Hunt from the helicopter

Board Kirk’s helicopter. You get the unique Mister Mangrove Laird 350 tranquilizer rifle. Watch the spotlight and fire at Mr. Mangrove’s centre mass; GamesRadar+ says there is no bullet drop. GTABase says hitting him makes no difference here because he always escapes to a shack, but Beebom warns that with very few hits he jumps you inside, so land a few darts anyway.

2. Search the shack and pick him up

After landing, go into the shack on the right of the search area. Mr. Mangrove collapses; try to pick him up (Triangle/Y) and his spores knock you out (GTABase, Beebom). If he does attack, mash Square/X, then dart him (Beebom).

3. Frog and fly sequence

  • As a frog, eat one fly from each of three swarms: over the dead boar just outside the shack, under a lantern or green light on the way to the docks, and by a carcass below the docks. Use Focus to find them and keep away from alligators (GTABase, GamesRadar+, Beebom).
  • As a fly, head east to Kirk’s helicopter (marked after a few seconds) and listen to his conversation (GTABase).

The final choice: shoot Kirk or Mr. Mangrove?

You wake up in the shack with a dart rifle, Kirk and an unconscious Mr. Mangrove. You can shoot Mr. Mangrove, shoot Kirk, or shoot nobody. Every ending completes the mission and counts for Strangers & Freaks (PowerPyx, GTABase). Only one ending pays money. Make a manual save first if you want to see more than one; gta6walk notes that no guide has confirmed whether the mission can be replayed.

Tips before you go into the swamp

  • Bring the flashlight and equip it before you leave the truck. The swamp is pitch black (GTABase, The Games Wiki).
  • Use Focus mode constantly: it shows sound ripples, effigies and flies (GamesRadar+, Beebom).
  • Skip to night by sleeping at a safehouse instead of waiting (GTABase).
  • Watch out for alligators in the water and during the frog section. Read the Leonida map guide for the region overview.
  • Phone call never came? Update 01.002.002 fixed calls and texts that did not trigger and blocked mission content. Install it and keep playing (Rockstar Support; see GTA 6 patch notes).
  • Do it legit. We do not cover cheats, glitches or exploits for this questline.

FAQ: GTA 6 Bigfoot and the Skunk Ape

Where is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Drive the road south of Hamlet to the woods on the south-east side of Cottonmouth Lake in Mariana County, across the water from Key Lento. Bigfoot (the Skunk Ape) is eating a deer carcass by the dirt road. Chase him into the trees to a tree effigy with green mist, inspect it and you pass out (PowerPyx, Game Rant, Beebom).

How do you start the Skunk Ape mission in GTA 6?

Read one of Kirk’s “Have you seen the Skunk Ape?” flyers: one is on the right just inside the front door of the Rusty Anchor bar in Key Lento, another is in Skeeter’s bar in Hamlet. This adds Kirk to your contacts. Call him, wait until night and meet him near Pelican River in Mariana County to start Search for the Skunk Ape (GamesRadar+, GTABase, Game Rant).

Do you need to see Bigfoot before starting Kirk’s missions?

No. GamesRadar+ says the open-world sighting is not mandatory for the questline. The flyer is what unlocks Kirk, although some players report flyers only appear after the first sighting, so do the sighting first if you cannot find one.

Is the GTA 6 Bigfoot mission missable?

No. GamesRadar+ says the chain can be done from Chapter 1 but does not expire, so you can finish it later in free roam. The final part is listed by PowerPyx and GTABase as Lucia-only from Chapter 2, so it may not appear until then anyway.

What is the reward for the Bigfoot mission in GTA 6?

Every player gets the unique Mister Mangrove Laird 350 tranquilizer rifle in the final mission. Only one ending also pays $10,000 through BuckMe. GTABase reports a $1,000 payment from Kirk after the first mission; some players say they received $500.

Does the Skunk Ape questline count for 100% completion?

Yes. GTABase lists all three Kirk missions as required for 100% completion, and any ending counts as a completed strand toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy (PowerPyx, GTABase).

Can you kill Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Not during the open-world sighting: Game Rant reports bullets do not injure him and you cannot catch him. His fate is decided by the choice at the end of Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove (see the spoiler section of this guide).
