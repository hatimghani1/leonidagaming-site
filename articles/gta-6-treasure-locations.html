GTA 6 Treasure Locations: All Kenton Shipwrecks, Dive Sites and Payouts

Diving for Kenton’s Treasures is the calmest money in Leonida: no police, no fence, just a boat, a scuba tank and a metal detector. Launch guides agree there are six shipwrecks off Key Lento plus two open-water dive sites, holding 41 relics in 7 sets and 10 gold doubloons. Below: how to unlock diving, where every site is, what each one holds, how the metal detector works, what Kenton pays and what counts for 100%.

Updated 27 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: Chapter 1 mission names only.

Status on 27 November 2026: the unlock (Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams), the six shipwreck names and the gear are confirmed by several hands-on guides (GameSpot, Push Square, KeenGamer, PowerPyx). Rockstar has not published an official treasure map, so locations, item lists and prices are launch-week player reports, credited line by line. Where sources disagree (set bonus, total value), we say so. No glitches or exploits.

How to unlock diving in GTA 6 (Island Dreams)

Treasure hunting opens with Island Dreams, a Jason mission in Chapter 1. RaidDex places it after Hey Arnold!; it starts at Island Dreams Boat Charters in Key Lento, Kenton’s dive shop (GameSpot calls him a captain who charters boat tours for divers).

  1. Meet Kenton and take a bachelor party out to the first wreck.
  2. Lead the divers through an underwater trench and search the wreck with the metal detector. Stay inside the marked area: the treasure is buried in sand, not between rocks.
  3. Free the diver who gets stuck at the wreck.
  4. On the way back a shark attacks. Equip the Arras Bullhead and shoot until it swims off.

RaidDex says you find three treasures in the mission and Kenton pays $400 afterwards (single-source figure). Those finds already count towards the first wreck. Kenton then calls, and purple chest markers appear on the map under “Search the Dive Sites”.

Where to get scuba gear

  • Kenton’s yellow boat at Island Dreams Boat Charters: walk to the stern and pick Scuba Gear (Triangle / Y). A second set sits on the dock if you want to take another boat (RaidDex, GTA BOOM).
  • Your own boat: GameSpot says Kenton has the scuba gear, Arras Bullhead and metal detector delivered to a boat you own outside Jason’s house.
  • Kenton’s boats: the Huntress is always docked at his shop; Push Square says the Rugger Fortune becomes available after the mission Maritime Disputes. Both can use autopilot (tap Triangle twice) and drop anchor from the vehicle menu (Push Square).

Whatever boat you take must have gear on board. If your own boat runs out after Chapter 1, go back to Kenton’s dock (RaidDex).

How to use the metal detector (step by step)

  1. Set a waypoint on a purple chest marker and stop the boat near the middle of the purple search circle.
  2. Jump in and hold R1 / RB to dive. Stay close to the seabed.
  3. Open the weapon wheel (L1 / LB) and pick the metal detector.
  4. Hold L2 / LT and turn slowly. Red means nothing nearby. When the light turns green and the beeps speed up, swim that way (GameSpot, RaidDex).
  5. A gold icon appears just before the find. Follow it even if the light changes, then press Triangle / Y at Treasure.
  6. Green light but no Treasure prompt? It is a chest. Look for a dark lump in the sand, hold L2 + R2 / LT + RT at Pull Out Chest and tap X / A, then open it (RaidDex, gta6wiki.eu).
  7. Oxygen shows top left. Press X / A to surface when it runs low, then dive again.

Tip: you don’t need to grid-search the whole circle. Circle the wreck’s edge first; several pieces lie a short swim away in open sand or along the reef (GTA BOOM, RaidDex). Select a purple marker on the map to see a counter such as “6/10 Treasures Found” before you dive.

One small naming slip to ignore: GameSpot’s list calls the first wreck “Nuestra Señora del Ocho”; the in-game name reported by every other guide is del Ocio.

Missing one last piece?

  • If you are exactly one short, it is usually a buried chest (La Noche and Ética Laxa each have one).
  • At Del Ocio, swim inside the hull through the opening; the map marker only clears at 10/10 (RaidDex, gta6wiki.eu).
  • Sweep open sand and the reef edge away from the ship, not just the hull.

The order Kenton unlocks the sites

  1. Island Dreams: Nuestra Señora del Ocio (story).
  2. First call: La Noche and Ética Laxa.
  3. Next calls: Cinco Torres, Lujuriosa and Santa Sierra, after you hand in completed sets and check in with Kenton.
  4. Last: Belpoint Strait and Bobcat Dive Site, far to the west and southwest. Take a fast boat with scuba gear and watch for new purple markers.

The order comes from RaidDex and Playtested; both say Kenton sends the next site after you hand in a full set, so bring finished sets to him promptly. He goes quiet for a while after Chapter 1 and comes back in Chapter 2 or 3.

How much Kenton pays for treasure

Sell at the counter of Island Dreams Boat Charters: hold L2 / LT to talk to Kenton and pick Treasures (doubloons have their own Doubloons line). The price shows before you confirm, and you can sell single pieces before a wreck is clear (KeenGamer, RaidDex).

GameSpot says Kenton eventually pays “tens of thousands” if you find every piece; the per-piece reports above point lower, so treat that as the optimistic end until the set bonus is measured. Either way it is low-heat money: PC Gamer calls treasure diving “mildly lucrative” but very time-consuming. For faster cash later, see jewelry store robberies or selling cars.

Chapter 1 warning: cash and your BuckMe checking balance are wiped when Chapter 1 ends (Game Rant). Sell your finds and move up to $5,000 into Family Savings before the point of no return. Our before Tourist Trap checklist has the full list.

Is anything missable?

No dive site is missable. GamesRadar+ says Kenton’s shipwrecks are only temporarily disabled after Chapter 1, and RaidDex says his wrecks stay open after the story. What you can lose is unbanked Chapter 1 cash, so sell and save before Tourist Trap.

Detector red, no prompt, shark: quick fixes

  • Detector stays red: you are outside the purple circle or too high. Drop to the seabed and turn a full circle slowly.
  • Green light, no Treasure prompt: it’s a chest. Look for the dark lump and pull it out with L2 + R2 / LT + RT.
  • No scuba prompt on your boat: go back to Kenton’s dock for gear.
  • Kenton doesn’t call: hand in a complete set and check in with him; after Chapter 1 he returns in Chapter 2 or 3. If a story call never arrives at all, install patch 01.002.002, which fixed missing phone calls (patch notes).
  • Shark attack: equip the Arras Bullhead and shoot several times. Ammo is low, so aim well (GameSpot, RaidDex).

FAQ: GTA 6 treasure locations

Where are the treasure locations in GTA 6?

There are eight Kenton dive sites. Six shipwrecks lie off Key Lento: Nuestra Señora del Ocio (the Island Dreams wreck, 10 doubloons), Nuestra Señora de la Noche, Nuestra Señora de la Ética Laxa (off Abismo Key), Urca de Cinco Torres, Nuestra Señora Lujuriosa and Urca de Santa Sierra. Two open-water sites without a wreck are Belpoint Strait and the Bobcat Dive Site west of Bobcat Key (Push Square, RaidDex, GTA BOOM).

How do you unlock treasure hunting in GTA 6?

Play the Chapter 1 story mission Island Dreams with Kenton Rees at Island Dreams Boat Charters in Key Lento. It gives you scuba gear, the metal detector and the Arras Bullhead speargun, and afterwards Kenton marks dive sites on your map with purple chest icons (GameSpot, RaidDex).

How many treasures are there in GTA 6?

Launch guides count 41 relics in 7 Kenton sets (7, 5, 6, 6, 6, 5 and 6 pieces), plus 10 gold doubloons at Nuestra Señora del Ocio that are not part of a set (KeenGamer, Push Square, RaidDex).

How much does Kenton pay for treasure in GTA 6?

About $200 per treasure, so roughly $1,000 to $1,400 for a full set (KeenGamer, PowerPyx, Output Lag). A full-set bonus is reported but its size is unconfirmed. All 41 relics come to about $8,200 before bonuses (RaidDex).

Where do you get scuba gear in GTA 6?

On the stern of Kenton’s yellow boat outside Island Dreams Boat Charters, with a second set on the dock. GameSpot says Kenton also has the gear delivered to a boat you own outside Jason’s house.

Do you need all treasures for 100% in GTA 6?

No. The 100% checklist needs 5 of the 7 Kenton sets, but also all 33 Underwater Discoveries. The six shipwrecks count as discoveries; Belpoint Strait and Bobcat Dive Site do not (PowerPyx, Push Square).

Can you miss Kenton’s treasures in GTA 6?

No. Kenton goes quiet after Chapter 1 and returns in Chapter 2 or 3, and the wrecks stay open after the story (GamesRadar+, RaidDex). Only unbanked Chapter 1 cash is lost, so sell finds and use Family Savings before Tourist Trap.
