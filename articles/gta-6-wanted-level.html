GTA 6 Wanted Level Explained: Stars, Heat Icons and How to Lose the Cops

GTA 6’s wanted system works in two layers. The stars (up to 6) show how hard the police are hunting you right now, and their colour tells you what they know. The heat icons under the stars show how they will recognise you: your face, outfit, car, visible gun, your partner or CCTV. To lose the cops: break line of sight, get out of the search area, clear every red icon, then stay out of the red heat zone until it fades. Here is what every star and icon means, a step-by-step escape plan, and what being Busted or Wasted really costs.

Updated 26 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S launch version. Spoiler-free.

Status on 23 November 2026: Rockstar explains the star states in-game, and the meanings below match Destructoid’s transcription of that help text plus hands-on guides from Polygon, GameSpot and Tech4Gamers. Star thresholds (how many cars, when helicopters arrive) are launch-week player reports and are labelled. No cheats, glitches or exploits: everything here is normal gameplay.
Stars that never cleared? That was a bug. Update 01.002.002 (23 November 2026) “fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to lose their Wanted Level” (Rockstar Support). Install it, then use the steps below. Details: GTA 6 patch notes.

Quick answer: how to lose the cops in GTA 6

  1. Leave before they arrive. If the stars are hollow or “responding”, just drive away calmly. (Polygon, Tech4Gamers)
  2. Break line of sight. Corners, off-road tracks, forests, bridges, water and buildings. (Polygon, Tech4Gamers)
  3. Exit the flashing search area on the minimap without being spotted. The stars flash, then disappear. (Destructoid, Tech4Gamers)
  4. Clear the red heat icons: swap hat/glasses, change outfit, respray or swap the car, holster or stash the gun, split Jason and Lucia up, destroy local CCTV. (Polygon, GameSpot)
  5. Stay out of the red heat zone on the map (or lie low) until it fades. Do not commit a new crime inside it. (Destructoid, Polygon)
  6. Do not kill cops. It raises the level and widens the search. Shoot tyres instead. (Polygon, Tech4Gamers)

How the wanted system works in GTA 6

Three big changes from GTA 5 decide everything:

  • A crime must be reported. Police only respond if a witness calls it in or an alarm goes off (GameSpot). No witnesses, or only criminal witnesses, means no wanted level at all (Polygon).
  • Police are not on the minimap. There are no vision cones to dodge. Use Focus to spot officers through walls, and the police scanner app to hear what dispatch knows (GameSpot, Tech4Gamers).
  • Losing the chase is not the end. After the stars go, a red heat zone stays on the map around your last known location. Get recognised or commit a crime there and the wanted level comes straight back (Destructoid).
Two clocks, not one: the stars measure the active hunt; the heat icons and red zone measure how easily you are recognised afterwards. You have to beat both.

GTA 6 wanted levels: 1 to 6 stars

Six stars are back (GameSpot). The number depends on how serious the crime is, and each extra star brings more units and a bigger search area. Reported responses at launch:

All GTA 6 heat icons and how to clear each one

The icons under the stars show what the police can recognise. A red icon is still active; it turns white or disappears once that identifier no longer matches (Polygon, Tech4Gamers). One matching detail is enough for an officer to recognise you, so clear them all.

IconPolice know…How to clear it
HeadYour appearancePut on or take off a cap and sunglasses from the item wheel (hold L1/LB), or get a haircut. A mask worn during the crime and removed out of sight works; putting one on after looks suspicious. (Polygon, Tech4Gamers)
Clothes hangerYour outfitChange clothes: keep a spare outfit in your personal vehicle’s trunk or buy one at a store (Polygon, GameSpot).
CarYour vehicleLose the chase first, then respray at a Pay ’n’ Spray (all 19 locations) or switch cars unseen (Polygon, GameSpot).
GunYou have a visible weaponHolster a handgun. Long guns cannot be hidden on your body: drop them or put them in a trunk (Polygon).
Two headsYour partner (two suspects)Talk to your partner and split Jason and Lucia up. Later in the story both need new looks (GameSpot).
CameraYou are on CCTVFind the recorder at the scene (in shops, usually behind the counter) and destroy the footage (Polygon).
Camera + cloudFootage is in the cloudCannot be destroyed. Rely on a disguise: clear everything else and leave the area (Polygon, Tech4Gamers).

Also: blood on your clothes or a burst dye pack draws attention, so shower at a safehouse (Tech4Gamers). Carrying a spare cap and glasses costs nothing and fixes the most common icon in seconds.

Step-by-step: how to lose a high wanted level in GTA 6

1. Get distance without raising the level

Use the fastest vehicle you have. A sports bike cuts through traffic well (Tech4Gamers); see our fastest cars in GTA 6 for getaway picks. If you must shoot, aim at tyres and limbs, not heads: every cop you kill adds stars and widens the search (Polygon). Tech4Gamers adds that killing police pushes your Criminal Profile toward Violent.

2. Break line of sight

Take several corners, then go off-road or into a forest, dive underwater, or go into a building (Polygon). On foot, climb ladders and slip through unlocked doors (Tech4Gamers). Against helicopters, cover beats speed: bridges, tunnels and tree canopy.

3. Hide if you cannot outrun them

In a car, hold Circle (PS5) / B (Xbox) to duck down; it also works in a parked car you have just jumped into (Tech4Gamers, single source). Sometimes simply ducking into a shop for a few minutes is enough after a minor crime (Radio Times).

4. Leave the search area

Get outside the flashing area on the minimap without being seen. The stars flash, and if no one spots you again, they disappear (Destructoid). Tech4Gamers names the train tracks in southwest Vice City and north of Ambrosia as clean escape routes with few police cars (single source). Check the regions on our GTA 6 map guide.

5. Clear the heat icons before you are seen again

If an officer spots you while icons are still red, the chase restarts and the heat zone refreshes (Polygon). Change hat, outfit and car, put the gun away, split up, then move on.

6. Stay out of the red zone

Outside the heat zone there is no wanted level (Polygon). It fades over time; higher levels last longer. Hover over the red area on the map to see which star level is still active there (Tech4Gamers). You can return once your icons are clear, but do not wave a gun around near the crime scene.

How to commit crimes without getting a wanted level

  • No witnesses, no report. Pick remote areas or quiet shops: one clerk, no customers, knock them out, destroy the CCTV, leave clean (Polygon).
  • Mask up first. Wear the mask during the job and take it off out of sight.
  • Watch for alarms. An alarm reports you even without witnesses (GameSpot).
  • Criminals do not call the cops. If the only witnesses are other criminals, police never hear about it (Polygon).
  • Night jobs mean fewer people on the street (GameSpot preview analysis).
  • Bank first. Before any risky job, deposit cash at an ATM (see below). Our GTA 6 money guide explains BuckMe and Family Savings.

Florida-style open carry also applies in Leonida: GameSpot’s preview reports that a gun on your back is not reported by itself, but aiming it at people is.

Stolen car trackers and alarms

High-end cars can be fitted with trackers (GameSpot):

  • Yellow tracker: police trace the car if you stop and wait for a few seconds.
  • Red tracker: the location is pinged whenever you drop below a certain speed, so keep moving.

Polygon recommends checking a car with the Waink app before stealing it to see if it has an alarm. Some Pay ’n’ Spray shops refuse cars depending on the tracker fitted (Tech4Gamers). Selling stolen cars is covered in the Port Gellhorn money guide and the vehicles hub.

Wanted level vs Criminal Profile: when police stop letting you surrender

Your stars are short-term; your Criminal Profile is a long-term score of how violent Jason and Lucia are (Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho, Irredeemable). Killing cops raises your wanted level now and drags your profile toward Violent (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+). The profile then changes how police treat you in every later chase:

  • Professional to Violent: normal police response; you can still surrender.
  • Psycho: you cannot surrender, and officers open fire faster (PC Gamer, GameSpot, GamesRadar+).
  • Irredeemable (permanent): no surrender, and police shoot on sight even at 1 star (Game Rant, Mobalytics).

So the cheapest way to keep chases survivable is to lose the cops instead of fighting them, and to shoot tyres and limbs when you must. Full breakdown and how to climb back: GTA 6 Criminal Profile explained.

Busted or Wasted in GTA 6: what you lose

Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson said it on the record (Trois Couleurs interview): undeposited cash is gone if you are wasted. Launch guides (GameSpot, Game Rant, The Nerd Stash, Tech4Gamers) add the rest:

ItemWasted / Busted
Cash in your pocket (max $20,000)Lost (GameSpot reports a smaller loss on arrest; plan for all of it)
BuckMe bank balanceSafe, minus a small reported BuckMe fee (amount not published)
Weapons you picked upLost
Weapons you ownKept: in your vehicle trunk or the Ammu-Nation weapons locker
Stash bags / stolen goods carriedLikely lost (confiscated on arrest per Game Rant)

Stolen, unregistered carGone, so use stolen cars for crimes (Tech4Gamers, gta.guide)

FAQ: GTA 6 wanted level and heat

How do you lose the cops in GTA 6?

Break line of sight (corners, off-road, forests, water or buildings), leave the flashing search area on the minimap without being spotted, then clear the red heat icons by changing your hat or glasses, outfit and car, hiding your gun and splitting Jason and Lucia up. Finally stay out of the red heat zone until it fades (Polygon, Destructoid, Tech4Gamers).

What do the different star colours mean in GTA 6?

Solid gray means cops are responding; hollow stars mean the suspect is unknown; solid white means police can see you and are in pursuit; gray after a chase means they lost visual; red means heat remains in the area around your last known location; black with a red outline means heat but no description of you (Rockstar in-game help via Destructoid).

What is heat in GTA 6?

Heat is the red area on the map around your last known location after you lose the police. Officers keep looking for you there, and being recognised or committing a crime inside it brings your wanted level back. Leave the area or lie low until it clears.

Does Pay n Spray remove your wanted level in GTA 6?

Not by itself. A respray clears the vehicle heat icon so police stop recognising the car, but you should lose the chase first. Some Pay n Spray shops refuse cars depending on the tracker fitted (GameSpot, Tech4Gamers).

Can you see police on the minimap in GTA 6?

No. Police cars and their vision cones are not shown on the minimap. Use Focus to spot officers and the police scanner app to hear what dispatch knows (GameSpot, Tech4Gamers).

What is the max wanted level in GTA 6?

Six stars, returning from older GTA games (GameSpot). Reaching it takes a prolonged crime spree, and Tech4Gamers reports the six-star heat area can stretch across the whole map.

What do you lose when you get busted in GTA 6?

Launch guides agree you lose the cash in your pocket and any weapons you picked up but do not own, and stolen goods are likely confiscated. Money in BuckMe and weapons you own are kept, apart from a small reported BuckMe fee. There is no jail time; you respawn at or near a safehouse (GameSpot, Game Rant).

Do you lose your car when you get busted in GTA 6?

Not if it is registered. If you are Busted or Wasted in your own car, or leave it in the police search area, it is towed to the nearest impound lot and listed under Map > Personal > Impounded Vehicles. Steal it back for free (with a 1–2 star wanted level) or, from Chapter 2, pay the Scooter Bros app to retrieve it. Full steps: GTA 6 impound lot guide. Stolen cars you never registered are gone (Tech4Gamers, Polygon, Game8).

Why can’t I surrender to the police in GTA 6?

Surrender is removed once your Criminal Profile reaches Psycho or Irredeemable (PC Gamer, GameSpot, GamesRadar+). At Irredeemable, which is permanent, Game Rant and Mobalytics report police open fire even at a 1-star wanted level. Avoid killing cops and civilians to bring the profile back down.
