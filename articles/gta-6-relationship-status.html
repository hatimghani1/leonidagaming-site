GTA 6 Relationship Status Explained: How to Reach Hot Together with Jason and Lucia

Jason and Lucia’s bond in GTA 6 has four levels: Running on Empty, Partners in Crime, Crazy in Love and Hot Together. It rises through small, everyday things you do together (greeting each other, holding hands, buying food, robberies as a pair, going home at night) and drops when you ignore texts, stand your partner up or play as only one character. It also feeds into which ending you get. Here is how it works, what moves the meter, and a simple routine to reach Hot Together without spoilers.

Updated 25 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Spoiler-light: no story events or ending details.

Status on 25 November 2026: the four level names, how to check them and the main actions are consistent across Polygon, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer hands-on guides. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told Polygon that the relationship is one of the factors behind the ending, and told press the romance itself is optional. Individual actions below are reviewer and launch-week player reports; Rockstar has not published exact point values.

Quick answer: how to improve your relationship in GTA 6

  1. Spend time together. Invite your partner to every mission and activity that allows it. (Polygon, GamesRadar+)
  2. Use the small interactions: greet your partner, hold hands, use the Talk and heart (kiss/hug) prompts. (Polygon, PC Gamer)
  3. Treat them: buy food or drinks and hand them over, restock their ammo at Ammu-Nation. (Polygon, GamesRadar+)
  4. Do crimes as a couple: successful robberies and gang hideouts together raise the bond faster than solo play. (GamesRadar+, PC Gamer)
  5. Answer texts and calls on WhatUp, and go home or say goodnight at night. (PC Gamer, GamesRadar+)
  6. Play as both characters. Switch often (hold right on the d-pad, then R1/RB): each side has to contribute. (GamesRadar+, Insider Gaming)

Each positive action shows a pink +heart on the right side of the screen with a chime (Polygon, PC Gamer). If you see it, it counted.

How to check your relationship status

  • Quick view: hold right on the d-pad. The character wheel shows the current heart icon (GamesRadar+, Polygon).
  • Full view: open the GTA 6 Index and go to the Jason & Lucia menu; the heart is at the bottom of the screen, next to each character’s Criminal Profile (GamesRadar+, Polygon).

Step-by-step: a daily routine to reach Hot Together

The meter grows slowly, and GTA Wiki notes that main missions alone are not enough. Free roam time together does the work. A routine that fits around the story:

  1. Morning: greet your partner, hold hands to the car, open the door for them.
  2. Before a job: visit Ammu-Nation together and restock their ammo; bank cash at an ATM first (what you lose if busted).
  3. During the day: do one robbery, hideout or bank job together, then one leisure activity (mini golf, gym, diving, haircut).
  4. Food stop: buy a snack or drink and hand it over.
  5. Switch characters at least once per session so both sides contribute. PC Gamer reached Hot Together with almost equal time as Jason and Lucia.
  6. Night: go home together and go to bed at the same time, or text goodnight if you are apart.
  7. Phone: answer every text and call from your partner, even when on a solo mission.
  8. Save: keep a manual save once you hit Hot Together (how to save in GTA 6).
Solo missions are fine. Both characters have missions they must or can do alone (PC Gamer). Just answer the phone and check in afterwards.

Chapter 1 head start: Coming Home

The relationship you start with is set in Chapter 1, when Jason picks Lucia up from prison in the mission Coming Home. Polygon reports that if Jason fixes up the house, buys flowers, collects Lucia’s clothes and picks her up on time, you start at Crazy in Love. Skip the optional prep and you start at Partners in Crime. KeenGamer lists the same errands (marked with an hourglass) and the house repairs.

Already past it? No problem: the level moves up and down freely, so the routine above still gets you to Hot Together. Full Chapter 1 checklist: before Tourist Trap.

Criminal Profile and the relationship

Jason and Lucia each have a separate Criminal Profile that tracks how violent you are (PC Gamer, Polygon): Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. It is a different meter, but the two are linked:

  • Irredeemable is permanent. You reach it by staying Psycho for too long; a crack through the Psycho icon is the warning (GamesRadar+, GameSpot).
  • GamesRadar+ warns that with an Irredeemable partner, the relationship may never recover, however many hearts you earn.
  • Crimes a mission requires do not count against you; only unnecessary violence does (GamesRadar+).

To protect both meters: wound or incapacitate instead of killing (yellow weak spots in Focus), avoid civilians and pets, and rob clean through back doors (Polygon). Our wanted level guide covers committing crimes without witnesses, and our GTA 6 Criminal Profile guide explains every level and how to climb back to Professional.

Does the relationship affect the ending? (spoiler-free)

Yes. Rob Nelson told Polygon there are several endings, and four things shape yours: story choices at branching moments, the relationship, your Criminal Profiles and how much is in Family Savings by the end. He also said there are no strictly “good” or “bad” endings.

Game Rant’s spoiler-free targets for the best possible ending, before the final point of no return in Chapter 5:

  • Relationship at Hot Together
  • Both Criminal Profiles at Professional or Hardened
  • $2,000,000 in Family Savings (see our GTA 6 money guide for the savings goals)
Save before the end. Game Rant reports that after your first completion the game returns you to just before the point of no return, but your relationship status is locked from then on. Keep a separate manual save before the final story mission if you want to see other endings. How many hours that takes: how long is GTA 6.

Can you romance other characters?

No. GamesRadar+ played 100+ hours and found no romance options beyond Jason and Lucia, and Polygon confirms you cannot fully break up or choose another partner. You can only decide how close the couple becomes.

FAQ: GTA 6 relationship status

How do you improve your relationship in GTA 6?

Spend time together: invite your partner to missions and activities, greet them, hold hands, use the Talk and heart prompts, buy them food or drinks, do robberies together, answer their texts and go home together at night. Play as both Jason and Lucia so both sides contribute (Polygon, GamesRadar+, PC Gamer).

What are the relationship levels in GTA 6?

There are four: Running on Empty (1/4), Partners in Crime (2/4), Crazy in Love (3/4) and Hot Together (4/4). You can move up and down between them at any time based on how you play (Polygon, GamesRadar+, PC Gamer).

How do you check your relationship status in GTA 6?

Hold right on the d-pad to see the heart icon on the character wheel, or open the GTA 6 Index and go to the Jason & Lucia menu, where the heart is shown at the bottom of the screen (GamesRadar+, Polygon).

Can Jason and Lucia break up in GTA 6?

No. There is no break-up and no 0/4 level. The lowest level is Running on Empty, and Jason and Lucia stay criminal partners on every mission. Some story moments and interactions change with the level (GamesRadar+, Polygon, Insider Gaming).

Does the relationship affect the ending in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told Polygon the ending is shaped by story choices, the relationship, both Criminal Profiles and your Family Savings balance. Game Rant's best-ending targets are Hot Together, Professional or Hardened profiles and $2,000,000 in Family Savings before the final point of no return.

Can you romance other characters in GTA 6?

No. GamesRadar+ found no romance options beyond Jason and Lucia after 100+ hours, and Polygon confirms you cannot choose another partner.

What is the fastest way to reach Hot Together in GTA 6?

Do joint activities and successful robberies together, since those move the meter faster than single gestures, and fill the gaps with greetings, holding hands, food, texts and going to bed at the same time. Prepare the house, flowers and clothes before Coming Home in Chapter 1 to start at Crazy in Love (Polygon, PC Gamer).
