GTA 6 file size: how many GB you need on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

GTA 6 needs 212 GB on PS5 and about 210 GB on Xbox Series X|S, before future updates. Here are the official figures, what the reported numbers mean, how much free space to really keep, how long the download takes, and how to make room without deleting your whole library.

Updated 21 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). No story spoilers.

Status on 21 November 2026: the game is on version 1.002.001 on both consoles, per launch reports. Rockstar has published no patch notes, and no new update has been reported since launch. GTA VI sold 32.5 million units before release and made $3.79 billion on day one, Rockstar said on 20 November, so expect servers and downloads to stay busy this weekend.

Why some sites say 225 GB for Xbox

Microsoft’s store counts in binary gigabytes. The same package is about 225.34 GB if you count in decimal gigabytes (1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes), which is what some news reports used. It is the same game, not a bigger Xbox version.

Why PS5 shows less than 212 GB after install

Radio Times reported the installed game on version 1.002.001 at about 210.5 GB on PS5, add-ons included. Sony’s 212 GB is a free-space floor for the download and install process, so clear the full amount anyway.

Tips to download faster

  1. Use a wired Ethernet cable if you can. It is the biggest single speed gain.
  2. Pause other downloads and streams on your network, including other consoles and 4K video.
  3. Leave the console in Rest Mode / Sleep mode overnight with automatic downloads on, as Rockstar’s pre-load FAQ recommends.
  4. Don’t keep cancelling and restarting. Pausing and resuming is fine; deleting the partial download starts you from zero.

How to free up space for GTA 6 (step by step)

On PS5

  1. Open Settings › Storage › Console Storage › Games and Apps.
  2. Press Delete Content, sort by size, and tick the biggest games you are not playing.
  3. Check Media Gallery too: long 4K captures add up fast.
  4. Have a USB drive? Move PS5 games to it instead of deleting them (Games and Apps › Move Items). Copying back later is faster than re-downloading.

On Xbox Series X|S

  1. Press the Xbox button and open My games & apps › See all › Manage to see what uses your drive.
  2. Uninstall large games, or use Free up space to remove optional add-ons and language packs.
  3. Move older or backward-compatible games to a USB drive (they can even run from there); move Series X|S games there for storage only.
  4. Delete old captures in Settings › Preferences › Capture & share if you save them to the console.

Expanding storage: what works for GTA 6

  • PS5 M.2 SSD: GTA VI can be installed and played from a compatible PCIe Gen4 M.2 NVMe SSD in the PS5’s expansion slot (Sony recommends 5,500 MB/s or faster and a heatsink).
  • Xbox Storage Expansion Card: the official Seagate or Western Digital cards run Series X|S games exactly like the internal drive.
  • USB external drives: fine for storing GTA VI on both consoles, but you cannot play it from USB. It has to be copied back to the internal or expansion SSD first.

GTA 6 file size FAQ

How big is GTA 6 on PS5?

Sony’s PlayStation Blog tells players to clear a minimum of 212 GB on PS5, and Rockstar says the download needs up to 212 GB depending on the console. Launch-week screenshots reported by RockstarINTEL showed about 209.75 GB to download and 212 GB installed. Plan for at least 220–230 GB free so updates have room.

How big is GTA 6 on Xbox Series X|S?

The Microsoft Store listing shows about 209.86 GB, the same figure for Series X and Series S. Some outlets quote 225.34 GB for Xbox; that is the same package counted in decimal gigabytes. Clear at least 230 GB to be safe.

Does GTA 6 fit on a Xbox Series S 512 GB?

Yes, but barely. The 512 GB Series S has about 364 GB of usable space, so GTA 6 takes well over half of it. Expect to uninstall other games or add a Storage Expansion Card.

Can I play GTA 6 from an external USB drive?

You can store it there, but not play it. PS5 games must run from the internal SSD or a compatible M.2 SSD, and Xbox Series X|S optimized games must run from the internal SSD or an official Storage Expansion Card. A USB drive is still useful to park the game and copy it back faster than re-downloading.

Does the GTA 6 disc or boxed copy reduce the download?

No. The boxed GTA VI copy contains a download code, not a disc, so you download the full game either way.

How big is the GTA 6 day-one patch?

Rockstar has not published patch notes. Reported pre-launch updates were 1.002.000 (13 November, about 1.07 GB on PS5) and 1.002.001 (16 November, about 602.5 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox). Pre-loaded copies usually installed both automatically.
