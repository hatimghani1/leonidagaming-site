GTA 6 Radio Stations: Every Station, Host and How to Unlock the 2 Secret Playlists

GTA 6 has 22 music stations (20 on the dial plus 2 unlockable playlists), 2 talk stations and 5 on-demand podcasts, according to the official credits Rockstar published at launch. Here is every station with its genre and hosts, how to listen on foot, and exactly where to scan the posters that unlock The Vault Radio and The Sniper of La Perle.

Updated 21 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). Light spoiler note in the unlock section (one location opens in Chapter 3).

Status on 21 November 2026: station names and hosts below are checked against Rockstar’s official GTA VI credits and music page. Unlock steps for the two secret playlists come from launch-week guides (PowerPyx, PC Gamer, Destructoid) and are labeled as reports. Rockstar has not published an in-game radio guide.

Quick answer

  • Music stations: 22 in the official credits, including 2 unlockable playlists (The Vault Radio, The Sniper of La Perle). (Official: Rockstar credits.)
  • Talk stations: The Wipe and WKTT. (Official.)
  • Podcasts: five on-demand shows, a series first. (Official.)
  • Songs: more than 800 licensed tracks are credited across the stations (GTA Culture counted 833 song credits; KeenGamer counted 810). (Our sources’ counts of the official credits.)
  • On foot: yes, through in-game headphones or earbuds paired with your phone. (Official, 8 October 2026.)
  • Returning favourites: Flash FM, V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM.

How to unlock the 2 secret playlists (The Vault Radio and The Sniper of La Perle)

Both playlists are in Rockstar’s credits, but neither is on the dial when you start. Launch guides agree on the method: scan posters with the WAiNK app on Jason’s or Lucia’s phone. PowerPyx reports the pair counts toward 100% completion under Progress › Collections › Exploration › Unique Playlists, which matches the “Unique Playlists (2 of 2)” item on our checklist. Everything in this section is a launch-week report, not an official Rockstar guide.

How to scan a poster with WAiNK

  1. Stand close to the poster. Your phone usually shows a WAiNK notification when something scannable is nearby (PC Gamer).
  2. Hold Up on the d-pad to bring up the phone camera/WAiNK scanner.
  3. Aim at the poster and press R1 (PS5) or RB (Xbox) to scan.

The Vault Radio: 1 poster

  1. Drive to The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City, south of Jack of Hearts and west of the NINE1NINE club (Destructoid, PC Gamer).
  2. Walk in through the front entrance and look at the back wall of the entrance corridor.
  3. Scan the red poster with a gas mask and “The Vault” on it.
  4. Tune in under Radio › The Vault in any vehicle or with headphones on. DJ Pretzel hosts the club’s house and techno set.

The Sniper of La Perle: 4 posters

Three posters are in La Perle (Vice City’s Haitian district) and one is in Crosstown. The game doesn’t show which ones you have already scanned, so do all four in one trip (PowerPyx).

How to change station and listen on foot

Rockstar’s 8 October 2026 announcement says that “for the first time ever” radio and podcasts are “available to listen to in vehicles and on foot through newly introduced headphones and earbuds connected to their smartphones.”

  • In a vehicle: launch guides report holding Left on the d-pad to open the radio wheel, then cycling to the station you want.
  • On foot: equip headphones or earbuds and use the same audio menu. Digital Spy’s review says headphones and earbuds come in several colours from electronics shops around the state.
  • On public transport: GameSpot’s review describes riding the bus with headphones on, listening to the radio or a true-crime podcast while checking texts.
  • Podcasts and unique playlists sit in the On Demand section; regular stations, including The Vault once unlocked, sit under Radio.

GTA 6 talk radio and podcasts

Talk stations (official credits)

  • The Wipe: three shows: Ask Dr. Nita (Anitta), El Otro Lado (John Leguizamo) and She Got Assets (Janelle James).
  • WKTT: The Richard Bastion Show (Jason Sudeikis) and Hit the Bricks (Stavros Halkias).

On-demand podcasts (official credits)

Drunk Kings, Final Act, Fuck Marry Murder, The Safe Trap (with Freddie Gibbs and The Alchemist) and Vice City True Stories (narrated by Billy Corben). Rockstar calls them “a series of on-demand podcasts for Jason and Lucia to experience beyond Leonida’s terrestrial radio”. Reviews mention true-crime episodes, and Digital Spy says some cover the history of Leonida and Liberty City.

The album, the score and where album songs play

  • Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album has 34 original tracks (Justice, Travis Scott, Dua Lipa, Morgan Wallen, Rauw Alejandro, JHAYCO, Burna Boy, Lana Del Rey, Keith Richards and more). It streams on major services and is sold on vinyl and CD. (Official: Rockstar music page.)
  • Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score by Justice: 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, out 11 December 2026 on streaming, CD and vinyl. Rockstar describes it as an adaptive score tuned to how you play. (Official.)
  • Album songs on the radio: most album tracks are in rotation. Per the credits as mapped by KeenGamer and GTA Culture: Travis Scott’s “RHYNO” is on Vice City Rap Radio, Dua Lipa’s “Go Easy” on Flash FM, Burna Boy’s “Bigger” on AfroBank FM, Rauw Alejandro’s “Macacoa 2000” on Cocoteo FM, and Keith Richards’ “Bright Lights, Big City” on Gloriana Blues Radio, the station he co-hosts. Morgan Wallen’s album song is credited to Flash FM, not Back Country Radio.
  • Previews: Rockstar’s music page has official audio previews of seven stations, so you can sample them before you play.

Which GTA 6 station to pick (our picks)

Personal taste, not data, but here is where to start:

  • Cruising Ocean Beach at sunset: Vice City FM or Flash FM for the full Vice City nostalgia hit.
  • Police chases: The Chamber 106.6 or V-Rock.
  • Airboats in the Grassrivers: Back Country Radio, which Rockstar pitches as “airboat-ready” country.
  • Long drives to Mount Kalaga: a podcast like Vice City True Stories, or Leonida Jazz Radio.
  • Laughs: WKTT and The Wipe. The Verge’s review singled out “the hilarious talk radio stations”.

GTA 6 radio stations FAQ

How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

Rockstar’s official GTA VI credits list 22 music stations, including the two unlockable playlists The Vault Radio and The Sniper of La Perle, plus two talk stations (The Wipe and WKTT) and five on-demand podcasts. Twenty music stations are on the dial from the start.

How do you unlock the secret radio stations in GTA 6?

Launch guides (PowerPyx, PC Gamer, Destructoid) report that you scan posters with the WAiNK app on your phone. The Vault Radio needs one red poster just inside The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City. The Sniper of La Perle needs four posters: Aunt Zola’s Soul Food, TWA Ponya Tattoo and Drip Drops in La Perle, and the recording studio upstairs in Jack of Hearts in Crosstown (available from Chapter 3).

Can you listen to the radio on foot in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar confirmed on 8 October 2026 that radio and podcasts play in vehicles and on foot through headphones and earbuds connected to Jason’s and Lucia’s smartphones. Launch reviews say headphones are sold at electronics shops around Leonida.

Who hosts the GTA 6 radio stations?

Hosts in Rockstar’s credits include Bad Bunny (Cocoteo FM), Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey (Back Country Radio), Burna Boy (AfroBank FM), Robyn and Alex Consani (Flash FM), Trick Daddy and Trina (Dirty South Classics), Kerry King and Tom Araya (The Chamber 106.6), “Weird Al” Yankovic and Brigitte Nielsen (Vice City FM), Lindsey Buckingham and Michael McDonald (V-Rock), and Gloria and Emilio Estefan (Radio La Ola).

Are Flash FM, V-Rock, Emotion 98.3 and Vice City FM back in GTA 6?

Yes. All four are in the official credits. Flash FM, V-Rock and Emotion 98.3 date back to GTA Vice City (2002); Vice City FM is the 1980s pop station hosted by “Weird Al” Yankovic and Brigitte Nielsen.

Can I stream the GTA 6 soundtrack?

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album (34 original tracks) is on major streaming services and sold on vinyl and CD via Rockstar’s music page. Justice’s Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score (30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks) arrives on 11 December 2026. Rockstar has not released a full in-game radio playlist on streaming services.
