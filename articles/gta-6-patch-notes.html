GTA 6 Patch Notes: Update 01.002.002 Fixes, Download Size and Known Issues

GTA 6 got its first post-launch update on 23 November 2026: version 01.002.002 on PS5 and 1.0.2.2 on Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar’s official notes list 7 fixes: crashes, weather stuck on one setting, autosave, phone calls that never arrived (including story and mission calls), a wanted level you could not lose, the gym deadlift bar, and partners not handing over bags for fences. It is about 604 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox, rolls out in stages, and adds no new content. Below: the full notes, what each fix means for your save, how to get the update, and the bugs players say are still there.

Updated 23 November 2026, 19:30 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S. Spoiler-free.

Status on 23 November 2026: the patch notes below are official, copied from Rockstar Support’s “Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Updates (PS5 / Xbox Series X|S)” page (last updated 23 November 2026). Download sizes and rollout details come from Polygon, RockstarINTEL and GTA6BUGS. “Still broken” items are player reports, clearly labelled, not Rockstar statements. This is the first update with published notes; the two pre-launch updates had none.

GTA 6 patch notes 01.002.002: the full official list

Rockstar Support, “[November 23, 2026] - General / Miscellaneous”, word for word:

  • Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly
  • Fixed several issues that resulted in phone calls not triggering correctly including those that progressed the story or unlocked mission content
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to lose their Wanted Level
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym
  • Fixed an issue that resulted in partners not handing over bags so that players could sell them to fences

Rockstar does not say which crashes or phone calls were fixed, and it gives no list of changed missions. Anything more specific you read elsewhere is a player observation, not part of the official notes.

GTA 6 update download size and how to get it

The update is small: 603.8 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S (Polygon, RockstarINTEL). Because it rolls out in stages, it may not appear the moment you look. Do not delete or reinstall the game to force it.

PS5 and PS5 Pro

  1. On the home screen, highlight Grand Theft Auto VI.
  2. Press Options and choose Check for Update.
  3. If nothing is offered, your wave has not arrived yet. Try again in a few hours, or leave the console in Rest Mode with automatic updates on.
  4. Confirm the version: highlight the game, press Options › Information; it should read 01.002.002.

Xbox Series X|S

  1. Open My games & apps and highlight Grand Theft Auto VI.
  2. Press the Menu button, choose Manage game and add-ons, then Updates.
  3. Install 1.0.2.2 if it is listed. Sleep mode with automatic updates on installs it for you.

Space check: GTA 6 already needs about 212 GB on PS5 and about 210 GB on Xbox. A patch needs some free working space to install, so keep a few GB spare. Full figures: GTA 6 file size.

What to do after updating (step by step)

  1. Make a manual save right before you quit your current session, then install the update.
  2. Load your latest manual save (not an old one: loading an older save can overwrite the autosave, per Dot Esports).
  3. Waiting for a call? Free-roam for an in-game day or sleep at a safehouse, then check the phone and map for new markers.
  4. Re-test what was broken for you: wanted level clearing, the gym, selling partner bags to a fence.
  5. Save to a new slot once things work, so you have a clean post-patch backup. GTA 6 gives you 15 manual slots.
  6. Still stuck? Report it on Rockstar Support with your platform, version number and the mission name. Do not use exploits or modded saves to get around it.

Bugs players say are not fixed yet (reports, not official)

Rockstar has not listed any known issues. These come from players who installed 01.002.002 and reported back on 23 November, so treat them as unconfirmed:

  • Jason greyed out after Missing Persons (Chapter 3): after the Missing Persons side mission in Kelly County, some players are stuck as Lucia and cannot switch back to Jason when the story needs him. GAMINGbible says players (including one of its own staff) still see it after the update, and GTA6BUGS logged 4 such reports. GAMINGbible’s advice: avoid Missing Persons while character switching is restricted, and keep several manual saves.
  • Raul’s call to Lucia after the Yasmani jewelry jobs: one player on GTA6BUGS says the update did not bring the call.
  • Fully Catered crash on the delivery cutscene: one player on GTA6BUGS still crashes on 01.002.002.
  • PS5 error CE-108255-1: no confirmed reports either way from updated players yet (GTA6BUGS, as of 19:00 Casablanca time on 23 November).
Protect your progress: the single best defence against any of these is a rotation of manual saves before every side mission chain and chapter change. Our Tourist Trap guide shows the save-first habit for Chapter 1, and the 100% checklist tracks what you have done outside your save file.

FAQ: GTA 6 patch notes and updates

What is the latest GTA 6 update?

Update 01.002.002 (1.0.2.2 on Xbox), released on 23 November 2026. It is the first post-launch patch and the first with official notes on Rockstar Support.

What does GTA 6 update 01.002.002 fix?

Seven things, per Rockstar Support: several crashes, weather not changing for long periods, autosave not working correctly, phone calls not triggering (including story and mission-unlock calls), being unable to lose a Wanted Level, the gym deadlift bar, and partners not handing over bags to sell to fences.

How big is the new GTA 6 update?

603.8 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S, according to Polygon and RockstarINTEL. RockstarINTEL says the total install size did not increase.

Why can’t I download the GTA 6 update yet?

It rolls out in stages through 23 November (Polygon). On PS5 use Options › Check for Update; if nothing appears, your wave has not arrived, so try again later. Do not reinstall the game.

Did the update fix GTA 6 autosave?

Rockstar lists “Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly” but does not say how often the game now autosaves. Keep making manual saves before you quit until that is clear.

Does the patch fix Jason being greyed out after Missing Persons?

It is not in the official notes, and players (GAMINGbible, GTA6BUGS) report it still happens after 01.002.002. Avoid Missing Persons while character switching is restricted and keep several manual saves.

Does the GTA 6 update add Online or new content?

No. It is a bug-fix patch with no new missions, vehicles or modes. Rockstar has not announced GTA 6 Online or a PC version.
