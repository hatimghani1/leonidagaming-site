GTA 6 mid-game money guide: Moses, hunting, and Port Gellhorn hustles

Chapter 2 is when repeatable money finally opens up: selling cars to Moses, hunting for Bret, and scraping together Starlet Motel rent if the story asks for it.

Updated 19 November 2026 (launch day). Light spoiler warning: Chapter 2 locations, unlocks, and a story money gate at the Starlet Motel. No ending details.

Updated as launch data drops — 19 November 2026. Figures are launch-day reports from Polygon, PC Gamer, and GameSpot, not official Rockstar payout tables. No cheats, glitches, or exploits.

If you are still in the Keys, start with the early-game money guide. If you already have the snap gun and Vice City fully open, skip ahead to the late-game money guide.

By Chapter 2, the action shifts to Port Gellhorn, a faded vacation town in the official six-region state of Leonida. Launch guides agree this is when the economy starts to breathe.

Always bank it

The mid-game is where people start carrying real cash — and then lose it. Launch reports still hold: cash on hand can disappear if you go Wasted; BuckMe / ATM deposits are safer; loot bags need a fence. Deposit often. Do not walk around Port Gellhorn with a full pocket “just until the next mission.”

Selling cars to Moses (the Chapter 2 staple)

The most important new earner in this chapter is Moses, a mechanic and car buyer in Port Gellhorn.

What launch guides agree on:

  • You meet Moses during Chapter 2 (GameSpot says he is one of two vehicle-contract contacts; Randy, the later high-end fence, is in western Vice City).
  • He explains the basics of stealing cars and supplies the early tools — PC Gamer and Polygon both mention a slim jim for older, lower-profile cars.
  • He wants untracked, older, low-profile vehicles, not the flashiest thing on the block. Some reports say he will even point you toward the cars he wants.
  • After a sale, Moses is on a cooldown. You cannot dump a second car on him immediately.
  • Condition matters more later with Randy, but it is still worth delivering a car that is not a burning wreck.

Reported payouts: PC Gamer puts ordinary Moses sales in the $1,000 to a few thousand range and calls that better, at this stage, than most smash-and-grabs — because your Chapter 2 fence pay is still relatively low. GameSpot describes some Moses contracts as paying more, “often upwards of $10,000.” Those two figures can both be true if contracts pay more than random street cars. Until more players publish spreadsheets, plan on a few thousand per regular sale, and treat five-figure contract claims as possible but not guaranteed.

Moses is the first steady, relatively low-risk loop in the game. Learn which cars he will take, check for trackers before you waste a drive across town, and use the cooldown time to do a hunt, a marked job, or a story mission.

Pay ’N’ Spray as a backup, not the main plan

You do not have to wait for Moses to be available. Polygon and GameSpot both say Pay ’N’ Spray shops will buy certain stolen cars, typically ones without an advanced tracker. This becomes a stronger loop later (Polygon highlights an Ocean Beach shop in Chapter 3+). In Port Gellhorn it is a useful overflow option when Moses is on cooldown. Details and later payouts are in the vehicles guide and the Vice City money guide.

Hunting for Bret (Jason only)

PC Gamer and Polygon describe hunting jobs for Bret, owner of Killtelligence Provisions, starting in Chapter 2 and available to Jason only. PC Gamer places him in Lummox County.

  • Take Bret’s requested hunts, or bag worthwhile animals you find in the wilds (officially, Mount Kalaga is the state’s hunting and fishing country — see the map guide).
  • Large animals — boars, bears, alligators — are the ones worth calling in. Launch reports say Bret can arrange a pickup so you do not haul a carcass across the state. Polygon notes that one-shot, pristine kills pay better (up to a few thousand).
  • Smaller game usually means you deliver the skins yourself, for less money.
  • PC Gamer also reports legendary animals, in the spirit of Red Dead Redemption 2, and gives a launch-day example of a large boar paying $2,500.

Hunting is slower than a clean car sale. It is still honest money, especially if you are already out in the backwoods, and it does not put a wanted level on you if you do it cleanly.

If you need rent for the Starlet Motel

Light spoiler. GameSpot reports that around the 30% mark of the story, Jason and Lucia end up at the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn. During this stretch you may need a couple of thousand dollars in rent before the story will move. GameSpot also notes you may be playing as Lucia here, with limited resources.

Their practical advice, which we are restating — not inventing:

  • Rob the stores marked on your map in Port Gellhorn (GameSpot describes green icons that can be hit by a single character): stick up the register, ask for the safe code if there is a back-room safe, and leave quickly.
  • Mug pedestrians only as filler; the take is small.
  • Sell cars to Moses whenever you can. This is the least chaotic way to assemble rent money.

You do not need a perfect, heat-free jewelry heist to clear this gate. You need a few thousand, banked, so you are not starting over after a bad death.

Other Chapter 2 jobs worth doing

Rob other criminals. PC Gamer’s top tip is to steal from crews already mid-job — a store hit, an armored car, a dock deal, or a bag-carrying getaway. Takes are reported around $1,000–$15,000, often better than running the same robbery yourself at this stage. Travel as Jason and Lucia when you can, so you can carry multiple bags to a fence. The heat that was on them becomes your heat, so grab the bags and leave.

Gang hideouts / illegal operations. GameSpot describes yellow map icons showing the gang, security level, and possible score. These pay in cash and goods you sell to a dealer, but they are gunfights, not errands. Use them when you are equipped; do not treat them as the rent solution.

Races and the Mud Club. Competitions appear more often now, including dirt racing associated with Ambrosia / the Thrillbilly Mud Club. Prize money is usually a few thousand and you can lose a buy-in. The better demolition-derby loop at Ambrosia Raceway is covered in the late-game guide.

What still waits until Chapter 3

Do not expect the snap gun, clone keys, Randy’s high-end car list, or easy bank work in Port Gellhorn. Those are the Vice City jump in income. Until then, Moses + banking + optional hunts and marked jobs is the honest mid-game plan.

FAQ

Where is Moses in GTA 6?

Launch guides place him in Port Gellhorn, unlocked during Chapter 2. He buys older, untracked cars and gives you early theft tools.

How much does Moses pay?

Regular sales are commonly reported at about $1,000 to a few thousand. Some contract payouts have been described as higher. We will tighten this range as more players report numbers.

What is the easiest way to make Starlet Motel rent?

Sell cars to Moses, hit the marked local stores, and bank every dollar. You need a few thousand, not a career-best heist.

Can Lucia hunt for Bret?

PC Gamer says Bret’s hunting missions are Jason only.
