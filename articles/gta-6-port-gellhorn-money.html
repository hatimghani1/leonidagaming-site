GTA 6 mid-game money guide: Moses, hunting, and Port Gellhorn hustles

Chapter 2 is when repeatable money finally opens up: selling cars to Moses, hunting for Bret, and scraping together Starlet Motel rent if the story asks for it.

By Chapter 2, the action shifts to Port Gellhorn, a faded vacation town in the official six-region state of Leonida. Launch guides agree this is when the economy starts to breathe.

Always bank it

Chapter 2 starts nearly broke: Game Rant reports that the end of Chapter 1 wipes your pocket cash, current account and house stash, leaving only Family Savings (see the Chapter 1 reset). Guns you owned can be taken back from the Ammu-Nation weapons locker, so check it before you buy. The mid-game is also where people start carrying real cash — and then lose it. Launch reports still hold: cash on hand can disappear if you go Wasted; BuckMe / ATM deposits are safer; loot bags need a fence. Deposit often. Do not walk around Port Gellhorn with a full pocket “just until the next mission.”

Selling cars to Moses (the Chapter 2 staple)

The most important new earner in this chapter is Moses, a mechanic and car buyer in Port Gellhorn.

What launch guides agree on:

  • Selling opens early in Chapter 2 after the main mission Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses), according to PowerPyx. GameSpot says he is one of two vehicle-contract contacts; Randy, the later high-end fence, is in western Vice City.
  • He only accepts cars with no tracker (PowerPyx). Scan first: hold Up on the D-pad to open WAiNK, aim at the car and press R1/RB to see its value, tracker and interested buyer.
  • He refuses cars stolen too close to his garage. PowerPyx’s fix: walk to the main road through Port Gellhorn and take a car from there.
  • He explains the basics of stealing cars and supplies the early tools — PC Gamer and Polygon both mention a slim jim for older, lower-profile cars.
  • He wants untracked, older, low-profile vehicles, not the flashiest thing on the block. Some reports say he will even point you toward the cars he wants.
  • After a sale his garage closes for a short cooldown, around 2 minutes (PowerPyx). Use it to scout the next car.
  • Condition matters more later with Randy, but it is still worth delivering a car that is not a burning wreck.

Reported payouts: PowerPyx says ordinary cars “typically pay $1000+” for one or two minutes of work. PC Gamer puts Moses sales in the $1,000 to a few thousand range and calls that better, at this stage, than most smash-and-grabs — because your Chapter 2 fence pay is still relatively low. GameSpot describes some Moses contracts as paying more, “often upwards of $10,000.” Those two figures can both be true if contracts pay more than random street cars. Until more players publish spreadsheets, plan on a few thousand per regular sale, and treat five-figure contract claims as possible but not guaranteed.

Moses is the first steady, relatively low-risk loop in the game. Learn which cars he will take, check for trackers before you waste a drive across town, and use the cooldown time to do a hunt, a marked job, or a story mission.

All 7 Moses Vehicle Drop-Offs (and the free Emperor Sultan)

Besides random sales, Moses has 7 Vehicle Drop-Offs: specific cars he texts you about. Push Square’s guide lists them all; you can track the ones left under Touch Pad > Robberies > Moses Drop-Offs. Finish all seven and Moses gifts you his Emperor Sultan, which costs $1,600 to register and keep.

Pay ’n’ Spray: useful for your own cars, not stolen ones (yet)

Correction (22 November): we previously called Pay ’n’ Spray a Chapter 2 backup for stolen cars. PowerPyx reports that selling stolen cars at any Pay ’n’ Spray unlocks early in Chapter 3, when you can also sell cars with a Standard Tracker (around $4,000+ for a good one). In Chapter 2, use the shops for repairs, resprays and selling cars you own, and send stolen cars to Moses. Polygon’s Ocean Beach loop is a Chapter 3+ method. Every buyer, the daily limit and step-by-step selling: how to sell cars in GTA 6. Details and later payouts are in the vehicles guide and the Vice City money guide.

Hunting for Bret (Jason only)

PC Gamer and Polygon describe hunting jobs for Bret, owner of Killtelligence Provisions, starting in Chapter 2 and available to Jason only. PC Gamer places him in Lummox County.

  • Take Bret’s requested hunts, or bag worthwhile animals you find in the wilds (officially, Mount Kalaga is the state’s hunting and fishing country — see the map guide).
  • Large animals — boars, bears, alligators — are the ones worth calling in. Launch reports say Bret can arrange a pickup so you do not haul a carcass across the state. Polygon notes that one-shot, pristine kills pay better (up to a few thousand).
  • Smaller game usually means you deliver the skins yourself, for less money.
  • PC Gamer also reports legendary animals, in the spirit of Red Dead Redemption 2, and gives a launch-day example of a large boar paying $2,500.

Hunting is slower than a clean car sale. It is still honest money, especially if you are already out in the backwoods, and it does not put a wanted level on you if you do it cleanly.

If you need rent for the Starlet Motel

Light spoiler. GameSpot reports that around the 30% mark of the story, Jason and Lucia end up at the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn. During this stretch you may need a couple of thousand dollars in rent before the story will move. GameSpot also notes you may be playing as Lucia here, with limited resources.

Their practical advice, which we are restating — not inventing:

  • Rob the stores marked on your map in Port Gellhorn (GameSpot describes green icons that can be hit by a single character): stick up the register, ask for the safe code if there is a back-room safe, and leave quickly.
  • Mug pedestrians only as filler; the take is small.
  • Sell cars to Moses whenever you can. This is the least chaotic way to assemble rent money.

You do not need a perfect, heat-free jewelry heist to clear this gate. You need a few thousand, banked, so you are not starting over after a bad death.

The $15,000 Vice City fund (Prepare for Vice City)

Near the end of Chapter 2 the story sets a bigger money target. PowerPyx’s walkthrough says the main mission Prepare for Vice City (after Coffee Break) asks Jason and Lucia to earn at least $15,000 while Dre’Quan sorts out a place to stay. Reaching it, and clearing the other Chapter 2 story missions, unlocks Checking Out, the move to Vice City.

Any money counts. PowerPyx recommends doing the jobs the game marks for you at this point, which pay and move the story on:

  • Thrillbillies (check out the Mud Club)
  • Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab (join Billy on a job)
  • Hit Avery Pawn and Hit Kingpin Bowling. The Kingpin Bowling safe code is fixed (0300, see Kolt safe codes); the Avery Pawn code is not, so threaten staff for it after you have seen the safe, or use a sticky bomb (GamesRadar+)
  • Impounded Vehicle (steal a towed car back from the Port Gellhorn police lot; see the impound lot guide)
  • Veteran Hunted (meet Bret, Jason only)
  • Afternoon Delight (rob Vicki’s boss)

Fill the gap with Moses sales and his drop-offs. Keep the money in your current account, not Family Savings: savings can never be withdrawn. Deposit at ATMs as you go so a bad death does not cost you the fund.

Kolt & Associates safe codes (Brooks Boatworks, La Cascara, Kingpin Bowling)

The three Kolt & Associates hideouts in Port Gellhorn each have a safe with a fixed code, the same for every player (Vice, tested in-game; VGC; Polygon):

  • Brooks Boatworks: 6823. Safe in the pool-table room inside the main warehouse, north Port Gellhorn. Polygon reports around $5,000 inside.
  • La Cascara Motel: 6969. Fourth (top) floor, next to the room with the Scrapper VF5C SMG (Game Rant, VGC).
  • Kingpin Bowling: 0300. Office safe in south Port Gellhorn; type the leading zero (Vice, VGC, Destructoid).

All three codes are on a map of Port Gellhorn on the wall of the small grey building on the east side of Brooks Boatworks (enter through the gap in the east fence). Inspecting it saves them to your notes, but you can also type them without finding the clue (Vice, VGC). Store and pawn shop safes are different: their codes are usually random. Other Chapter 2 jobs worth doing

Rob other criminals. PC Gamer’s top tip is to steal from crews already mid-job — a store hit, an armored car, a dock deal, or a bag-carrying getaway. Takes are reported around $1,000–$15,000, often better than running the same robbery yourself at this stage. Travel as Jason and Lucia when you can, so you can carry multiple bags to a fence. The heat that was on them becomes your heat, so grab the bags and leave.

Gang hideouts / illegal operations. GameSpot describes yellow map icons showing the gang, security level, and possible score. These pay in cash and goods you sell to a dealer, but they are gunfights, not errands. Use them when you are equipped; do not treat them as the rent solution. The Kolt hideout safes have fixed codes: see above.

Races and the Mud Club. Competitions appear more often now, including dirt racing associated with Ambrosia / the Thrillbilly Mud Club. Prize money is usually a few thousand and you can lose a buy-in. The better demolition-derby loop at Ambrosia Raceway is covered in the late-game guide.

What still waits until Chapter 3

Do not expect the snap gun, jewelry store jobs for Yasmani, Pay ’n’ Spray stolen-car sales, the Key Cloner and Randy’s Wish List, or bank work in Port Gellhorn. Those are the Vice City jump in income, covered in the late-game money guide. Until then, Moses + banking + optional hunts and marked jobs is the honest mid-game plan.

FAQ

Where is Moses in GTA 6?

Moses runs a garage in Port Gellhorn. PowerPyx says car selling opens early in Chapter 2 after the main mission Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses). He buys untracked cars and gives you a slim jim for older cars.

Why won’t Moses buy my car?

Usually one of three reasons (PowerPyx): the car has a tracker (Moses only takes cars with no tracker), it was stolen too close to his garage, or his garage is on its roughly 2-minute cooldown after your last sale. Scan cars with WAiNK before stealing them.

How much does Moses pay?

PowerPyx says ordinary cars typically pay $1,000+, and PC Gamer reports $1,000 to a few thousand. GameSpot describes some Moses contracts as paying more. Finishing all 7 Moses Vehicle Drop-Offs also earns his Emperor Sultan (Push Square).

How much money do you need for Vice City in GTA 6?

At least $15,000. PowerPyx says the Chapter 2 mission Prepare for Vice City needs that much before Checking Out (the move to Vice City) unlocks. See how to raise it.

What is the easiest way to make Starlet Motel rent?

Sell cars to Moses, hit the marked local stores, and bank every dollar. You need a few thousand, not a career-best heist.

What are the Port Gellhorn safe codes in GTA 6?

The three Kolt & Associates hideouts use fixed codes: Brooks Boatworks 6823, La Cascara Motel 6969 and Kingpin Bowling 0300. All three are on a wall map in the small grey building at Brooks Boatworks (Vice, VGC, Polygon). The Avery Pawn safe code is random: threaten staff after seeing the safe, or use a sticky bomb (GamesRadar+). Full list: GTA 6 safe codes.

Can Lucia hunt for Bret?

PC Gamer and PowerPyx say Bret’s hunting missions are Jason only.
