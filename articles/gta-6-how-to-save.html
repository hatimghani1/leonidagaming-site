How to Save in GTA 6: Manual Saves, Autosave, 15 Slots and When You Can’t Save

To save in GTA 6, press Options (PS5) or Menu (Xbox Series X|S), open the Game tab and choose Save Game, then pick one of 15 manual slots. You can do it anywhere in free roam, with no safehouse or bed needed. GTA 6 does autosave, but only into one slot that is overwritten, mostly when a mission ends, so players who quit after hours of free roam have lost progress. Below: the exact steps, when saving is greyed out, how autosave really works, how to load, and a 15-slot plan so you never lose a mission or a story choice.

Quick answer: how to save in GTA 6

  • Manual save: Pause → Game → Save Game → choose a slot → confirm. Works anywhere in free roam. (Polygon, Dot Esports, KeenGamer, Meristation.)
  • Save slots: 15 manual slots plus 1 separate autosave entry. (Polygon, Dot Esports, KeenGamer.)
  • Autosave: yes, mainly when a mission ends (plus some milestones and open-world events), always into the same single slot. (Polygon, Dot Esports, KeenGamer, Meristation.)
  • No phone quick save: unlike GTA 5, there is no quick-save option on the phone. (Dot Esports, Everyblip.)
  • You can’t save: during the prologue, during an active mission, in combat, with a wanted level, or while falling. (Polygon, Dot Esports, KeenGamer.)
  • No mission replay: an older manual save is the only way to replay a mission or see another outcome. (KeenGamer, Meristation, Dot Esports; also IGN and PowerPyx in our game length guide.)

How to manually save in GTA 6 (step by step)

Manual saving lives in the pause menu, not on the phone. These steps match the PS5 and Xbox Series X|S versions:

  1. Get to a quiet moment in free roam. Finish or leave the current mission, lose any wanted level and make sure you aren’t in a fight or mid-air.
  2. Open the pause menu. Press Options on PS5 or Menu on Xbox Series X|S.
  3. Go to the Game tab. It holds both Save Game and Load Game.
  4. Select Save Game. If it’s greyed out, check the restrictions list below.
  5. Pick a slot. Choose an empty slot for a new save, or an old slot you no longer need. Overwriting is permanent once you confirm.
  6. Confirm and wait. Let the save finish writing before you quit to the menu or close the game.

Each slot shows the current mission, completion percentage, date and time and a short story summary (Everyblip), so name-free slots are still easy to tell apart.

When does manual saving unlock?

Not straight away. Save Game stays greyed out for the whole prologue. Dot Esports and KeenGamer both say it unlocks once the opening is over and Jason reaches his house at the marina in the Keys, after his first job for Brian. Until then the game only autosaves, so don’t quit in the middle of the prologue expecting your place to be kept. As soon as it unlocks, make a post-prologue save in a slot you never overwrite, so a second playthrough can skip the long intro.

Does GTA 6 autosave? How autosave works

Yes, GTA 6 has autosave, but it is less frequent than many players expect, which is why “does GTA 6 autosave” became a breakout search in launch week. What the guides agree on:

  • When it saves: after you complete a mission (main or optional). Meristation adds certain map exploration milestones; KeenGamer adds some open-world events.
  • Where it saves: one dedicated autosave entry, separate from your 15 manual slots. Each autosave replaces the last one.
  • What it misses: long free-roam sessions. Meristation and Everyblip warn that little or nothing is autosaved between missions, so after a crash or if you just close the game, you can go back to the end of the last mission.
  • Can you turn it off? One guide cited by Everyblip says there is no settings option to disable it.
Launch-week reports (unconfirmed by Rockstar): Meristation reports a streamer losing more than ten hours after closing the game straight after his first session, trusting autosave. Polygon and Dot Esports also mention reports of autosave not working as expected. Rockstar hasn’t said how often it autosaves, so treat autosave as crash insurance only and always make a manual save before you stop playing.
Update 01.002.002 (23 November 2026): Rockstar’s first post-launch patch officially “fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly” (Rockstar Support). It does not say whether autosave now fires more often in free roam, so keep the manual-save habit. All 7 fixes: GTA 6 patch notes.

How to load a save or the autosave in GTA 6

  1. Open the pause menu (Options / Menu).
  2. Go to Game → Load Game.
  3. Pick a manual slot, or the entry marked (Autosave), which shows the mission and completion percentage.
  4. Confirm. Any progress since that save that isn’t stored in another slot is lost.

Careful: Dot Esports says loading an older save can overwrite your current autosave. Before you load an old file to replay something, make a manual save of where you are now. One guide cited by Everyblip also reports that a free-roam save can reload you at your safehouse rather than the exact spot, with carried loot or an unregistered car gone. So stash loot and park registered cars before you save and quit (see the car drop-off guide for registering cars).

How to use the 15 save slots (no mission replay)

GTA 6 has no mission replay and no chapter select, and your choices and Jason and Lucia’s relationship can change how missions and endings play out. An older manual save is your only way back. KeenGamer also reports that some side missions close when their chapter ends. A slot plan based on the five chapters, as KeenGamer, Dot Esports and Everyblip suggest:

How to avoid losing progress in GTA 6

  1. Manual save at the end of every session. Don’t just close the game or leave it suspended while you play something else (Meristation’s advice after the launch-week reports).
  2. Save after long free-roam stretches. Collectibles, car scans, animals and robberies between missions may not be autosaved. Our checklist keeps your list on your device, but the game only remembers what you saved.
  3. Save before every important mission, and again after it.
  4. Bank cash before risky jobs. Dying or being arrested costs the cash you carry (see the money guide); a save won’t bring it back unless you reload.
  5. Turn on cloud saves. On PS5, cloud backup of saves needs a PlayStation Plus membership; on Xbox Series X|S, saves sync to the cloud automatically.
  6. Leave room on the drive. GTA 6 is already 212 GB+; see the file size guide if your console is nearly full.

Do you need a safehouse to save? Saving vs sleeping

No. Unlike older GTA games, you don’t have to walk to a bed to save: the pause menu works anywhere outside a mission. Safehouses still matter:

  • Sleeping in your safehouse bed skips time and restores health (KeenGamer, Everyblip). It is not the same as saving, so still save from the menu afterwards.
  • Jason’s first safehouse is his stilt house in Key Lento in the Leonida Keys; the bedroom floorboards work as a cash stash until bank access opens (KeenGamer). Read the Chapter 1 money reset before you stash too much there.
  • Shore Court Garage near Ocean Beach comes with the Vintage Vice City Pack and stores the pack’s Vapid Stanier.

FAQ: saving in GTA 6

How do you save in GTA 6?

Pause the game (Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox Series X|S), open the Game tab, choose Save Game and pick one of 15 slots. You can do it anywhere in free roam, as long as you are not in a mission, in combat, wanted by the police or falling.

Does GTA 6 autosave?

Yes. GTA 6 autosaves mainly when a mission ends, plus some milestones and open-world events, into one autosave entry that is overwritten each time. Little is saved between missions, so make a manual save before you quit.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

15 manual save slots plus one separate autosave entry, according to Polygon, Dot Esports and KeenGamer. The autosave never uses one of your 15 slots.

Why can’t I save in GTA 6?

Save Game is greyed out during the prologue, during any active mission, in combat, with a wanted level and while falling. Using Rockstar’s official cheat codes also disables saving for that session; restart the game to save again.

Can you quick save in GTA 6?

There is no phone quick save like in GTA 5. The pause menu save works anywhere in free roam, so it does the same job.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. There is no mission replay or chapter select at launch, so keep manual saves before big missions and at the start and end of each chapter if you want to replay them or try a different choice.
