GTA 6 Bank Vaults: How to Rob Them, All 8 Vault Locations and Payouts

Large bank vaults are the biggest repeatable payout in GTA 6: PowerPyx lists $65,000 to $205,000 per bank for a few minutes of work. They unlock at the very end of Chapter 4 with the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is…, and the vault only opens when an armored security van delivers cash. Below: how to unlock vaults, a step-by-step robbery routine, all 8 vault locations with payouts, the 14 small banks, Yasmani’s van tips and how to escape the instant 4-star wanted level.

Updated 22 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). Spoiler-light: mission names and unlock points only, no story details.

Status on 22 November 2026: Rockstar has not published an official bank or vault guide. Everything below is reported by launch-week guide writers who played the retail game: PowerPyx (bank list, payout ranges, unlocks), Games.gg (matching 22-bank list), GamesRadar+ (Dye-Pack Jammer) and Tech4Gamers (mission walkthrough). Payouts are ranges, not guarantees. No glitches, exploits or money cheats here, only the intended in-game method.

Quick answer: how to rob a bank vault in GTA 6

  • Unlock: finish the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is… (map label “Wait at the Cafe”), offered by Yasmani at the very end of Chapter 4. (Reported: PowerPyx, Tech4Gamers.)
  • Open the vault: you can’t crack it yourself. Follow an armored security van to a large bank and go in while the guard is delivering cash and the vault is open.
  • Payout: $65,000–$205,000 per large bank, paid as marked bills. (Reported: PowerPyx.)
  • Heat: an instant 4-star wanted level. Have a fast motorcycle or car parked nearby (how to shake it: GTA 6 wanted level guide).
  • Find vans faster: buy a $10,000 security van tip from Yasmani after his earlier jobs.
  • Cash out: sell the marked bills to a fence. Yasmani in Little Cuba pays the most.

How to unlock bank robberies and vaults

Banks open up in two stages, and both are tied to the story.

Stage 1: small banks and the Dye-Pack Jammer (Chapter 3)

The main mission Credit Check (map label “Meet Raul’s Crew”), Chapter 3’s 16th mission, gives you the Dye-Pack Jammer from Karim. GamesRadar+ says it can’t be missed and is the only way to get the jammer. Without it, fences refuse marked bills and the dye pack covers Jason and Lucia in pink paint. Credit Check also unlocks the 14 small banks, which have a cash drawer and a safe but no vault (PowerPyx).

Stage 2: vaults with That’s Where The Money Is… (end of Chapter 4)

Vaults are an endgame feature. Tech4Gamers reports that Yasmani contacts you about the job after the main mission VCIA Takedown, near the very end of Chapter 4 and before the final missions. The mission is optional and acts as a tutorial: you wait at Cafe Orquidea in Ocean Beach, tail a security van to a bank and rob the vault. After it, all 8 large banks can be robbed whenever a van makes a delivery. Reports put the tutorial haul at roughly $80,000 if you take everything (reported by PowerPyx via Tech4Gamers and EveryBlip).

Before the ending: PowerPyx notes that vaults arrive right around the story’s point of no return. Guides report nothing is missable and free roam continues after the credits, but make a manual save before the final chapter anyway. Our GTA 6 length guide explains why saves matter, as story missions can’t be replayed.

How to rob a bank vault in GTA 6, step by step

This is the routine the tutorial teaches, with tips from the published walkthroughs.

  1. Prepare. Carry explosives and a disguise, and park a fast getaway vehicle near the bank. A motorcycle is ideal for cutting through traffic (PowerPyx). Bank your cash at an ATM first: launch reports say you lose cash on hand if you die.
  2. Find a security van. Tail one you spot near the Vice City banks, wait at a large bank for a delivery, or buy a $10,000 tip from Yasmani so the van is marked on your map.
  3. Tail it at a distance. Getting too close alerts the driver; in the tutorial the tail fails and restarts (EveryBlip, Tech4Gamers).
  4. Follow the guard inside. When the van parks, the guard carries a briefcase of cash into the bank and the vault opens for a short window (PowerPyx).
  5. Start the robbery. Put on the disguise and draw your weapon. In the tutorial you plant explosives on the doorway at the back of the room and deal with the guards in the vault (Tech4Gamers).
  6. Grab the marked bills. One bag won’t hold it all. Hold Right on the d-pad and press Circle (PS5) or B (Xbox) to hand a full bag to your partner, or switch characters and keep looting (Tech4Gamers).
  7. Loot the cash drawer and the safe too. Every large bank has a vault, a cash drawer and a safe, and you need all three for the bank to count as completed (PowerPyx).
  8. Escape the 4 stars. See the escape plan below.
  9. Sell the bills. Take them to Yasmani in Little Cuba, or leave them at a garage for him to collect (Tech4Gamers).

Yasmani’s security van tips ($10,000)

  • Who: Yasmani, the fence in Little Cuba you meet through the story in Chapter 3.
  • When: after That’s Where The Money Is…, and only once you’ve finished his earlier jobs, which include robbing two Payday Loans stores (PowerPyx).
  • Cost: $10,000 per tip. The van appears on your map with a purple marker (Tech4Gamers).
  • How: call Yasmani from the Reminders tab on your phone (Tech4Gamers).
  • Worth it? Usually. A $10,000 tip against a $65,000–$205,000 vault is a small cost, and it saves a lot of driving around waiting for a van. Pick a tip near a high-paying Lombank if you can.

Yasmani also sells cheaper tips for store jobs. Those don’t mark security vans. The jewelry stores he sends you to earlier are covered in our Vice City money guide.

How to escape 4 stars after a vault job

  1. Plan the exit before you go in. In the tutorial bank there is no back door, so you fight out through the front (Tech4Gamers).
  2. Use a fast bike. PowerPyx recommends a fast motorcycle to cut through traffic. If you want the quickest cars for the job, see the fastest cars in GTA 6.
  3. Let your partner drive. Your partner can drive while you shoot at pursuing cars (EveryBlip).
  4. Avoid highways. Highways get frequent roadblocks. Duck into a parking lot instead (Tech4Gamers).
  5. Change car, then change look. Switch to another vehicle out of sight, and swap the disguise for a hat and glasses so the police lose track of you (PowerPyx, Tech4Gamers).

Selling marked bills

Vault and bank cash is marked bills, not money you can spend. It only becomes cash when a fence buys it, and fences won’t take it until you have the Dye-Pack Jammer (GamesRadar+). Yasmani in Little Cuba pays the most. Tech4Gamers reports you can store stolen goods at a garage from Chapter 3 and have Yasmani collect them. Bags you carry are usually lost if you’re wasted or busted, so sell or store the haul quickly.

Where should vault money go? PowerPyx notes the last Family Savings goal (Save for the Future) is $2 million, and vaults are the fastest way to reach it. All the other money targets are in the money guide hub.

Bank jobs, 100% completion and the Maximum Withdrawal trophy

  • 100% Completion: all 22 Bank Jobs (14 small + 8 large) are required, and each needs every target looted: drawer and safe, plus the vault at large banks (PowerPyx).
  • Maximum Withdrawal trophy/achievement: rob 5 large bank vaults and escape (PowerPyx).
  • Track every bank with the new Bank Jobs list in our GTA 6 100% checklist. Progress saves in your browser.

Mistakes that cost you the whole haul

  • Robbing banks before Credit Check. No Dye-Pack Jammer means no fence will buy the bills.
  • Tailing the van too closely. You alert the driver and have to start the tail again.
  • Forgetting the drawer or safe. The bank won’t count as complete on the 100% list.
  • Dying with the bags. Marked bills are lost if you’re wasted or busted before a fence buys them.
  • Looking for shortcuts. Videos promising “infinite vault money” rely on glitches or mods. They can break saves and go against Rockstar’s terms. The honest method above already pays six figures.

GTA 6 bank vault FAQ

How do you unlock bank vaults in GTA 6?

Finish the optional mission That’s Where The Money Is… (map label: Wait at the Cafe). Yasmani offers it near the very end of Chapter 4, after the main mission VCIA Takedown. It walks you through your first vault robbery, and after it every large bank vault can be hit whenever a security van makes a delivery (PowerPyx, Tech4Gamers).

How do you open a bank vault in GTA 6?

You can’t force it. A large bank’s vault only opens for a short time when an armored security van delivers cash and a guard carries the briefcase inside. Follow the van to the bank, follow the guard in, and rob the vault while the door is open (PowerPyx).

How much money is in a GTA 6 bank vault?

PowerPyx lists between $65,000 and $205,000 per large bank, depending on the branch. The two best are Lombank Downtown and one of the Lombank branches in Ocean Beach, at $135,000–$205,000 each. The take is paid in marked bills that you have to sell to a fence.

Where do you get security van tips in GTA 6?

From Yasmani, the fence in Little Cuba. After That’s Where The Money Is… he sells security van tips for $10,000, but only once you’ve done his earlier jobs, including robbing two Payday Loans stores. The tip marks a van on your map (PowerPyx, Tech4Gamers).

How many banks can you rob in GTA 6?

22 in total: 14 small banks (cash drawer and safe) that open after the Chapter 3 mission Credit Check, and 8 large banks with vaults that open after That’s Where The Money Is…. Completing all 22 Bank Jobs is part of the 100% checklist (PowerPyx, Games.gg).

Do you need the Dye-Pack Jammer to rob banks?

Yes, if you want to keep the money. GamesRadar+ reports that fences won’t accept marked bills and dye packs will cover you in pink paint until you get the Dye-Pack Jammer from Karim in Credit Check, Chapter 3’s 16th mission.

How many vaults do you need for the Maximum Withdrawal trophy?

Five. PowerPyx says the Maximum Withdrawal trophy and achievement asks you to rob 5 large bank vaults; our checklist lists it as rob 5 Bank Vaults and escape.
