GTA 6 Pay ’n’ Spray Locations: All 19 Shops on the Map, Costs and How to Use Them

A Pay ’n’ Spray is the fastest way to stop the police recognising your car in GTA 6, and from Chapter 3 it is also where you sell and register stolen cars. Leonida has 19 of them. Here is every location by region with nearby landmarks, how to find the closest one in two button presses, what each service costs, and the five reasons the garage door stays shut.

Updated 28 November 2026 (Africa/Casablanca). PS5 / Xbox Series X|S, patch 01.002.002. Light spoilers: Chapter 1–3 unlocks only.

Status on 28 November 2026: the count of 19 comes from PowerPyx’s location guide and matches Mobalytics, GTAVibes (cross-checked with GTABase’s map) and Leonipedia. Dexerto’s interactive map pins 20, which no second outlet backs yet. How respraying works is confirmed by GamesRadar+; prices and unlock timing are hands-on player reports, credited line by line. Rockstar has not published an official list. No glitches or exploits.

Pay ’n’ Spray sits at the centre of the car side of GTA 6. It is how you clear vehicle heat, the safe place to keep a car you care about out of the impound lot, and one of the three buyers in our how to sell cars guide. For everything else on wheels, start at the GTA 6 vehicles hub.

How to find the nearest Pay ’n’ Spray

  1. Uncover the map. Each shop marks itself with an orange spray-can icon once you reveal its area (PowerPyx, Leonipedia). Driving or flying the main highways reveals most of them quickly.
  2. Open the Index. Go to Vehicles → Pay ’n’ Spray. It lists every shop you have discovered, sorted by drive time (GamesRadar+).
  3. Pick the top entry to set a GPS route on your minimap.
  4. Track your total. On the map, the filter Map → Vehicles → Pay ’n’ Sprays shows the names and a counter in the bottom-left corner of how many you have found (PowerPyx).

Each garage is named after its area, such as “Pay ’n’ Spray - Key Lento” (Leonipedia), so the names in the table below match what you see in game.

What a Pay ’n’ Spray does (and when it unlocks)

Drive into the open bay and the shop menu shows four services. L1 / R1 (LB / RB) switch to a Stored Vehicles tab for cars in your garages (HowToVideoGame). Every service is paid from your BuckMe account, not pocket cash (GamesRadar+, gta6.house), so keep money in the bank: BuckMe and Family Savings explained.

ServiceWhat it doesAvailable from
RepairFixes engine and collision damage. Always the first option.Chapter 1
ResprayNew paint colour; police stop recognising the car and its vehicle heat clears.Chapter 1
RegisterMakes a stolen car yours (and disables a Standard Tracker) for a fee based on the model.Early Chapter 3
SellBuys the car; the money goes to BuckMe. Good condition pays more.Early Chapter 3

Sources: Leonipedia, gta6.house, HowToVideoGame, GamesRadar+. The map card for a shop reads: “Garage offering repairs and resprays to lower recognition. They also buy and register vehicles with standard trackers” (HowToVideoGame). Performance upgrades belong to mod shops, not Pay ’n’ Spray (gta6.house).

  • Chapter 1: your first visit is the Hamlet shop, after Jason takes a red Phoenix out of the Hamlet impound and loses the police. Repair and respray are free that time, and Respray stays locked until you repair (PowerPyx and GTA BOOM via GTA6Walk, Leonipedia). GTA6Walk and Leonipedia place this in Unwanted Attention; GamesRadar+ and gta6.house name the unlock mission Just a Scratch.
  • Chapter 2: still repairs and resprays only. Stolen cars go to Moses in Port Gellhorn instead: see the Port Gellhorn money guide.
  • Early Chapter 3: after a text exchange with Moses, a tip says “Pay ’n’ Sprays now buy and register untracked vehicles and any with a standard tracker” (HowToVideoGame, gta6motors).
  • Later: after you deliver the ’64 Declasse Mamba GT to Randy, he calls to say Pay ’n’ Sprays will also take Advanced Tracker cars (details in tracked cars).

How to respray a car and lose vehicle heat

When the police note your car, a red vehicle icon appears under your wanted stars. A respray removes it (GamesRadar+). Step by step:

  1. Break line of sight. You cannot enter while the police can see you, shown by fully filled stars (GamesRadar+). Leave the shaded search area first.
  2. Route to the nearest shop with Index → Vehicles → Pay ’n’ Spray.
  3. Drive into the bay and choose Repair if the car is damaged. A damaged car shows “Vehicle must be repaired first” on Respray (HowToVideoGame).
  4. Choose Respray and a clearly different colour. A shade close to the current paint is refused as “Too similar to current color”; gray over red works (Leonipedia, GTA BOOM via GTA6Walk).
  5. Confirm with Cross (PS5) / A (Xbox). The vehicle heat icon disappears.
  6. Clear the rest. Your stars and the outfit, face and weapon icons stay. Swap your hat or glasses, change clothes and stay out of the red area: full checklist in our escape plan.
Quicker alternative: if you do not care about the car, simply leave it. GamesRadar+ notes the vehicle heat icon clears as soon as you step out. Don’t do this with your own registered car inside the search area, or it may be towed: why cars get impounded.

Why the Pay ’n’ Spray won’t let you in

  • The police can see you. Solid stars mean an active visual; break line of sight first (GamesRadar+).
  • Advanced Tracker before Randy’s deal. The game says “Pay ’n’ Sprays don’t currently service vehicles with trackers installed” (HowToVideoGame).
  • Too early in the story. Before Chapter 3 there is no Sell or Register option; use Moses in Chapter 2.
  • Respray greyed out. Repair the car first, or pick a colour that is not “too similar”.
  • Sell limit reached. Players report three stolen-car sales per in-game day (Leonipedia and others in our limits table). Wait for a new in-game day.

Not enough money in BuckMe? Services come out of the bank, so deposit cash at an ATM first.

FAQ: GTA 6 Pay ’n’ Spray

How many Pay ’n’ Sprays are there in GTA 6?

19. PowerPyx, Mobalytics, GTAVibes and Leonipedia all list the same 19 shops: 10 in Vice-Dale County (Vice City and Hamlet), 4 in Mariana County (Keys and Grassrivers), 2 in Kelly County, 2 in Lummox County and 1 in Leonard County. Dexerto’s interactive map pins 20, so treat 19 as the confirmed count until a second outlet backs a 20th.

How do you find the closest Pay ’n’ Spray in GTA 6?

Open the Index, go to Vehicles and pick Pay ’n’ Spray. The game lists every shop you have discovered, sorted by drive time, and selecting one sets a GPS route (GamesRadar+). Shops appear on the map as an orange spray-can icon once you uncover that area (PowerPyx).

Does Pay ’n’ Spray remove your wanted level in GTA 6?

No. A respray only clears the vehicle heat icon, so police stop recognising that car. Your stars and any outfit, face or weapon heat icons stay until you deal with them, and you have to lose the police before the shop lets you in (GamesRadar+, Leonipedia, GTA6Walk).

Why can’t I enter a Pay ’n’ Spray in GTA 6?

The usual reasons: police can still see you (solid wanted stars), the car has an Advanced Tracker before Randy’s deal (the game says Pay ’n’ Sprays don’t currently service vehicles with trackers installed), or the car is damaged, which locks Respray until you Repair it (GamesRadar+, HowToVideoGame).

How much does a respray cost in GTA 6?

Prices depend on the car. Hands-on guides report about $100 for a respray, and repairs of roughly $255 to $355 for damaged cars. Your first repair and respray in Hamlet during the Chapter 1 story are free. Money comes out of your BuckMe bank account (gta6motors, HowToVideoGame, GTA6Walk, GamesRadar+).

Can you sell cars at Pay ’n’ Spray in GTA 6?

Yes, from early Chapter 3. A tip then says Pay ’n’ Sprays now buy and register untracked vehicles and any with a standard tracker. Players report a limit of three stolen cars per in-game day. Cars with an Advanced Tracker are accepted only after Randy’s Mamba GT job (HowToVideoGame, Leonipedia, gta6motors).

Which Pay ’n’ Spray is the first one in GTA 6?

Hamlet. Early in Chapter 1 the story sends Jason there in a red Phoenix taken from the Hamlet impound, and the repair and respray are free on that visit (PowerPyx and GTA BOOM via GTA6Walk, Leonipedia). Some guides name the unlock mission Just a Scratch (GamesRadar+, gta6.house).
